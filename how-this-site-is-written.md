Much of the writing on this site was drafted with the help of Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. I want you to know that plainly, and to know how it is used, so that you can weigh what you read here.

## What I do

The beliefs this site holds and teaches are mine, and so is the responsibility for them. I choose each subject and each question, I decide the direction a page should take, and I decide what is published. My Story is my own account of how the Lord found me.

## What Claude does

Claude drafts wording at my request. I read the draft, comment on it and correct it, and Claude revises it. This is repeated until I am satisfied. Claude also helps with practical work: the design and building of the site, and checking that Bible quotations match the printed text.

Claude is a tool, and like any tool it can be wrong. It can write smoothly and sound sure of itself about something that is not true. That is one reason why doctrine on this site is my responsibility and not the AI’s, and why I ask you to test everything here by the Bible itself.

## The Bible on this site

Quotations are from the Authorised (King James) Version. Words printed in italics are words the translators added to make the English read clearly, as in a printed Bible. Quotations are checked against a full copy of the text, never written from memory.

The four Gospels are given in the Geneva Bible (1599). Its spelling was modernised with Claude’s help, word by word; the words themselves were kept.

## The notes on blog posts

The share of the work done by AI differs from post to post, so every post ends with a note on how it was written. The note includes a row of five dots for each stage of the work. Each dot stands for about a fifth: a filled dot (●) is the AI’s share and an empty dot (○) is mine. So ○○○○○ means the stage was all mine, and ●●●●● means it was nearly all AI. The figures are my own rough estimate for that post, and the stages are the same in every post so that you can compare one post with another:

- **Doctrinal standpoint and house rules:** what the post believes, and the rules all writing on the site must follow.
- **Question and direction:** the subject, and what the post sets out to answer.
- **Ideas and arguments:** the main points and the reasoning behind them.
- **Choice of Scripture:** which Bible passages are used.
- **First-draft wording:** who wrote the first version of the words.
- **Revision and correction:** the rounds of changes before publishing.
- **Checking of Scripture and doctrine:** making sure quotations are exact and teaching is sound.

Other pages do not have the dots. Instead, each has a short line at the foot saying how AI was used, with a link to this page.

## Why I tell you this

The apostle Paul wrote that he and those with him “have renounced the hidden things of dishonesty, not walking in craftiness, nor handling the word of God deceitfully; but by manifestation of the truth commending ourselves to every man’s conscience in the sight of God” (2 Corinthians 4:2). A site that speaks of truth should be open about how it is written.

Whoever or whatever wrote the words, the test is the same: “To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, *it is* because *there is* no light in them” (Isaiah 8:20). “Prove all things; hold fast that which is good” (1 Thessalonians 5:21).

This page, too, was drafted by Claude at my request.
