Some of the writing on this site, including the articles so far, was drafted with the help of Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. I want you to know that plainly, and to know how it is used, so that you can weigh what you read here.

## What I do

The beliefs this site holds and teaches are mine, and so is the responsibility for them. I choose each subject and each question, I decide the direction a page should take, and I decide what is published. My Story is my own account, in my own words, of how the Lord found me (save one or two minor places where Claude suggested something clearer). Claude created the shortened version.

## What Claude does

Claude drafts wording at my request. I read the draft, comment on it and correct it, and Claude revises it. This is repeated until I am satisfied, and often involves many revisions. Claude also helps with practical work: the design and building of the site, and checking that Bible quotations match the printed text.

Claude is a tool, and like any tool it can be wrong. It can write smoothly and sound sure of itself about something that is not true. That is one reason why doctrine on this site is my responsibility and not the AI’s, and why I ask you to test everything here by the Bible itself.

## A horse and its rider

A Christian brother I know likens using AI to riding a horse. A good horse is strong and swift, but a careless rider cannot steer it, ends up falling off, and blames the horse. A careful rider keeps a steady hand on the reins: he watches for the horse straying and brings it back, knowing that the responsibility for reaching the destination is his, not the horse’s. A careful rider on a good horse can go further and faster than a man on foot.

That is how I seek to use Claude, though I am still learning to ride. Every good gift is from above, and that includes the wisdom by which men have made such tools, so I thank the Lord for it (James 1:17; Isaiah 28:26, 29). Each piece is prayerfully reflected on, weighed against the Bible and revised many times, gathering insights along the way; the change log on each article and post records how it has grown. Where I go wrong, the fault is mine and not the horse’s; and for whatever is good and true here, the glory belongs to God alone.

## The Bible on this site

Quotations are from the Authorised (King James) Version. Words printed in italics are words the translators added to make the English read clearly, as in a printed Bible. Quotations are checked against a full copy of the text, never written from memory.

The four Gospels are given in the Geneva Bible (1599). Its spelling was modernised with Claude’s help, word by word; the words themselves were kept.

## Why I tell you this

The apostle Paul wrote that he and those with him “have renounced the hidden things of dishonesty, not walking in craftiness, nor handling the word of God deceitfully; but by manifestation of the truth commending ourselves to every man’s conscience in the sight of God” (2 Corinthians 4:2). A site that speaks of truth should be open about how it is written.

However the words were drafted, the test is the same: “To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, *it is* because *there is* no light in them” (Isaiah 8:20). “Prove all things; hold fast that which is good” (1 Thessalonians 5:21).
