truth

On the Day of Judgment there will be no line across the levels at all. God’s line runs straight down the middle, through every level, from the best to the worst.

And, behold, there are last which shall be first, and there are first which shall be last.Luke 13:30, KJV
And before him shall be gathered all nations: and he shall separate them one from another, as a shepherd divideth his sheep from the goats: And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on the left.Matthew 25:32-33, KJV

On the right, going to heaven: repentant people from every level who trust in Christ alone. On the left, going to hell: unrepentant people from every level who trust in themselves, their religion, or nothing at all.

nodifference

We sort people by how good they are, and the sorting is real: a kind neighbour is not the same as a murderer, and the Bible never says otherwise. But on the Day of Judgment nobody is measured against their neighbours. They are measured against God’s law, summarised by the Ten Commandments, and every one of us falls short. (Exodus 20:1-17)

For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;Romans 3:23, KJV
But we are all as an unclean thing, and all our righteousnesses are as filthy rags; and we all do fade as a leaf; and our iniquities, like the wind, have taken us away.Isaiah 64:6, KJV

Even our best deeds (our “righteousnesses”) are stained. So the question is not “how high up the levels am I?” but “who am I trusting in?”

twosides

Your line was drawn across the levels. God’s line runs straight down through them. Here are six people from the Bible: the Pharisee and the publican come from a story Jesus told, and the other four from real life. Most of us would put the first three near the top and the last three near the bottom. Now see which side of God’s line each one is on.

Those we would put near the top, yet unrepentant, not trusting in Christ

Those we would put near the bottom, yet repentant, trusting in God’s mercy in Christ

What made the difference was never how good they were, but whether they turned from their sin and trusted in Christ alone.

whichside

The right-hand side is not for the good. It is for sinners who have stopped trusting in themselves and trust in Jesus Christ, who lived the perfect life we have not lived and died for the sins of all who trust Him. Being saved is a gift: it cannot be earned by climbing the levels.

For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.Ephesians 2:8-9, KJV
But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness.Romans 4:5, KJV

That means no one is too bad to cross over to the right, and no one is good enough to stay on the left safely.

He that believeth on the Son hath everlasting life: and he that believeth not the Son shall not see life; but the wrath of God abideth on him.John 3:36, KJV
This is a faithful saying, and worthy of all acceptation, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners; of whom I am chief.1 Timothy 1:15, KJV

Which side of the line are you on today?
