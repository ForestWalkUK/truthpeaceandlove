Truth, Peace and Love - CHANGELOG
=================================

Note: from now on, every version bump carries its own entry in this file, added in the same
commit that raises site_version. This file is not published (it is in the exclude list in
_config.yml).

Version 7.10.0 (27th November, A.D. 2026) - Three-part version numbers; optional label for the Important note
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New: the site version now has three parts, major.minor.fix, from this version on. 7.9
  counts as 7.9.0, and this is the first minor step after it. The rule is in CLAUDE.md.
  Article and post change logs keep their own two-part numbers.
New: an optional "document_label:" in a page's front matter changes the first word of the
  Important note (_includes/standing-note.html), "This ... was drawn up by me", and nothing
  else. Without it the note is unchanged; a before-and-after build showed every other page,
  including the Sorrow and Joy Confession, rendering exactly as before.
Change: the Mark 11:24 study (now "A Question-and-Answer Study") uses document_label: study,
  so its Important note opens "This study was drawn up by me"; it keeps document: catechism
  and still reads "not the catechism of any church". Its change log is at 4.6.

Version 7.9 (26th November, A.D. 2026) - Hover colours only on devices that can hover
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix: hover colour swaps no longer stick on touch screens after a tap. On a phone, a tapped
  element kept its :hover style until the next tap, so the floating up button stayed
  paper-coloured after use. The colour-swapping hover rules (.ctl and its #, the size
  buttons' disabled state, .float-top, the chapter-number links and the menu's
  bottom-border colour) are now inside
  @media (hover:hover), declarations unchanged. Underline-only hover rules and the
  :focus-visible styles are untouched.

Version 7.8 (26th November, A.D. 2026) - Back to #top and a floating up button; #thecut two sides
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
New: every page now ends with a "Back to #top" link (in default.html, after the AI note),
  styled like the site's other "Back to #..." lead-ins. It is a plain link to
  <body id="top">, so it works with JavaScript switched off. It is not printed.
New: a small floating up-arrow button, bottom right (44 x 44px, ink with a paper-coloured
  arrow, square corners, clear of phone safe areas). It appears once the reader is more
  than two screen heights down the page, and hides near the top and whenever the Back to
  #top link or the footer is on screen. A click scrolls to the top (instantly when the
  reader prefers reduced motion) and moves keyboard focus to the top of the page. It sits
  above the #thecut reveal and below VerseClick's pop-ups, and is not printed.
Improvement: #thecut's people section is now #twosides. Its opening paragraph says what
  it shows; the six people are set out in two groups, left and right of God's line,
  each card giving "Where we'd put him" and "God's line" lines, with the outcome tag
  directly under its own quotation. The Pharisee's note adds Luke 18:9, a closing
  sentence follows the cards, and "bands" is now "levels" in all reader-facing text.
  Page change log 1.6.

Version 7.7 (26th November, A.D. 2026) - Drop capital: more space after a one-letter first word
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Improvement: when a drop-capital paragraph begins with a one-letter word (I, A), the gap
  after the capital is doubled (0.27em to 0.54em where the browser has initial-letter;
  0.08em to 0.16em in the float fallback), so that "I instinctively" no longer reads as
  one word. A small script in default.html adds class "drop-word" to such paragraphs
  (same selector as the TPL Drop cap CSS); style.css does the rest.

Version 7.6 (26th November, A.D. 2026) - Drop capitals for second openings and My Story
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Improvement: a paragraph can be marked as an opening paragraph by hand with class
  "opener" ({: .opener} on the line under it in Markdown), and then gets the same drop
  capital and small-capital first line as an automatic one (style.css selectors widened
  with :is()). Used on the Halloween post's full article, and on My Story's short
  version and longer account (My Story's layout has no automatic drop capital).

Version 6.8 (26th November, A.D. 2026) - Knewave on every heading of 24px or more
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix and feature: Knewave now on every heading of 24px or more, including the home
  title. Restored the earlier heading sizes that v6.7 shrank (door labels had been fixed
  at 26px, page hashtag titles capped at 52px).
  Knewave never below 24px; letter-spacing reset to 0.
  Diagnosis: no overriding rule was found. On the live site (v6.7) the computed font of
  .tag-title was already Knewave; the likeliest cause is a stylesheet cached on the
  reader's device, which the version number in the stylesheet address (now ?v=6.8) refreshes.
  Headings that could fall under 24px are floored with max(24px, ...). Left as they were:
  the accordion headings, prose h3, the wordmark and small text.
