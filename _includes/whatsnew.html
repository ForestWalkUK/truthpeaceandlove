{% comment %} #whatsnew on the home page (v2.8). Lists anything posted in the past month, newest first: every blog post, and any page whose front matter has a "date:" line (e.g. date: 2026-11-02). Pages without "date:" are not listed, because the site cannot tell when they were added. The list is made when GitHub builds the site. The site only rebuilds when something is committed, so a small script also hides entries once they are more than a month old by the reader's own calendar.
