# Mark 14

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And two days after followed the feast of the Passover, and of unleavened bread: and the high Priests, and Scribes sought how they might take him by craft, and put him to death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> But they said, Not in the feast day, least there be any tumult among the people.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And when he was in Bethany in the house of Simon the leper, as he sat at table, there came a woman having a box of ointment of spikenard, very costly, and she brake the box, and poured it on his head.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Therefore some disdained among themselves, and said, To what end is this waste of ointment?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> For it might have been sold for more than three hundred pence, and been given unto the poor, and they murmured against her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> But Jesus said, Let her alone: why trouble ye her? she hath wrought a good work on me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> For ye have the poor with you always, and when ye will ye may do them good, but me ye shall not have always.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> She hath done that she could: she came afore hand to anoint my body to the burying.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Verily I say unto you, wheresoever this Gospel shall be preached throughout the whole world, this also that she hath done, shall be spoken of in remembrance of her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Then Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve, went away unto the high Priests, to betray him unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And when they heard it, they were glad, and promised that they would give him money: therefore he sought how he might conveniently betray him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Now the first day of unleavened bread, when they sacrificed the Passover, his disciples said unto him, Where wilt thou that we go and prepare, that thou mayest eat the Passover?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then he sent forth two of his disciples, and said unto them, Go ye into the city, and there shall a man meet you bearing a pitcher of water: follow him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And whithersoever he goeth in, say ye to the good man of the house, The Master sayeth, Where is the lodging where I shall eat the Passover with my disciples?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And he will show you an upper chamber which is large, trimmed and prepared: there make it ready for us.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> So his disciples went forth, and came to the city, and found as he had said unto them, and made ready the Passover.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And at even he came with the twelve.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And as they sat at table and did eat, Jesus said, Verily I say unto you, that one of you shall betray me, which eateth with me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Then they began to be sorrowful and to say to him one by one, Is it I? And another, Is it I?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And he answered and said unto them, It is one of the twelve that dippeth with me in the platter.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Truly the Son of man goeth his way, as it is written of him: but woe be to that man, by whom the Son of man is betrayed: it had been good for that man, if he had never been born.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And as they did eat, Jesus took the bread, and when he had given thanks, he brake it and gave it to them, and said, Take, eat, this is my body.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Also he took the cup, and when he had given thanks, gave it to them: and they all drank of it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And he said unto them, This is my blood of that new Testament, which is shed for many.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Verily I say unto you, I will drink no more of the fruit of the vine until that day, that I drink it new in the kingdom of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And when they had sung a Psalm, they went out to the mount of Olives.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Then Jesus said unto them, All ye shall be offended by me this night: for it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> But after that I am risen, I will go into Galilee before you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And Peter said unto him, Although all men should be offended at thee, yet would not I.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Then Jesus said unto him, Verily I say unto thee, this day, even in this night, before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> But he said more earnestly. If I should die with thee, I will not deny thee: likewise also said they all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> After, they came into a place named Gethsemane: then he said to his disciples, Sit ye here, till I have prayed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And he took with him Peter, and James, and John, and he began to be troubled, and in great heaviness,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And said unto them, My soul is very heavy, even unto the death: tarry here, and watch.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> So he went forward a little, and fell down on the ground, and prayed, that if it were possible, that hour might pass from him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And he said, Abba, Father, all things are possible unto thee: take away this cup from me: nevertheless not that I will, but that thou wilt, be done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> Then he came, and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, Simon, sleepest thou? couldest not thou watch one hour?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Watch ye, and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit in deed is ready, but the flesh is weak.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And again he went away, and prayed, and spake the same words.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And he returned, and found them a sleep again: for their eyes were heavy: neither knew they what they should answer him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And he came the third time, and said unto them, Sleep henceforth, and take your rest: it is enough: the hour is come: behold, the Son of man is delivered into the hands of sinners.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> Rise up: let us go: lo, he that betrayeth me, is at hand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> And immediately while he yet spake, came Judas that was one of the twelve, and with him a great multitude with swords and staves from the high Priests, and Scribes, and Elders.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> And he that betrayed him, had given them a token, saying, Whomsoever I shall kiss, he it is: take him and lead him away safely.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> And as soon as he was come, he went straightway to him, and said, Hail Master, and kissed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> Then they laid their hands on him, and took him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And one of them that stood by, drew out a sword, and smote a servant of the high Priest, and cut off his ear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> And Jesus answered and said to them, Ye be come out as against a thief with swords, and with staves, to take me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> I was daily with you teaching in the Temple, and ye took me not: but this is done that the Scriptures should be fulfilled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> Then they all forsook him, and fled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> And there followed him a certain young man, clothed in linen upon his bare body, and the young men caught him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> But he left his linen cloth, and fled from them naked.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> So they led Jesus away to the high Priest, and to him came together all the high Priests, and the Elders, and the Scribes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> And Peter followed him afar off, even into the hall of the high Priest, and sat with the servants, and warmed himself at the fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> And the high Priests, and all the Council sought for witness against Jesus, to put him to death, but found none.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> For many bare false witness against him, but their witness agreed not together.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">57</span> Then there arose certain, and bare false witness against him, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">58</span> We heard him say, I will destroy this Temple made with hands, and within three days I will build another, made without hands.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">59</span> But their witness yet agreed not together.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">60</span> Then the high Priest stood up amongst them, and asked Jesus, saying, Answerest thou nothing? what is the matter that these bear witness against thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">61</span> But he held his peace, and answered nothing. Again the high Priest asked him, and said unto him, Art thou that Christ the Son of the Blessed?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">62</span> And Jesus said, I am he, and ye shall see the Son of man sit at the right hand of the power of God, and come in the clouds of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">63</span> Then the high Priest rent his clothes, and said, What have we any more need of witnesses?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">64</span> Ye have heard the blasphemy: what think ye? And they all condemned him to be worthy of death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">65</span> And some began to spit at him, and to cover his face, and to beat him with fists, and to say unto him, Prophesy. And the sergeants smote him with their rods.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">66</span> And as Peter was beneath in the hall, there came one of the maids of the high Priest.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">67</span> And when she saw Peter warming him self, she looked on him, and said, Thou wast also with Jesus of Nazareth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">68</span> But he denied it, saying, I know him not, neither wot I what thou sayest. Then he went out into the porch, and the cock crew.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">69</span> Then a maid saw him again, and began to say to them that stood by, This is one of them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">70</span> But he denied it again: and anon after, they that stood by, said again to Peter, Surely thou art one of them: for thou art of Galilee, and thy speech is like.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">71</span> And he began to curse, and swear, saying, I know not this man of whom ye speak.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">72</span> Then the second time the cock crew, and Peter remembered the word that Jesus had said unto him, Before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice, and weighing that with himself, he wept.</p>
