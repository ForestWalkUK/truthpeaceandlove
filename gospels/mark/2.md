# Mark 2

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> After a few days, he entered into Capernaum again, and it was noised that he was in the house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And anon, many gathered together, in so much that the places about the door could not receive any more and he preached the word unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And there came unto him, that brought one sick of the palsy, borne of four men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And because they could not come near unto him for the multitude, they uncovered the roof of the house where he was: and when they had broken it open, they let down the bed, wherein the sick of the palsy lay.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Now when Jesus saw their faith, he said to the sick of the palsy, Son, thy sins are forgiven thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And there were certain of the Scribes sitting there, and reasoning in their hearts,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Why doeth this man speak such blasphemies? who can forgive sins, but God only?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And immediately when Jesus perceived in his spirit, that thus they reasoned with themselves, he said unto them, Why reason ye these things in your hearts?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Whether is it easier to say to the sick of the palsy, Thy sins are forgiven thee? or to say, Arise, and take up thy bed, and walk?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> But that ye may know, that the Son of man hath authority in earth to forgive sins, (he said unto the sick of the palsy.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> I say unto thee, Arise and take up thy bed, and get thee hence into thine own house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And by and by he arose, and took up his bed, and went forth before them all, in so much that they were all amazed, and glorified God, saying, We never saw such a thing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then he went forth again toward the sea, and all the people resorted unto him, and he taught them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And as Jesus passed by, he saw Levi the son of Alpheus sit at the receipt of custom, and said unto him, Follow me. And he arose and followed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And it came to pass, as Jesus sat at table in his house, many Publicans and sinners sat at table also with Jesus, and his disciples: for there were many that followed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And when the Scribes and Pharisees saw him eat with the Publicans and sinners, they said unto his disciples, How is it, that he eateth and drinketh with Publicans and sinners?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Now when Jesus heard it, he said unto them, The whole have no need of the Physician, but the sick. I came not to call the righteous, but the sinners to repentance.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And the disciples of John, and the Pharisees did fast, and came and said unto him, Why do the disciples of John, and of the Pharisees fast, and thy disciples fast not?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And Jesus said unto them, Can the children of the marriage chamber fast, whiles the bridegroom is with them? as long as they have the bridegroom with them, they cannot fast.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> But the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then shall they fast in those days.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Also no man soweth a piece of new cloth in an old garment: for else the new piece that filled it up, taketh away somewhat from the old, and the breach is worse.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Likewise, no man putteth new wine into old vessels: for else the new wine breaketh the vessels, and the wine runneth out, and the vessels are lost: but new wine must be put into new vessels.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And it came to pass as he went through the corn on the Sabbath day, that his disciples, as they went on their way, began to pluck the ears of corn.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And the Pharisees said unto him, Behold, why do they on the Sabbath day, that which is not lawful?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And he said to them, Have ye never read what David did when he had need, and was an hungered, both he, and they that were with him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> How he went into the house of God, in the days of Abiathar the high Priest, and did eat the show bread, which were not lawful to eat, but for the Priests, and gave also to them which were with him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And he said to them, The Sabbath was made for man, and not man for the Sabbath.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Wherefore the Son of man is Lord, even of the Sabbath.</p>
</div>
