# Mark 3

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And he entered again into the Synagogue, and there was a man which had a withered had.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And they watched him, whether he would heal him on the Sabbath day, that they might accuse him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then he said unto the man which had the withered hand, Arise: stand forth in the midst.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And he said to them, Is it lawful to do a good deed on the Sabbath day, or to do evil? to save the life, or to kill? But they held their peace.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Then he looked round about on them angrily, mourning also for the hardness of their hearts, and said to the man, Stretch forth thine hand. And he stretched it out: and his hand was restored, as whole as the other.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And the Pharisees departed, and straightway gathered a council with the Herodians against him, that they might destroy him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> But Jesus avoided with his disciples to the sea: and a great multitude followed him from Galilee, and from Judea,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And from Jerusalem, and from Idumea, and beyond Jordan: and they that dwelled about Tyre and Sidon, when they had heard what great things he did, came unto him in great number.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And he commanded his disciples, that a little ship should wait for him, because of the multitude, lest they should throng him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> For he had healed many, in so much that they pressed upon him to touch him, as many as had plagues.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And when the unclean spirits saw him, they fell down before him, and cried, saying, Thou art the Son of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And he sharply rebuked them, to the end they should not utter him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then he went up into a mountain, and called unto him whom he would, and they came unto him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And he appointed twelve that they should be with him, and that he might send them to preach,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And that they might have power to heal sicknesses, and to cast out devils.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And the first was Simon, and he named Simon, Peter,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then James the son of Zebedee, and John James brother (and surnamed them Boanerges, which is, the sons of thunder,)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James, the son of Alpheus, and Thaddeus, and Simon the Canaanite,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him, and they came home.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And the multitude assembled again, so that they could not so much as eat bread.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And when his kinsfolks heard of it, they went out to lay hold on him: for they said that he was beside himself.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And the Scribes which came down from Jerusalem, said, He hath Beelzebub, and through the prince of the devils he casteth out devils.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> But he called them unto him, and said unto them in parables, How can Satan drive out Satan?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> For if a kingdom be divided against it self, that kingdom can not stand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Or if a house be divided against it self, that house can not continue.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> So if Satan make insurrection against himself, and be divided, he can not endure but is at an end.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> No man can enter into a strong mans house, and take away his goods, except he first bind that strong man, and then spoil his house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Verily I say unto you, all sins shall be forgiven unto the children of men, and blasphemies, wherewith they blaspheme:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> But he that blasphemeth against the holy Ghost, shall never have forgiveness, but is culpable of eternal damnation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Because they said, He had an unclean spirit.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Then came his brethren and mother, and stood without, and sent unto him, and called him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And the people sat about him, and they said unto him, Behold, thy mother, and thy brethren seek for thee without.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> But he answered them, saying, Who is my mother and my brethren?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And he looked round about on them, which sat in compass about him, and said, Behold my mother and my brethren.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> For whosoever doeth the will of God, he is my brother, and my sister, and mother.</p>
</div>
