# Mark 1

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> The beginning of the Gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> As it is written in the Prophets, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> The voice of him that crieth in the wilderness is, Prepare the way of the Lord: make his paths straight.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> John did baptize in the wilderness, and preach the baptism of amendment of life, for remission of sins.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And all the country of Judea, and they of Jerusalem went out unto him, and were all baptized of him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Now John was clothed with camels hear, and with a girdle of a skin about his loins: and he did eat Locusts and wild honey,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And preached, saying, A stronger than I cometh after me, whose shoes latchet I am not worthy to stoop down, and unloose.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Truth it is, I have baptized you with water: but he will baptize you with the holy Ghost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And it came to pass in those days, that Jesus came from Nazareth, a city of Galilee, and was baptized of John in Jordan.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And as soon as he was come out of the water, John saw the heavens cloven in twain, and the holy Ghost descending upon him like a dove.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Then there was a voice from heaven, saying, Thou art my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And immediately the Spirit driveth him into the wilderness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And he was there in the wilderness forty days, and was tempted of Satan: he was also with the wild beasts, and the Angels ministered unto him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Now after that John was committed to prison, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the Gospel of the kingdom of God,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And saying, The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent and believe the Gospel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And as he walked by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea, (for they were fishers.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then Jesus said unto them, Follow me, and I will make you to be fishers of men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And straightway they forsook their nets, and followed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And when he had gone a little further thence, he saw James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, as they were in the ship, mending their nets.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And anon he called them: and they left their father Zebedee in the ship with his hired servants, and went their way after him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> So they entered into Capernaum, and straightway on the Sabbath day he entered into the Synagogue, and taught.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And they were astonied at his doctrine, for he taught them as one that had authority, and not as the Scribes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And there was in their Synagogue a man in whom was an unclean spirit, and he cried out,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Saying, Ah, what have we to do with thee, O Jesus of Nazareth? Art thou come to destroy us? I know thee what thou art, even that holy one of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, Hold thy peace, and come out of him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And the unclean spirit tare him, and cried with a loud voice, and came out of him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And they were all amazed, so that they demanded one of another, saying, What thing is this? what new doctrine is this? for he commandeth even the foul spirits with authority, and they obey him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And immediately his fame spread abroad throughout all the region bordering on Galilee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And as soon as they were come out of the Synagogue, they entered into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And Simons wives mother lay sick of a fever, and anon they told him of her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And he came and took her by the hand, and lifted her up, and the fever forsook her by and by, and she ministered unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And when even was come, at what time the sun setteth, they brought to him all that were diseased, and them that were possessed with devils.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And the whole city was gathered together at the door.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And he healed many that were sick of divers diseases: and he cast out many devils, and suffered not the devils to say that they knew him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And in the morning very early before day, Jesus arose and went out into a solitary place, and there prayed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And Simon, and they that were with him, followed carefully after him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And when they had found him, they said unto him, All men seek for thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Then he said unto them, Let us go into the next towns, that I may preach there also: for I came out for that purpose.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And he preached in their Synagogues, throughout all Galilee, and cast the devils out.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And there came a leper to him, beseeching him, and kneeled down unto him, and said to him, If thou wilt, thou canst make me clean.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And Jesus had compassion, and put forth his hand, and touched him, and said to him, I will: be thou clean.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And as soon as he had spoken, immediately the leprosy departed from him, and he was made clean.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> And after he had give him a straight commandment, he sent him away forthwith,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> And said unto him, See thou say nothing to any man, but get thee hence, and show thy self to the Priest, and offer for thy cleansing those things, which Moses commanded, for a testimonial unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> But when he was departed, he began to tell many things, and to publish the matter: so that Jesus could no more openly enter into the city, but was without in desert places: and they came to him from every quarter.</p>
</div>
