# Mark 4

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And he began again to teach by the sea side, and there gathered unto him a great multitude, so that he entered into a ship, and sat in the sea, and all the people was by the sea side on the land.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And he taught them many things in parables, and said unto them in his doctrine,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Hearken: Behold, there went out a sower to sow.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And it came to pass as he sowed, that some fell by the way side, and the fowls of the heaven came, and devoured it up.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And some fell on stony ground, where it had not much earth, and by and by sprang up, because it had not depth of earth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> But as soon as the Sun was up, it was burnt up, and because it had not root, it withered away.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And some fell among the thorns, and the thorns grew up, and choked it, so that it gave no fruit.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Some again fell in good ground, and did yield fruit that sprung up, and grew, and it brought forth, some thirty fold, some sixty fold, and some an hundred fold.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Then he said unto them, He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And when he was alone, they that were about him with the twelve, asked him of the parable.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And he said unto them, To you it is given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God: but unto them that are without, all things be done in parables,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> That they seeing, may see, and not discern: and they hearing, may hear, and not understand, least at any time they should turn, and their sins should be forgiven them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Again he said unto them, Perceive ye not this parable? how then should ye understand all other parables?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> The sower soweth the word.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And these are they that receive the seed by the ways side, in whom the word is sown: but when they have heard it, Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And likewise they that receive the seed in stony ground, are they, which when they have heard the word, straightways receive it with gladness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Yet have they no root in themselves, and endure but a time: for when trouble and persecution ariseth for the word, immediately they be offended.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Also they that receive the seed among the thorns, are such as hear the word:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> But the cares of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things enter in, and choke the word, and it is unfruitful.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> But they that have received seed in good ground, are they that hear the word, and receive it, and bring forth fruit: one corn thirty, another sixty, and some an hundred.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Also he said unto them, Cometh the candle in, to be put under a bushel, or under the bed, and not to be put on a candlestick?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> For there is nothing hid, that shall not be opened: neither is there a secret, but that it shall come to light.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> If any man have ears to hear, let him hear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And he said unto them, Take heed what ye hear. With what measure ye mete, it shall be measured unto you: and unto you that hear, shall more be given.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> For unto him that hath, shall it be given, and from him that hath not, shall be taken away, even that he hath.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Also he said, So is the kingdom of God, as if a man should cast seed in the ground,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And should sleep, and rise up night and day, and the seed should spring and grow up, he not knowing how.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> For the earth bringeth forth fruit of it self, first the blade, then the ears, after that full corn in the ears.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And as soon as the fruit showeth it self, anon he putteth in the sickle, because the harvest is come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> He said moreover, Whereunto shall we liken the kingdom of God? or with what comparison shall we compare it?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> It is like a grain of mustard seed, which when it is sown in the earth, is the least of all seeds that be in the earth:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> But after that it is sown, it groweth up, and is greatest of all herbs, and beareth great branches, so that the fowls of heaven may build under the shadow of it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And with many such parables he preached the word unto them, as they were able to hear it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And without parables spake he nothing unto them: but he expounded all things to his disciples apart.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Now the same day when even was come, he said unto them, Let us pass over unto the other side.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And they left the multitude, and took him as he was in the ship, and there were also with him other little ships.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And there arose a great storm of wind, and the waves dashed into the ship, so that it was now full.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And he was in the sterne asleep on a pillow: and they awoke him, and said to him, Master, carest thou not that we perish?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And he rose up, and rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea, Peace, and be still. So the wind ceased, and it was a great calm.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> Then he said unto them, Why are ye so fearful? how is it that ye have no faith?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And they feared exceedingly, and said one to another, Who is this, that both the wind and sea obey him?</p>
</div>
