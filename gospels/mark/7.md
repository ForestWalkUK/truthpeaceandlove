# Mark 7

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">7</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Then gathered unto him the Pharisees, and certain of the Scribes which came from Jerusalem.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And when they saw some of his disciples eat meat with common hands, (that is to say, unwashen) they complained.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> (For the Pharisees, and all the Jews, except they wash their hands oft, eat not, holding the tradition of the Elders.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And when they come from the market, except they wash, they eat not: and many other things there be, which they have taken upon them to observe, as the washing of cups, and pots, and of brazen vessels, and of beds.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Then asked him the Pharisees and Scribes, Why walk not thy disciples according to the tradition of the Elders, but eat meat with unwashen hands?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Then he answered and said unto them, Surely Esaias hath prophesied well of you, hypocrites, as it is written, This people honoureth me with lips, but their heart is far away from me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> But they worship me in vain, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> For ye lay the commandment of God apart, and observe the tradition of men, as the washing of pots and of cups, and many other such like things ye do.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And he said unto them, Well, ye reject the commandment of God, that ye may observe your own tradition.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> For Moses said, Honour thy father and thy mother: and Whosoever shall speak evil of father or mother, let him die the death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> But ye say, If a man say to father or mother, Corban, that is, By the gift that is offered by me, thou mayest have profit, he shall be free.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> So ye suffer him no more to do any thing for his father, or his mother,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Making the word of God of none authority, by your tradition which ye have ordained: and ye do many such like things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Then he called the whole multitude unto him, and said unto them, Hearken you all unto me, and understand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> There is nothing without a man, that can defile him, when it entereth into him: but the things which proceed out of him, are they which defile the man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> If any have ears to hear, let him hear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And when he came into an house, away from the people, his disciples asked him concerning the parable.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And he said unto them, What? are ye without understanding also? Do ye not know that whatsoever thing from without entereth into a man, cannot defile him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Because it entereth not into his heart, but into the belly, and goeth out into the draught which is the purging of all meats?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Then he said, That which cometh out of man, that defileth man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> For from within, even out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Thefts, covetousness, wickedness, deceit, uncleanness, a wicked eye, backbiting, pride, foolishness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> All these evil things come from within, and defile a man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And from thence he rose, and went into the borders of Tyre and Sidon, and entered into an house, and would that no man should have known: but he could not be hid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> For a certain woman, whose little daughter had an unclean spirit, heard of him, and came, and fell at his feet,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> (And the woman was a Greek, a Syrophenician by nation) and she besought him that he would cast out the devil out of her daughter.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> But Jesus said unto her, Let the children first be fed: for it is not good to take the childrens bread, and to cast it unto whelps.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then she answered, and said unto him, Truth, Lord: yet in deed the whelps eat under the table of the childrens crumbs.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Then he said unto her, For this saying go thy way: the devil is gone out of thy daughter.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And when she was come home to her house, she found the devil departed, and her daughter lying on the bed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And he departed again from the coasts of Tyre and Sidon, and came unto the sea of Galilee, through the midst of the coasts of Decapolis.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And they brought unto him one that was deaf and stammered in his speech, and prayed him to put his hand upon him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Then he took him aside from the multitude, and put his fingers in his ears, and did spit, and touched his tongue.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And looking up to heaven, he sighed, and said unto him, Ephphatha, that is, Be opened.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And straightway his ears were opened, and the string of his tongue was loosed, and he spake plain.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And he commanded them, that they should tell no man: but how much soever he forbad them, the more a great deal they published it,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And were beyond measure astonied, saying, He hath done all things well: he maketh both the deaf to hear, and the dumb to speak.</p>
