# Mark 15

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">15</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And anon in the dawning, the high Priests held a Council with the Elders, and the Scribes, and the whole Council, and bound Jesus, and led him away, and delivered him to Pilate.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Then Pilate asked him, Art thou the King of the Jews? And he answered, and said unto him, Thou sayest it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And the high Priests accused him of many things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Wherefore Pilate asked him again, saying, Answerest thou nothing? behold how many things they witness against thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> But Jesus answered no more at all, so that Pilate marvelled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Now at the feast, Pilate did deliver a prisoner unto them, whomsoever they would desire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then there was one named Barabbas, which was bound with his fellows, that had made insurrection, who in the insurrection had committed murder.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And the people cried aloud, and began to desire that he would do as he had ever done unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Then Pilate answered them, and said, Will ye that I let loose unto you the King of the Jews?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> For he knew that the high Priests had delivered him of envy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> But the high Priests had moved the people to desire that he would rather deliver Barabbas unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And Pilate answered, and said again unto them, What will ye then that I do with him, whom ye call the King of the Jews?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And they cried again, Crucify him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Then Pilate said unto them, But what evil hath he done? And they cried the more fervently, Crucify him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> So Pilate willing to content the people, loosed them Barabbas, and delivered Jesus, when he had scourged him, that he might be crucified.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Then the soldiers led him away into the hall, which is the common hall, and called together the whole band,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And clad him with purple, and platted a crown of thorns, and put it about his head,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And began to salute him, saying, Hail, King of the Jews.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And they smote him on the head with a reed, and spat upon him, and bowed the knees, and did him reverence.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And when they had mocked him, they took the purple off him, and put his own clothes on him, and led him out to crucify him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And they compelled one that passed by, called Simon of Cyrene (which came out of the country, and was father of Alexander and Rufus) to bear his cross.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And they brought him to a place named Golgotha, which is by interpretation, the place of dead mens skulls.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And they gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh: but he received it not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And when they had crucified him, they parted his garments, casting lots for them, what every man should have.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And it was the third hour, when they crucified him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And the title of his cause was written above, THAT KING OF THE JEWS.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> They crucified also with him two thieves, the one on the right hand, and the other on his left.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Thus the Scripture was fulfilled, which saith, And he was counted among the wicked.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And they that went by, railed on him, wagging their heads, and saying, Hey, thou that destroyest the Temple, and buildest it in three days,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Save thy self, and come down from the cross.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Likewise also even the high Priests mocking, said among themselves with the Scribes, He saved other men, himself he cannot save.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Let Christ the King of Israel now come down from the cross, that we may see, and believe. They also that were crucified with him, reviled him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Now when the sixth hour was come, darkness arose over all the land until the ninth hour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And at the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani? which is by interpretation, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And some of them that stood by, when they heard it, said, Behold, he calleth Elias.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And one ran, and filled a sponge full of vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink, saying, Let him alone: let us see if Elias will come, and take him down.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And Jesus cried with a loud voice, and gave up the ghost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And the veil of the Temple was rent in twain, from the top to the bottom.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Now when the Centurion, which stood over against him, saw that he thus crying gave up the ghost, he said, Truly this man was the Son of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> There were also women, which beheld afar off, among whom was Mary Magdalene, and Mary (the mother of James the less, and of Joses) and Salome,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Which also when he was in Galilee, followed him, and ministered unto him, and many other women which came up with him unto Jerusalem.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And now when the night was come (because it was the day of the preparation that is before the Sabbath)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Joseph of Arimathea, an honorable counsellor, which also looked for the kingdom of God, came, and went in boldly unto Pilate, and asked the body of Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> And Pilate marvelled, if he were already dead, and called unto him the Centurion, and asked of him whether he had been any while dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> And when he knew the truth of the Centurion, he gave the body to Joseph:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> Who bought a linen cloth, and took him down, and wrapped him in the linen cloth, and laid him in a tomb that was hewn out of a rock, and rolled a stone unto the door of the sepulchre:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And Mary Magdalene, and Mary Joses mother, beheld where he should be laid.</p>
