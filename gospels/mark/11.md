# Mark 11

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">11</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And when they came near to Jerusalem, to Bethphage and Bethany unto the mount of Olives, he sent forth two of his disciples,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And said unto them, Go your ways into that town that is over against you, and as soon as ye shall enter into it, ye shall find a colt tied, whereon never man sat: loose him, and bring him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And if any man say unto you, Why do ye this? Say that the Lord hath need of him, and straightway he will send him hither.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And they went their way, and found a colt tied by the door without, in a place where two ways met, and they loosed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Then certain of them, that stood there, said unto them, What do ye loosing the colt?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And they said unto them, as Jesus had commanded them: So they let them go.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And they brought the colt to Jesus, and cast their garments on him, and he sat upon him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And many spread their garments in the way: other cut down branches off the trees, and strawed them in the way.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And they that went before, and they that followed, cried, saying, Hosanna: blessed be he that cometh in the Name of the Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Blessed be the kingdom that cometh in the Name of the Lord of our father David: Hosanna, O thou which art in the highest heavens.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> So Jesus entered into Jerusalem, and into the Temple: and when he had looked about on all things, and now it was evening, he went forth unto Bethany with the twelve.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And on the morrow when they were come out from Bethany, he was hungry.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And seeing a fig tree afar off, that had leaves, he went to see if he might find any thing thereon: but when he came unto it, he found nothing but leaves: for the time of figs was not yet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Then Jesus answered, and said to it, Never man eat fruit of thee hereafter while the world standeth: and his disciples heard it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And they came to Jerusalem, and Jesus went into the Temple, and began to cast out them that sold and bought in the Temple, and overthrew the tables of the money changers, and the seats of them that sold doves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Neither would he suffer that any man should carry a vessel through the Temple.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And he taught, saying unto them, Is it not written, Mine house shall be called the house of prayer unto all nations? but you have made it a den of thieves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And the Scribes and high Priests heard it, and sought how to destroy him: for they feared him, because the whole multitude was astonied at his doctrine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> But when even was come, Jesus went out of the city.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And in the morning as they journeyed together, they saw the fig tree dried up from the roots.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then Peter remembered, and said unto him, Master, behold, the fig tree which thou cursedst, is withered.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And Jesus answered, and said unto them, Have the faith of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> For verily I say unto you, that whosoever shall say unto this mountain, Be thou taken away, and cast into the sea, and shall not waver in his heart, but shall believe that those things which he saith, shall come to pass, whatsoever he saith, shall be done to him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Therefore I say unto you, Whatsoever ye desire when ye pray, believe that ye shall have it, and it shall be done unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> But when ye shall stand, and pray, forgive, if ye have any thing against any man, that your Father also which is in heaven, may forgive you your trespasses.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> For if you will not forgive, your Father which is in heaven, will not pardon you your trespasses.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Then they came again to Jerusalem: and as he walked in the Temple, there came to him the high Priests, and the Scribes, and the Elders,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And said unto him, By what authority doest thou these things? and who gave thee this authority, that thou shouldest do these things?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Then Jesus answered, and said unto them, I will also ask of you a certain thing, and answer ye me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> The baptism of John, was it from heaven, or of men? answer me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And they thought with themselves, saying, If we shall say, From heaven, he will say, Why then did ye not believe him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> But if we say, Of men, we fear the people: for all men counted John, that he was a Prophet in deed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Then they answered, and said unto Jesus, We cannot tell. And Jesus answered, and said unto them, Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.</p>
