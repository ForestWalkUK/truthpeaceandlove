# Mark 5

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And they came over to the other side of the sea into the country of the Gadarenes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And when he was come out of the ship, there met him incontinently out of the graves, a man which had an unclean spirit:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Who had his abiding among the graves, and no man could bind him, no not with chains:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Because that when he was often bound with fetters and chains, he plucked the chains asunder, and brake the fetters in pieces, neither could any man tame him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And always both night and day he cried in the mountains, and in the graves, and struck himself with stones.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And when he saw Jesus afar off, he ran, and worshipped him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And cried with a loud voice, and said, What have I to do with thee, Jesus the Son of the most high God? I will that thou swear to me by God, that thou torment me not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> (For he said unto him, Come out of the man, thou unclean spirit.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And he asked him, What is thy name? and he answered, saying, My name is Legion: for we are many.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And he prayed him instantly, that he would not send them away out of the country.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Now there was there in the mountains a great heard of swine, feeding.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And all the devils besought him, saying, Send us into the swine, that we may enter into them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And incontinently Jesus gave them leave. Then the unclean spirits went out, and entered into the swine, and the heard ran headlong from the high bank into the sea, (and there were about two thousand swine) and they were choked up in the sea.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And the swineherds fled, and told it in the city, and in the country, and they came out to see what it was that was done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And they came to Jesus, and saw him that had been possessed with the devil, and had the legion, sit both clothed, and in his right mind: and they were afraid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And they that saw it, told them, what was done to him that was possessed with the devil, and concerning the swine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then they began to pray him, that he would depart from their coasts.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And when he was come into the ship, he that had been possessed with the devil, prayed him that he might be with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Howbeit, Jesus would not suffer him, but said unto him, Go thy way home to thy friends, and show them what great things the Lord hath done unto thee, and how he hath had compassion on thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> So he departed, and began to publish in Decapolis, what great things Jesus had done unto him: and all men did marvel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And when Jesus was come over again by ship unto the other side, a great multitude gathered together to him, and he was near unto the sea.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And behold, there came one of the rulers of the Synagogue, whose name was Jairus: and when he saw him, he fell down at his feet,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And besought him instantly, saying, My little daughter lieth at point of death: I pray thee that thou wouldest come and lay thine hands on her, that she may be healed, and live.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Then he went with him, and a great multitude followed him, and thronged him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> (And there was a certain woman, which was diseased with an issue of blood twelve years,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And had suffered many things of many physicians, and had spent all that she had, and it availed her nothing, but she became much worse.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> When she had heard of Jesus, she came in the press behind, and touched his garment.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> For she said, If I may but touch his clothes, I shall be whole.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And straightway the course of her blood was dried up, and she felt in her body, that she was healed of that plague.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And immediately when Jesus did know in himself the virtue that went out of him, he turned him round about in the press, and said, Who hath touched my clothes?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And his disciples said unto him, Thou seest the multitude throng thee, and sayest thou, Who did touch me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And he looked round about, to see her that had done that.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And the woman feared and trembled: for she knew what was done in her, and she came and fell down before him, and told him the whole truth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And he said to her, Daughter, thy faith hath made thee whole: go in peace, and be whole of thy plague.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> While he yet spake, there came from the same ruler of the Synagogues house certain which said, Thy daughter is dead: why diseasest thou the Master any further?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> As soon as Jesus heard that word spoken, he said unto the ruler of the Synagogue, Be not afraid: only believe.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And he suffered no man to follow him save Peter and James, and John the brother of James.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> So he came unto the house of the ruler of the Synagogue, and saw the tumult, and them that wept and wailed greatly.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And he went in, and said unto them, Why make ye this trouble, and weep? the child is not dead, but sleepeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And they laughed him to scorn: but he put them all out, and took the father, and the mother of the child, and them that were with him, and entered in where the child lay,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And took the child by the hand, and said unto her, Talitha cumi, which is by interpretation, Maiden, I say unto thee, arise.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And straightway the maiden arose, and walked: for she was of the age of twelve years, and they were astonied out of measure.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> And he charged them straitly that no man should know of it, and commanded to give her meat.</p>
