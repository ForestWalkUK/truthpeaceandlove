# Luke 16

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And he said also unto his disciples, There was a certain rich man, which had a steward, and he was accused unto him, that he wasted his goods.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And he called him, and said unto him, How is it that I hear this of thee? Give an accounts of thy stewardship: for thou mayest be no longer steward.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then the steward said within himself, What shall I do? for my master taketh away from me the stewardship. I cannot dig, and to beg I am ashamed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> I know what I will do, that when I am put out of the stewardship, they may receive me into their houses.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Then called he unto him every one of his masters debtors, and said unto the first, How much owest thou unto my master?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And he said, An hundred measures of oil. And he said to him, Take thy writing, and sit down quickly, and write fifty.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then said he to another, How much owest thou? And he said, An hundred measures of wheat. Then he said to him, Take thy writing, and write four score.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And the Lord commended the unjust steward, because he had done wisely. Wherefore the children of this world are in their generation wiser than the children of light.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And I say unto you, Make you friends with the riches of iniquity, that when ye shall want, they may receive you into everlasting habitations.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> He that is faithful in the least, he is also faithful in much: and he that is unjust in the least, is unjust also in much.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> If then ye have not been faithful in the wicked riches, who will trust you in the true treasure?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And if ye have not been faithful in another mans goods, who shall give you that which is yours?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> No servant can serve two masters: for either he shall hate the one, and love the other: or else he shall lean to the one, and despise the other. Ye can not serve God and riches.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> All these things heard the Pharisees also which were covetous, and they scoffed at him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Then he said unto them, Ye are they, which justify your selves before men: but God knoweth your hearts: for that which is highly esteemed among men, is abomination in the sight of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> The Law and the Prophets endured until John: and since that time the kingdom of God is preached, and every man presseth into it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Now it is more easy that heaven and earth should pass away, then that one title of the Law should fall.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Whosoever putteth away his wife, and marieth another, committeth adultery: and whosoever marieth her that is put away from her husband, committeth adultery.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> There was a certain rich man, which was clothed in purple and fine linen, and fared well and delicately every day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Also there was a certain beggar named Lazarus, which was laid at his gate full of sores,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And desired to be refreshed with the crumbs that fell from the rich mans table: yea, and the dogs came and licked his sores.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And it was so that the beggar died, and was carried by the Angels into Abrahams bosom. The rich man also died, and was buried.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And being in hell in torments, he lift up his eyes, and saw Abraham a far off, and Lazarus in his bosom.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Then he cried, and said, Father Abraham, have mercy on me, and send Lazarus that he may dip the tip of his finger in water, and cool my tongue: for I am tormented in this flame.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> But Abraham said, Son, remember that thou in thy life time receivedst thy pleasures, and likewise Lazarus pains: now therefore is he comforted, and thou art tormented.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Besides all this, between you and us there is a great gulf set, so that they which would go from hence to you, can not: neither can they come from thence to us.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Then he said, I pray thee therefore, father, that thou wouldest send him to my fathers house,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> (For I have five brethren) that he may testify unto them, least they also come into this place of torment.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Abraham said unto him, They have Moses and the Prophets: let them hear them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And he said, Nay, father Abraham: but if one came unto them from the dead, they will amend their lives.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Then he said unto him, If they hear not Moses and the Prophets, neither will they be persuaded, though one rise from the dead again.</p>
