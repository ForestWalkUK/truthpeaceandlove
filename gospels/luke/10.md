# Luke 10

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> After these things, the Lord appointed other seventy also, and sent them, two and two before him into every city and place, whither he himself should come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And he said unto them, The harvest is great, but the labourers are few: pray therefore the Lord of the harvest to send forth labourers into his harvest.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Go your ways: behold, I send you forth as lambs among wolves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Bear no bag, neither scrip, nor shoes, and salute no man by the way.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And into whatsoever house ye enter, first say, Peace be to this house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And if the son of peace be there, your peace shall rest upon him: if not, it shall turn to you again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And in that house tarry still, eating and drinking such things as by them shall be set before you: for the labourer is worthy of his wages. Go not from house to house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> But into whatsoever city ye shall enter, if they receive you, eat such things as are set before you,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And heal the sick that are there, and say unto them, The kingdom of God is come near unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> But into whatsoever city ye shall enter, if they will not receive you, go your ways out into the streets of the same, and say,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Even the very dust, which cleaveth on us of your city, we wipe off against you: notwithstanding know this, that the kingdom of God was come near unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> For I say to you, that it shall be easier in that day for them of Sodom, then for that city.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Woe be to thee, Chorazin: woe be to thee, Bethsaida: for if the miracles had been done in Tyre and Sidon, which have been done in you, they had a great while agone repented, sitting in sackcloth and ashes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Therefore it shall be easier for Tyre, and Sidon, at the judgement, then for you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And thou, Capernaum, which art exalted to heaven, shalt be thrust down to hell.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> He that heareth you, heareth me: and he that despiseth you, despiseth me: and he that despiseth me, despiseth him that sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And the seventy turned again with joy, saying, Lord, even the devils are subdued to us through thy Name.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And he said unto them, I saw Satan, like lightening, fall down from heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Behold, I give unto you power to tread on Serpents, and Scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy, and nothing shall hurt you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Nevertheless, in this rejoice not, that the spirits are subdued unto you: but rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> That same hour rejoiced Jesus in the spirit, and said, I confess unto thee, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that thou hast hid these things from the wise and understanding, and hast revealed them to babes: even so, Father, because it so pleased thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> All things are given me of my Father: and no man knoweth who the Son is, but the Father: neither who the Father is, save the Son, and he to whom the Son will reveal him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And he turned to his disciples, and said secretly, Blessed are the eyes, which see that ye see.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> For I tell you that many Prophets and Kings have desired to see those things, which ye see, and have not seen them: and to hear those things which ye hear, and have not heard them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then behold, a certain Lawyer stood up, and tempted him, saying, Master, what shall I do, to inherit eternal life?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And he said unto him, What is written in the Law? how readest thou?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And he answered, and said, Thou shalt love thy Lord God with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy strength, and with all thy thought, and thy neighbour as thy self.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then he said unto him, Thou hast answered right: this do, and thou shalt live.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> But he willing to justify himself, said unto Jesus, Who is then my neighbour?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And Jesus answered, and said, A certain man went down from Jerusalem to Jericho, and fell among thieves, and they robbed him of his raiment, and wounded him, and departed, leaving him half dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Now so it fell out, that there came down a certain Priest that same way, and when he saw him, he passed by on the other side.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And likewise also a Levite, when he was come near to the place, went and looked on him, and passed by on the other side.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Then a certain Samaritan, as he journeyed, came near unto him, and when he saw him, he had compassion on him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And went to him, and bound up his wounds, and poured in oil and wine, and put him on his own beast, and brought him to an Inn, and made provision for him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And on the morrow when he departed, he took out two pence, and gave them to the host, and said unto him, Take care of him, and whatsoever thou spendest more, when I come again, I will recompense thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Which now of these three, thinkest thou, was neighbour unto him that fell among the thieves?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And he said, He that showed mercy on him. Then said Jesus unto him, Go, and do thou likewise.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Now it came to pass, as they went, that he entered into a certain town, and a certain woman named Martha, received him into her house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And she had a sister called Mary, which also sat at Jesus feet, and heard his preaching.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> But Martha was cumbered about much serving, and came to him, and said, Master, doest thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore, that she help me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And Jesus answered, and said unto her, Martha, Martha, thou carest, and art troubled about many things:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> But one thing is needful, Mary hath chosen the good part, which shall not be taken away from her.</p>
</div>
