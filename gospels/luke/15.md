# Luke 15

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<p class="bible-note">In this edition of the Geneva Bible, Luke 15 has 31 verses. Its last verse holds what many other Bibles number as verses 31 and 32.</p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">15</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Then resorted unto him all the Publicans and sinners, to hear him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Therefore the Pharisees and Scribes murmured, saying, He receiveth sinners, and eateth with them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then spake he this parable to them, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> What man of you having an hundred sheep, if he lose one of them, doeth not leave ninety and nine in the wilderness, and go after that which is lost, until he find it?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And when he hath found it, he layeth it on his shoulders with joy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And when he cometh home, he calleth together his friends and neighbours, saying unto them, Rejoice with me: for I have found my sheep which was lost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> I say unto you, that likewise joy shall be in heaven for one sinner that converteth, more than for ninety and nine just men, which need none amendment of life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Either what woman having ten groats, if she lose one groat, doth not light a candle, and sweep the house, and seek diligently till she find it?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And when she hath found it, she calleth her friends, and neighbours, saying, Rejoice with me: for I have found the groat which I had lost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Likewise I say unto you, there is joy in the presence of the Angels of God, for one sinner that converteth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> He said moreover, A certain man had two sons.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And the younger of them said to his father, Father, give me the portion of the goods that falleth to me. So he divided unto them his substance.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> So not many days after, when the younger son had gathered all together, he took his journey into a far country, and there he wasted his goods with riotous living.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Now when he had spent all, there arose a great dearth throughout that land, and he began to be in necessity.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Then he went and clave to a citizen of that country, and he sent him to his farm, to feed swine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And he would fain have filled his belly with the husks, that the swine ate: but no man gave them him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then he came to him self, and said, How many hired servants at my fathers have bread enough, and I die for hunger?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> I will rise and go to my father, and say unto him, Father, I have sinned against heave, and before thee,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And am no more worthy to be called thy son: make me as one of thy hired servants.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> So he arose and came to his father, and when he was yet a great way off, his father saw him, and had compassion, and ran and fell on his neck, and kissed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And the son said unto him, Father, I have sinned against heaven, and before thee, and am no more worthy to be called thy son.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Then the father said to his servants, Bring forth the best robe, and put it on him, and put a ring on his hand, and shoes on his feet,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And bring the fat calf, and kill him, and let us eat, and be merry:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> For this my son was dead, and is alive again: and he was lost, but he is found. And they began to be merry.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Now the elder brother was in the field, and when he came and drew near to the house, he heard melody, and dancing,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And called one of his servants, and asked what those things meant.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And he said unto him, Thy brother is come, and thy father hath killed the fat calf, because he hath received him safe and sound.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then he was angry, and would not go in: therefore came his father out and entreated him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> But he answered and said to his father, Lo, these many years have I done thee service, neither brake I at any time thy commandment, and yet thou never gavest me a kid that I might make merry with my friends.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> But when this thy son was come, which hath devoured thy good with harlots, thou hast for his sake killed the fat calf.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And he said unto him, Son, thou art ever with me, and all that I have, is thine. It was meet that we should make merry, and be glad: for this thy brother was dead, and is alive again: and he was lost, but he is found.</p>
