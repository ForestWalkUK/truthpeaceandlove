<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">3</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Now in the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, Pontius Pilate being governor of Judea, and Herod being Tetrarch of Galilee, and his brother Philip Tetrarch of Ituraea, and of the country of Trachonitis, and Lysanias the Tetrarch of Abilene,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> (When Annas and Caiaphas were the high Priests) the word of God came unto John, the son of Zacharias in the wilderness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And he came into all the coasts about Jordan, preaching the baptism of repentance for the remission of sins,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> As it is written in the book of the sayings of Esaias the Prophet, which saith, The voice of him that crieth in the wilderness is, Prepare ye the way of the Lord: make his paths straight.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Every valley shall be filled, and every mountain and hill shall be brought low, and crooked things shall be made straight, and the rough ways shall be made smooth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And all flesh shall see the salvation of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then said he to the people that were come out to be baptized of him, O generations of vipers, who hath forewarned you to flee from the wrath to come?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Bring forth therefore fruits worthy amendment of life, and begin not to say with your selves, We have Abraham to our father: for I say unto you, that God is able of these stones to raise up children unto Abraham.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Now also is the axe laid unto the root of the trees: therefore every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit, shall be hewn down, and cast into the fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Then the people asked him, saying, What shall we do then?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And he answered, and said unto them, He that hath two coats, let him part with him that hath none: and he that hath meat, let him do likewise.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then came there Publicans also to be baptized, and said unto him, Master, what shall we do?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And he said unto them, Require no more than that which is appointed unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> The soldiers likewise demanded of him, saying, And what shall we do? And he said unto them, Do violence to no man, neither accuse any falsely, and be content with your wages.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> As the people waited, and all men mused in their hearts of John, if he were not that Christ,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> John answered, and said to them all, In deed I baptize you with water, but one stronger than I, cometh, whose shoes latchet I am not worthy to unloose: he will baptize you with the holy Ghost, and with fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Whose fan is in his hand, and he will make clean his floor, and will gather the wheat into his garner, but the chaff will he burn up with fire that never shall be quenched.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Thus then exhorting with many other things, he preached unto the people.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> But when Herod the Tetrarch was rebuked of him, for Herodias his brother Philips wife, and for all the evils which Herod had done,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> He added yet this above all, that he shut up John in prison.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Now it came to pass, as all the people were baptized, and that Jesus was baptized and did pray, that the heaven was opened:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And the holy Ghost came down in a bodily shape like a dove, upon him, and there was a voice from heaven, saying, Thou art my beloved Son: in thee I am well pleased.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And Jesus himself began to be about thirty year of age, being as men supposed the son of Joseph, which was the son of Eli,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> The son of Matthat, the son of Levi, the son of Melchi, the son of Janna, the son of Joseph,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> The son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Naum, the son of Esli, the son of Nagge,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> The son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semei, the son of Joseph, the son of Juda,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> The son of Joanna, the son of Rhesa, the son of Zorobabel, the son of Salathiel, the son of Neri,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> The son of Melchi, the son of Addi, the son of Cosam, the son of Elmodam, the son of Er,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> The son of Jose, the son of Eliezer, the son of Jorim, the son of Matthat, the son of Levi,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> The son of Simeon, the son of Juda, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonan, the son of Eliakim,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> The son of Melea, the son of Menan, the son of Mattatha, the son of Nathan, the son of David,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> The son of Jesse, the son of Obed, the son of Booz, the son of Salmon, the son of Naasson,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> The son of Aminadab, the son of Aram, the son of Esrom, the son of Phares, the son of Juda,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> The son of Jacob, the son of Isaac, the son of Abraham, the son of Thara, the son of Nachor,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> The son of Saruch, the son of Ragau, the son of Phalec, the son of Heber, the son of Sala,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> The son of Cainan, the son of Arphaxad, the son of Sem, the son of Noe, the son of Lamech,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> The son of Mathusala, the son of Enoch, the son of Jared, the son of Maleleel, the son of Cainan,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> The son of Enos, the son of Seth, the son of Adam, the son of God.</p>
</div>
