# Luke 23

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">23</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Then the whole multitude of them arose, and led him unto Pilate.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And they began to accuse him, saying, We have found this man perverting the nation, and forbidding to pay tribute to Caesar, saying, That he is Christ a King.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And Pilate asked him, saying, Art thou the King of the Jews? And he answered him, and said, Thou sayest it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Then said Pilate to the high Priests, and to the people, I find no fault in this man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> But they were the more fierce, saying, He moveth the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning at Galilee, even to this place.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Now when Pilate heard of Galilee, he asked whether the man were a Galilean.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And when he knew that he was of Herods jurisdiction, he sent him to Herod, which was also at Jerusalem in those days.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And when Herod saw Jesus, he was exceedingly glad: for he was desirous to see him of a long season, because he had heard many things of him, and trusted to have seen some sign done by him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Then questioned he with him of many things: but he answered him nothing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> The high Priests also and Scribes stood forth, and accused him vehemently.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And Herod with his men of war, despised him, and mocked him, and arrayed him in white, and sent him again to Pilate.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And the same day Pilate and Herod were made friends together: for before they were enemies one to another.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then Pilate called together the high Priests and the rulers, and the people,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And said unto them, Ye have brought this man unto me, as one that perverted the people: and behold, I have examined him before you, and have found no fault in this man, of those things whereof ye accuse him:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> No, nor yet Herod: for I sent you to him: and lo, nothing worthy of death is done of him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> I will therefore chastise him, and let him loose.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> (For of necessity he must have let one loose unto them at the feast.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Then all the multitude cried at once, saying, Away with him, and deliver unto us Barabbas:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Which for a certain insurrection made in the city, and murder, was cast in prison.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Then Pilate spake again to them, willing to let Jesus loose.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> But they cried, saying, Crucify, crucify him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And he said unto them the third time, But what evil hath he done? I find no cause of death in him: I will therefore chastise him, and let him loose.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> But they were instant with loud voices, and required that he might be crucified: and the voices of them and of the high Priests prevailed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> So Pilate gave sentence, that it should be as they required.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And he let loose unto them him that for insurrection and murder was cast into prison, whom they desired, and delivered Jesus to do with him what they would.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And as they led him away, they caught one Simon of Cyrene, coming out of the field, and on him they laid the cross, to bear it after Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And there followed him a great multitude of people, and of women, which women bewailed and lamented him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> But Jesus turned back unto them, and said, Daughters of Jerusalem, weep not for me, but weep for your selves, and for your children.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> For behold, the days will come, when men shall say, Blessed are the barren, and the wombs that never bare, and the paps which never gave suck.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Then shall they begin to say to the mountains, Fall on us: and to the hills, Cover us.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> For if they do these things to a green tree, what shall be done to the dry?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And there were two others, which were evil doers, led with him to be slain.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And when they were come to the place, which is called Calvary, there they crucified him, and the evil doers: one at the right hand, and the other at the left.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them: for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And the people stood, and beheld: and the rulers mocked him with them, saying, He saved others: let him save himself, if he be that Christ, the Chosen of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> The soldiers also mocked him, and came and offered him vinegar,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And said, If thou be the King of the Jews, save thy self.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And a superscription was also written over him, in Greek letters, and in Latin, and in Hebrew, THIS IS THAT KING OF THE JEWS.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And one of the evil doers, which were hanged, railed on him, saying, If thou be that Christ, save thy self and us.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> But the other answered, and rebuked him, saying, Fearest thou not God, seeing thou art in the same condemnation?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> We are in deed righteously here: for we receive things worthy of that we have done: but this man hath done nothing amiss.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And he said unto Jesus, Lord, remember me, when thou comest into thy kingdom.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Then Jesus said unto him, Verily I say unto thee, to day shalt thou be with me in Paradise.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> And it was about the sixth hour: and there was a darkness over all the land, until the ninth hour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> And the Sun was darkened, and the veil of the Temple rent through the midst.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> And Jesus cried with a loud voice, and said, Father, into thine hands I commend my spirit. And when he thus had said, he gave up the ghost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> Now when the Centurion saw what was done, he glorified God, saying, Of a surety this man was just.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> And all the people that came together to that sight, beholding the things, which were done, smote their breasts, and returned.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> And all his acquaintance stood a far off, and the women that followed him from Galilee, beholding these things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> And behold, there was a man named Joseph, which was a counsellor, a good man and a just.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> He did not consent to the counsel and deed of them, which was of Arimathea, a city of the Jews: who also himself waited for the kingdom of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> He went unto Pilate, and asked the body of Jesus,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> And took it down, and wrapped it in a linen cloth, and laid it in a tomb hewn out of a rock, wherein was never man yet laid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> And that day was the preparation, and the Sabbath drew on.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> And the women also that followed after, which came with him from Galilee, beheld the sepulchre, and how his body was laid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> And they returned and prepared odours, and ointments, and rested the Sabbath day according to the commandment.</p>
