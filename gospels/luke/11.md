# Luke 11

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And so it was, that as he was praying in a certain place, when he ceased, one of his disciples said unto him, Lord, teach us to pray, as John also taught his disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And he said unto them, When ye pray, say, Our Father, which art in heaven, hallowed be thy Name: Thy kingdom come: Let thy will be done, even in earth, as it is in heaven:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Our daily bread give us for the day:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And forgive us our sins: for even we forgive every man that is indebted to us: And lead us not into temptation: but deliver us from evil.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Moreover he said unto them, Which of you shall have a friend, and shall go to him at midnight, and say unto him, Friend, lend me three loaves?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> For a friend of mine is come out of the way to me, and I have nothing to set before him:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And he within should answer, and say, Trouble me not: the door is now shut, and my children are with me in bed: I can not rise and give them to thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> I say unto you, Though he would not arise and give him, because he is his friend, yet doubtless because of his importunity, he would rise, and give him as many as he needed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And I say unto you, Ask, and it shall be given you: seek, and ye shall find: knock, and it shall be opened unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> For every one that asketh, receiveth: and he that seeketh, findeth: and to him that knocketh, it shall be opened.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> If a son shall ask bread of any of you that is a father, will he give him a stone? or if he ask a fish, will he for a fish give him a serpent?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Or if he ask an egg, will he give him a scorpion?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> If ye then which are evil, can give good gifts unto your children, how much more shall your heavenly Father give the holy Ghost to them, that desire him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Then he cast out a devil which was dumb: and when the devil was gone out, the dumb spake, and the people wondered.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> But some of them said, He casteth out devils through Beelzebub the chief of the devils.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And others tempted him, seeking of him a sign from heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> But he knew their thoughts, and said unto them, Every kingdom divided against it self, shall be desolate, and an house divided against an house, falleth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> So if Satan also be divided against himself, how shall his kingdom stand, because ye say that I cast out devils through Beelzebub?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> If I through Beelzebub cast out devils, by whom do your children cast them out? Therefore shall they be your judges.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> But if I by the finger of God cast out devils, doubtless the kingdom of God is come unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> When a strong man armed keepeth his palace, the things that he possesseth, are in peace.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> But when a stronger than he, cometh upon him, and overcometh him: he taketh from him all his armour wherein he trusted, and divideth his spoils.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> He that is not with me, is against me: and he that gathereth not with me, scattereth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> When the unclean spirit is gone out of a man, he walketh through dry places, seeking rest: and when he findeth none he saith, I will return unto mine house whence I came out.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And when he cometh, he findeth it swept and garnished.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Then goeth he, and taketh to him seven other spirits worse than himself: and they enter in, and dwell there: so the last state of that man is worse than the first.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And it came to pass as he said these things, a certain woman of the company lifted up her voice, and said unto him, Blessed is the womb that bare thee, and the paps which thou hast sucked.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> But he said, Yea, rather blessed are they that hear the word of God, and keep it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And when the people were gathered thick together, he began to say, This is a wicked generation: they seek a sign, and there shall no sign be given them, but the sign of Jonas the Prophet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> For as Jonas was a sign to the Ninevites: so shall also the Son of man be to this generation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> The Queen of the South shall rise in judgement, with the men of this generation, and shall condemn them: for she came from the utmost parts of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and behold, a greater than Solomon is here.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> The men of Nineve shall rise in judgement with this generation, and shall condemn it: for they repented at the preaching of Jonas: and behold, a greater than Jonas is here.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> No man when he hath lighted a candle, putteth it in a privy place, neither under a bushel: but on a candlestick, that they which come in, may see the light.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> The light of the body is the eye: therefore when thine eye is single, then is thy whole body light: but if thine eye be evil, then thy body is dark.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Take heed therefore, that the light which is in thee, be not darkness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> If therefore thy whole body shall be light, having no part dark, then shall all be light, even as when a candle doth light thee with the brightness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And as he spake, a certain Pharisee besought him to dine with him: and he went in, and sat down at table.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And when the Pharisee saw it, he marvelled that he had not first washed before dinner.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And the Lord said to him, In deed ye Pharisees make clean the outside of the cup, and of the platter: but the inward part is full of ravening and wickedness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> Ye fools, did not he that made that which is without, make that which is within also?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Therefore, give alms of those things which you have, and behold, all things shall be clean to you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> But woe be to you, Pharisees: for ye tithe the mint and the rue, and all manner herbs, and pass over judgement and the love of God: these ought ye to have done, and not to have left the other undone.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Woe be to you, Pharisees: for ye love the uppermost seats in the Synagogues, and greetings in the markets.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> Woe be to you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites: for ye are as graves which appear not, and the men that walk over them, perceive not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Then answered one of the Lawyers, and said unto him, Master, thus saying thou puttest us to rebuke also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> And he said, Woe be to you also, ye Lawyers: for ye lade men with burdens grievous to be borne, and ye your selves touch not the burdens with one of your fingers.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> Woe be to you: for ye build the sepulchres of the Prophets, and your fathers killed them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> Truly ye bear witness, and allow the deeds of your fathers: for they killed them, and ye build their sepulchres.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> Therefore said the wisdom of God, I will send them Prophets and Apostles, and of them they shall slay, and persecute away,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> That the blood of all the Prophets, shed from the foundation of the world, may be required of this generation,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> From the blood of Abel unto the blood of Zacharias, which was slain between the altar and the Temple: verily I say unto you, it shall be required of this generation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> Woe be to you, Lawyers: for ye have taken away the key of knowledge: ye entered not in your selves, and them that came in, ye forbade.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> And as he said these things unto them, the Scribes and Pharisees began to urge him sore, and to provoke him to speak of many things,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> Laying wait for him, and seeking to catch some thing of his mouth, whereby they might accuse him.</p>
</div>
