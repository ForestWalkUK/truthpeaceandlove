<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">4</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And Jesus full of the holy Ghost returned from Jordan, and was led by that Spirit into the wilderness,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And was there forty days tempted of the devil, and in those days he did eat nothing: but when they were ended, he afterward was hungry.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then the devil said unto him, If thou be the Son of God, command this stone that it be made bread.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> But Jesus answered him, saying, It is written, That man shall not live by bread only, but by every word of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Then the devil took him up into an high mountain, and showed him all the kingdoms of the world, in the twinkling of an eye.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And the devil said unto him, All this power will I give thee, and the glory of those kingdoms: for that is delivered to me: and to whomsoever I will, I give it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> If thou therefore wilt worship me, they shall be all thine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> But Jesus answered him, and said, Hence from me, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him alone thou shalt serve.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Then he brought him to Jerusalem, and set him on a pinnacle of the Temple, and said unto him, If thou be the Son of God, cast thy self down from hence,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> For it is written, That he will give his Angels charge over thee to keep thee:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And with their hands they shall lift thee up, least at any time thou shouldest dash thy foot against a stone.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And Jesus answered, and said unto him, It is said, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And when the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a little season.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And Jesus returned by the power of the spirit into Galilee: and there went a fame of him throughout all the region round about.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> For he taught in their Synagogues, and was honoured of all men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And he came to Nazareth where he had been brought up, and (as his custom was) went into the Synagogue on the Sabbath day, and stood up to reade.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And there was delivered unto him the book of the Prophet Esaias: and when he had opened the book, he found the place, where it was written,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me, that I should preach the Gospel to the poor: he hath sent me, that I should heal the broken hearted, that I should preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, that I should set at liberty them that are bruised:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And that I should preach the acceptable year of the Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And he closed the book, and gave it again to the minister, and sat down: and the eyes of all that were in the Synagogue were fastened on him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then he began to say unto them, This day is the Scripture fulfilled in your ears.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And all bare him witness, and wondered at the gracious words, which proceeded out of his mouth, and said, Is not this Josephs son?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Then he said unto them, Ye will surely say unto me this proverb, Physician, heal thy self: whatsoever we have heard done in Capernaum, do it here likewise in thine own country.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And he said, Verily I say unto you, No Prophet is accepted in his own country.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> But I tell you of a truth, many widows were in Israel in the days of Elias, when heaven was shut three years and six months, when great famine was throughout all the land:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> But unto none of them was Elias sent, save into Sarepta, a city of Sidon, unto a certain widow.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Also many lepers were in Israel, in the time of Eliseus the Prophet: yet none of them was made clean, saving Naaman the Syrian.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then all that were in the Synagogue, when they heard it, were filled with wrath,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And rose up, and thrust him out of the city, and led him unto the edge of the hill, whereon their city was built, to cast him down headlong.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> But he passed through the midst of them, and went his way,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And came down into Capernaum a city of Galilee, and there taught them on the Sabbath days.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And they were astonied at his doctrine: for his word was with authority.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And in the Synagogue there was a man which had a spirit of an unclean devil, which cried with a loud voice,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Saying, Oh, what have we to do with thee, thou Jesus of Nazareth? art thou come to destroy us? I know who thou art, even the holy one of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, Hold thy peace, and come out of him. Then the devil throwing him in the midst of them, came out of him, and hurt him nothing at all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> So fear came on them all, and they spake among themselves, saying, What thing is this: for with authority and power he commandeth the foul spirits, and they come out?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And the fame of him spread abroad throughout all the places of the country round about.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And he rose up, and came out of the Synagogue, and entered into Simons house. And Simons wives mother was taken with a great fever, and they required him for her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Then he stood over her, and rebuked the fever, and it left her, and immediately she arose, and ministered unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> Now at the sun setting, all they that had sick folks of divers diseases, brought them unto him, and he laid his hands on every one of them, and healed them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And devils also came out of many, crying, and saying, Thou art that Christ that Son of God: but he rebuked them, and suffered them not to say that they knew him to be that Christ.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And when it was day, he departed, and went forth into a desert place, and the people sought him, and came to him, and kept him that he should not depart from them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> But he said unto them, Surely I must also preach the kingdom of God to other cities: for therefore am I sent.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> And he preached in the Synagogues of Galilee.</p>
