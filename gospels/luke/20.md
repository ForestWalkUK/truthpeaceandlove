# Luke 20

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And it came to pass, that on one of those days, as he taught the people in the Temple, and preached the Gospel, the high Priests and the Scribes came upon him with the Elders,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And spake unto him, saying, Tell us by what authority thou doest these things, or who is he that hath given thee this authority?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And he answered, and said unto them, I also will ask you one thing: tell me therefore:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> The baptism of John, was it from heaven, or of men?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And they reasoned within themselves, saying, If we shall say, From heaven, he will say, Why then believed ye him not?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> But if we shall say, Of men, all the people will stone us: for they be persuaded that John was a Prophet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Therefore they answered, that they could not tell whence it was.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Then Jesus said unto them, Neither tell I you, by what authority I do these things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Then began he to speak to the people this parable, A certain man planted a vineyard, and let it forth to husbandmen: and went into a strange country, for a great time.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And at the time convenient he sent a servant to the husbandmen, that they should give him of the fruit of the vineyard: but the husbandmen did beat him, and sent him away empty.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Again he sent yet another servant: and they did beat him, and foul entreated him, and sent him away empty.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Moreover he sent the third, and him they wounded, and cast out.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then said the Lord of the vineyard, What shall I do? I will send my beloved son: it may be that they will do reverence, when they see him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> But when the husbandmen saw him, they reasoned with themselves, saying, This is the heir: come, let us kill him, that the inheritance may be ours.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> So they cast him out of the vineyard, and killed him. What shall the Lord of the vineyard therefore do unto them?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> He will come and destroy these husbandmen, and will give out his vineyard to others. But when they heard it, they said, God forbid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And he beheld them, and said, What meaneth this then that is written, The stone that the builders refused, that is made the head of the corner?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Whosoever shall fall upon that stone, shall be broken: and on whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Then the high Priests, and the Scribes the same hour went about to lay hands on him: (but they feared the people) for they perceived that he had spoken this parable against them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And they watched him, and sent forth spies, which should fain themselves just men, to take him in his talk, and to deliver him unto the power and authority of the governor.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And they asked him, saying, Master, we know that thou sayest, and teachest right, neither doest thou accept mans person, but teachest the way of God truly.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Is it lawful for us to give Caesar tribute or no?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> But he perceived their craftiness, and said unto them, Why tempt ye me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Show me a penny. Whose image and superscription hath it? They answered, and said, Caesars.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then he said unto them, Give then unto Caesar the things which are Caesars, and to God those which are Gods.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And they could not reprove his saying before the people: but they marvelled at his answer, and held their peace.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Then came to him certain of the Sadducees (which deny that there is any resurrection) and they asked him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Saying, Master, Moses wrote unto us, If any mans brother die having a wife, and he die without children, that his brother should take his wife, and raise up seed unto his brother.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Now there were seven brethren, and the first took a wife, and he died without children.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And the second took the wife, and he died childless.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Then the third took her: and so likewise the seven died, and left no children.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And last of all the woman died also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Therefore at the resurrection, whose wife of them shall she be? for seven had her to wife.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Then Jesus answered, and said unto them, The children of this world marry wives, and are married.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> But they which shall be counted worthy to enjoy that world, and the resurrection from the dead, neither marry wives, neither are married.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> For they can die no more, forasmuch as they are equal unto the Angels, and are the sons of God, since they are the children of the resurrection.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And that the dead shall rise again, even Moses showed it besides the bush, when he said, The Lord is the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> For he is not the God of the dead, but of them which live: for all live unto him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Then certain of the Scribes answered, and said, Master, thou hast well said.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And after that, durst they not ask him any thing at all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Then said he unto them, How say they that Christ is Davids son?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And David himself saith in the book of the Psalms, The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit at my right hand,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Till I shall make thine enemies thy footstool.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> Seeing David called him Lord, how is he then his son?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Then in the audience of all the people he said unto his disciples,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> Beware of the Scribes, which willingly go in long robes, and love salutations in the markets, and the highest seats in the assemblies, and the chief rooms at feasts:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> Which devour widows houses, and in show make long prayers: These shall receive greater damnation.</p>
</div>
