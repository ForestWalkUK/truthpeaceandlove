# Luke 1

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Forasmuch as many have taken in hand to set forth the story of those things, whereof we are fully persuaded,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> As they have delivered them unto us, which from the beginning saw them their selves, and were ministers of the word,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> It seemed good also to me (most noble Theophilus) as soon as I had searched out perfectly all things from the beginning, to write unto thee thereof from point to point,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> That thou mightest acknowledge the certainty of those things, whereof thou hast been instructed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> In the time of Herod King of Judea, there was a certain Priest named Zacharias, of the course of Abia: and his wife was of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elisabeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Both were just before God, and walked in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord, without reproof.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And they had no child, because that Elisabeth was barren: and both were well stricken in age.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And it came to pass, as he executed the Priests office before God, as his course came in order,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> According to the custom of the Priests office, his lot was to burn incense, when he went into the Temple of the Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And the whole multitude of the people were without in prayer, while the incense was burning.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Then appeared unto him an Angel of the Lord standing at the right side of the altar of incense.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And when Zacharias saw him, he was troubled, and fear fell upon him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> But the Angel said unto him, Fear not, Zacharias: for thy prayer is heard, and thy wise Elisabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name John.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And thou shalt have joy and gladness, and many shall rejoice at his birth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> For he shall be great in the sight of the Lord, and shall neither drink wine, nor strong drink: and he shall be filled with the holy Ghost, even from his mothers womb.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And many of the children of Israel shall he turn to their Lord God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> For he shall go before him in the spirit and power of Elias, to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children, and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just men, to make ready a people prepared for the Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Then Zacharias said unto the Angel, Whereby shall I know this? for I am an old man, and my wife is of a great age.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And the Angel answered, and said unto him, I am Gabriel that stand in the presence of God, and am sent to speak unto thee, and to show thee these good tidings.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And behold, thou shalt be dumb, and not be able to speak, until the day that these things be done, because thou believedst not my words, which shall be fulfilled in their season.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Now the people waited for Zacharias, and marvelled that he tarried so long in the Temple.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And when he came out, he could not speak unto them: then they perceived that he had seen a vision in the Temple: For he made signs unto them, and remained dumb.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And it came to pass, when the days of his office were fulfilled, that he departed to his own house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And after those days, his wife Elisabeth conceived, and hid her self five months, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Thus hath the Lord dealt with me, in the days wherein he looked on me, to take from me my rebuke among men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And in the sixth month, the Angel Gabriel was sent from God unto a city of Galilee, named Nazareth,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> To a virgin affianced to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David, and the virgins name was Mary.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And the Angel went in unto her, and said, Hail thou that art freely beloved: the Lord is with thee: blessed art thou among women.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And when she saw him, she was troubled at his saying, and thought what manner of salutation that should be.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Then the Angel said unto her, Fear not, Mary: for thou hast found favour with God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> For lo, thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and bear a son, and shalt call his name Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the most High, and the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And he shall reign over the house of Jacob for ever, and of his kingdom shall be none end.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Then said Mary unto the Angel, How shall this be, seeing I know not man?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And the Angel answered, and said unto her, The holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the most High shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee, shall be called the Son of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And behold, thy cousin Elisabeth, she hath also conceived a son in her old age: and this is her sixth month, which was called barren.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> For with God shall nothing be impossible.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Then Mary said, Behold the servant of the Lord: be it unto me according to thy word. So the Angel departed from her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And Mary arose in those days, and went into the hill country with hast to a city of Juda,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And entered into the house of Zacharias, and saluted Elisabeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And it came to pass, as Elisabeth heard the salutation of Mary, the babe sprang in her belly, and Elisabeth was filled with the holy Ghost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And she cried with a loud voice, and said, Blessed art thou among women, because the fruit of thy womb is blessed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> And whence cometh this to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> For lo, as soon as the voice of thy salutation sounded in mine ears, the babe sprang in my belly for joy,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> And blessed is she that believed: for those things shall be performed, which were told her from the Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> Then Mary said, My soul magnifieth the Lord,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And my spirit rejoiceth in God my Saviour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> For he hath looked on the poor degree of his servant: for behold, from henceforth shall all ages call me blessed,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> Because he that is mighty, hath done for me great things, and holy is his Name.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> And his mercy is from generation to generation on them that fear him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> He hath showed strength with his arm: he hath scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> He hath put down the mighty from their seats, and exalted them of low degree.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> He hath filled the hungry with good things, and sent away the rich empty.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> He hath upholden Israel his servant to be mindful of his mercy</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> (As he hath spoken to our fathers, to wit, to Abraham, and his seed) for ever.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> And Mary abode with her about three months: after, she returned to her own house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">57</span> Now Elisabeths time was fulfilled, that she should be delivered, and she brought forth a son.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">58</span> And her neighbours, and cousins heard tell how the Lord had showed his great mercy upon her, and they rejoiced with her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">59</span> And it was so that on the eight day they came to circumcise the babe, and called him Zacharias after the name of his father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">60</span> But his mother answered, and said, Not so, but he shall be called John.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">61</span> And they said unto her, There is none of thy kindred, that is named with this name.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">62</span> Then they made signs to his father, how he would have him called.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">63</span> So he asked for writing tables, and wrote, saying, His name is John, and they marvelled all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">64</span> And his mouth was opened immediately, and his tongue, and he spake and praised God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">65</span> Then fear came on all them that dwelt near unto them, and all these words were noised abroad throughout all the hill country of Judea.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">66</span> And all they that heard them, laid them up in their hearts, saying, What manner child shall this be! and the hand of the Lord was with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">67</span> Then his father Zacharias was filled with the holy Ghost, and prophesied, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">68</span> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, because he hath visited and redeemed his people,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">69</span> And hath raised up the horne of salvation unto us, in the house of his servant David,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">70</span> As he spake by the mouth of his holy Prophets, which were since the world began, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">71</span> That he would send us deliverance from our enemies, and from the hands of all that hate us,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">72</span> That he might show mercy towards our fathers, and remember his holy covenant,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">73</span> And the oath which he sware to our father Abraham.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">74</span> Which was, that he would grant unto us, that we being delivered out of the hands of our enemies, should serve him without fear,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">75</span> All the days of our life, in holiness and righteousness before him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">76</span> And thou, babe, shalt be called the Prophet of the most High: for thou shalt go before the face of the Lord, to prepare his ways,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">77</span> And to give knowledge of salvation unto his people, by the remission of their sins,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">78</span> Through the tender mercy of our God, whereby the day spring from an high hath visited us,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">79</span> To give light to them that sit in darkness, and in the shadow of death, and to guide our feet into the way of peace.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">80</span> And the child grew, and waxed strong in spirit, and was in the wilderness, till the day came that he should show him self unto Israel.</p>
</div>
