# Luke 24

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Now the first day of the week early in the morning, they came unto the sepulchre, and brought the odours, which they had prepared, and certain women with them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And they found the stone rolled away from the sepulchre,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And went in, but found not the body of the Lord Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And it came to pass, that as they were amazed thereat, behold, two men suddenly stood by them in shining vestures.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And as they were afraid, and bowed down their faces to the earth, they said to them, Why seek ye him that liveth, among the dead?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> He is not here, but is risen: remember how he spake unto you, when he was yet in Galilee,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Saying, that the son of man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and the third day rise again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And they remembered his words,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And returned from the sepulchre, and told all these things unto the eleven, and to all the remnant.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Now it was Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary the mother of James, and other women with them, which told these things unto the Apostles.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> But their words seemed unto them, as a feigned thing, neither believed they them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then arose Peter, and ran unto the sepulchre, and looked in, and saw the linen clothes laid by themselves, and departed wondering in himself at that which was come to pass.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And behold, two of them went that same day to a town which was from Jerusalem about threescore furlongs, called Emmaus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And they talked together of all these things that were done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And it came to pass, as they communed together, and reasoned, that Jesus himself drew near, and went with them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> But their eyes were holden, that they could not know him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And he said unto them, What manner of communications are these that ye have one to another as ye walk and are sad?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And the one (named Cleopas) answered, and said unto him, Art thou only a stranger in Jerusalem, and hast not known the things which are come to pass therein in these days?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And he said unto them, What things? And they said unto him, Of Jesus of Nazareth, which was a Prophet, mighty in deed and in word before God, and all people,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And how the high Priests, and our rulers delivered him to be condemned to death, and have crucified him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> But we trusted that it had been he that should have delivered Israel, and as touching all these things, to day is the third day, that they were done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Yea, and certain women among us made us astonied, which came early unto the sepulchre.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And when they found not his body, they came, saying, that they had also seen a vision of Angels, which said, that he was alive.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Therefore certain of them which were with us, went to the sepulchre, and found it even so as the women had said, but him they saw not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then he said unto them, O fools and slow of heart to believe all that the Prophets have spoken!</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Ought not Christ to have suffered these things, and to enter into his glory?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And he began at Moses, and at all the Prophets, and interpreted unto them in all the Scriptures the things which were written of him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And they drew near unto the town, which they went to, but he made as though he would have gone further.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> But they constrained him, saying, Abide with us: for it is towards night, and the day is far spent. So he went in to tarry with them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And it came to pass, as he sat at table with them, he took the bread, and blessed, and brake it, and gave it to them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Then their eyes were opened, and they knew him: and he was no more seen of them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And they said between themselves, Did not our hearts burn within us, while he talked with us by the way, and when he opened to us the Scriptures?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And they rose up the same hour, and returned to Jerusalem, and found the Eleven gathered together, and them that were with them,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Which said, The Lord is risen in deed, and hath appeared to Simon.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Then they told what things were done in the way, and how he was known of them in breaking of bread.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And as they spake these things, Jesus himself stood in the midst of them, and said unto them, Peace be to you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> But they were abashed and afraid, supposing that they had seen a spirit.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Then he said unto them, Why are ye troubled? and wherefore do doubts arise in your hearts?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Behold mine hands and my feet: for it is I my self: handle me, and see: for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye see me have.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And when he had thus spoken, he showed them his hands and feet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And while they yet believed not for joy, and wondered, he said unto them, Have ye here any meat?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And they gave him a piece of a broiled fish, and of an honey combe,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> And he took it, and did eat before them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> And he said unto them, These are the words, which I spake unto you while I was yet with you, that all must be fulfilled which are written of me in the Law of Moses, and in the Prophets, and in the Psalms.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Then opened he their understanding, that they might understand the Scriptures,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> And said unto them, Thus is it written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer, and to rise again from the dead the third day,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And that repentance, and remission of sins should be preached in his Name among all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> Now ye are witnesses of these things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> And behold, I do send the promise of my Father upon you: but tarry ye in the city of Jerusalem, until ye be endued with power from an high.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> Afterward he lead them out into Bethany, and lift up his hands, and blessed them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> And it came to pass, that as he blessed them, he departed from them, and was carried up into heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> And they worshipped him, and returned to Jerusalem with great joy,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> And were continually in the Temple, praising, and lauding God, Amen.</p>
