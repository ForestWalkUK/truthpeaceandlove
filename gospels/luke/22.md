# Luke 22

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Now the feast of unleavened bread drew near, which is called the Passover.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And the high Priests and Scribes sought how they might kill him: for they feared the people.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then entered Satan into Judas, who was called Iscariot, and was of the number of the twelve.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And he went his way, and communed with the high Priests and captains, how he might betray him to them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> So they were glad, and agreed to give him money.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And he consented, and sought opportunity to betray him unto them, when the people were away.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then came the day of unleavened bread, when the Passover must be sacrificed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And he sent Peter and John, saying, Go and prepare us the Passover, that we may eat it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And they said to him, Where wilt thou, that we prepare it?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Then he said unto them, Behold, when ye be entered into the city, there shall a man meet you, bearing a pitcher of water: follow him into the house that he entereth in,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And say unto the good man of the house, The Master saith unto thee, Where is the lodging where I shall eat my Passover with my disciples?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then he shall show you a great high chamber trimmed: there make it ready.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> So they went, and found as he had said unto them, and made ready the Passover.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And when the hour was come, he sat down, and the twelve Apostles with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Then he said unto them, I have earnestly desired to eat this Passover with you, before I suffer.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> For I say unto you, Henceforth I will not eat of it any more, until it be fulfilled in the kingdom of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And he took the cup, and gave thanks, and said, Take this, and divide it among you,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> For I say unto you, I will not drink of the fruit of the vine, until the kingdom of God be come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And he took bread, and when he had given thanks, he brake it, and gave to them, saying, This is my body, which is given for you: do this in the remembrance of me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Likewise also after supper he took the cup, saying, This cup is that new Testament in my blood, which is shed for you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Yet behold, the hand of him that betrayeth me, is with me at the table.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And truly the Son of man goeth as it is appointed: but woe be to that man, by whom he is betrayed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Then they began to enquire among themselves which of them it should be, that should do that.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And there arose also a strife among them, which of them should seem to be the greatest.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> But he said unto them, The Kings of the Gentiles reign over them, and they that bear rule over them, are called bountiful.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> But ye shall not be so: but let the greatest among you be as the least: and the chiefest as he that serveth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> For who is greater, he that sitteth at table, or he that serveth? Is not he that sitteth at table? And I am among you as he that serveth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And ye are they which have continued with me in my temptations.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Therefore I appoint unto you a kingdom, as my Father hath appointed unto me,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> That ye may eat, and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on seats, and judge the twelve tribes of Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And the Lord said, Simon, Simon, behold, Satan hath desired you, to winnow you as wheat.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> But I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: therefore when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And he said unto him, Lord, I am ready to go with thee into prison, and to death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> But he said, I tell thee, Peter, the cock shall not crow this day, before thou hast thrice denied that thou knewest me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And he said unto them, When I sent you without bag, and scrip, and shoes, lacked ye any thing? And they said, Nothing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Then he said to them, But now he that hath a bag, let him take it, and likewise a scrip: and he that hath none, let him sell his coat, and buy a sword.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> For I say unto you, That yet the same which is written, must be performed in me, Even with the wicked was he numbered: for doubtless those things which are written of me, have an end.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And they said, Lord, behold, here are two swords. And he said unto them, It is enough.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And he came out, and went (as he was wont) to the mount of Olives: and his disciples also followed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And when he came to the place, he said to them, Pray, lest ye enter into temptation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And he was drawn aside from them about a stones cast, and kneeled down, and prayed,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> Saying, Father, if thou wilt, take away this cup from me: nevertheless, not my will, but thine be done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> And there appeared an Angel unto him from heaven, comforting him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> But being in an agony, he prayed more earnestly: and his sweat was like drops of blood, trickling down to the ground.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> And he rose up from prayer, and came to his disciples, and found them sleeping for heaviness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> And he said unto them, Why sleep ye? rise and pray, least ye enter into temptation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And while he yet spake, behold, a company, and he that was called Judas one of the twelve, went before them, and came near unto Jesus to kiss him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> And Jesus said unto him, Judas, betrayest thou the Son of man with a kiss?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> Now when they which were about him, saw what would follow, they said unto him, Lord, shall we smite with sword?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> And one of them smote a servant of the high Priest, and struck off his right ear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> Then Jesus answered, and said, Suffer them thus far: and he touched his ear, and healed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> Then Jesus said unto the high Priests, and captains of the Temple, and the Elders which were come to him, Be ye come out as unto a thief with swords and staves?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> When I was daily with you in the Temple, ye stretched not forth the hands against me: but this is your very hour, and the power of darkness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> Then took they him, and led him, and brought him to the high Priests house. And Peter followed afar off.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> And when they had kindled a fire in the midst of the hall, and were set down together, Peter also sat down among them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> And a certain maid beheld him as he sat by the fire, and having well looked on him, said, This man was also with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">57</span> But he denied him, saying, Woman, I know him not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">58</span> And after a little while, another man saw him, and said, Thou art also of them. But Peter said, Man, I am not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">59</span> And about the space of an hour after, a certain other affirmed, saying. Verily even this man was with him: for he is also a Galilean.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">60</span> And Peter said, Man, I know not what thou sayest. And immediately while he yet spake, the cock crew.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">61</span> Then the Lord turned back, and looked upon Peter: and Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he had said unto him, Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">62</span> And Peter went out, and wept bitterly.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">63</span> And the men that held Jesus, mocked him, and struck him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">64</span> And when they had blindfolded him, they smote him on the face, and asked him, saying, Prophecy who it is that smote thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">65</span> And many other things blasphemously spake they against him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">66</span> And as soon as it was day, the Elders of the people, and the high Priests and the Scribes came together, and led him into their council,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">67</span> Saying, Art thou that Christ? tell us. And he said unto them, If I tell you, ye will not believe it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">68</span> And if also I ask you, you will not answer me, nor let me go.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">69</span> Hereafter shall the Son of man sit at the right hand of the power of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">70</span> Then said they all, Art thou then the Son of God? And he said to them, Ye say, that I am.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">71</span> Then said they, What need we any further witness? for we our selves have heard it of his own mouth.</p>
