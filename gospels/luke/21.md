# Luke 21

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">21</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And as he beheld, he saw the rich men, which cast their gifts into the treasury.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And he saw also a certain poor widow which cast in thither two mites:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And he said, Of a truth I say unto you, that this poor widow hath cast in more than they all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> For they all have of their superfluity cast into the offerings of God: but she of her penury hath cast in all the living that she had.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Now as some spake of the Temple, how it was garnished with goodly stones, and with consecrate things, he said,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Are these the things that ye look upon? the days will come wherein a stone shall not be left upon a stone, that shall not be thrown down.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then they asked him, saying, Master, but when shall these things be? and what sign shall there be when these things shall come to pass?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And he said, Take heed, that ye be not deceived: for many will come in my Name, saying, I am Christ, and the time draweth near: follow ye not them therefore.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And when ye hear of wars and seditions, be not afraid: for these things must first come, but the end followeth not by and by.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Then said he unto them, Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And great earthquakes shall be in divers places, and hunger, and pestilence, and fearful things, and great signs shall there be from heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> But before all these, they shall lay their hands on you, and persecute you, delivering you up to the assemblies, and into prisons, and bring you before Kings and rulers for my Names sake.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And this shall turn to you, for a testimonial.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Lay it up therefore in your hearts, that ye cast not before hand, what ye shall answer.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> For I will give you a mouth and wisdom, where against all your adversaries shall not be able to speak, nor resist.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Yea, ye shall be betrayed also of your parents, and of your brethren, and kinsmen, and friends, and some of you shall they put to death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And ye shall be hated of all men for my Names sake.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Yet there shall not one hear of your heads perish.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> By your patience possess your souls.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And when ye see Jerusalem besieged with soldiers, then understand that the desolation thereof is near.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then let them which are in Judea, flee to the mountains: and let them which are in the midst thereof, depart out: and let not them that are in the country, enter therein.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> For these be the days of vengeance, to fulfill all things that are written.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> But woe be to them that be with child, and to them that give suck in those days: for there shall be great distress in this land, and wrath over this people.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And they shall fall on the edge of the sword, and shall be led captive into all nations, and Jerusalem shall be trodden under foot of the Gentiles, until the time of the Gentiles be fulfilled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then there shall be signs in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars, and upon the earth trouble among the nations with perplexity: the sea and the waters shall roar.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And mens hearts shall fail them for fear, and for looking after those things which shall come on the world: for the powers of heaven shall be shaken.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And then shall they see the Son of man come in a cloud, with power and great glory.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And when these things begin to come to pass, then look up, and lift up your heads: for your redemption draweth near.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And he spake to them a parable, Behold, the fig tree, and all trees,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> When they now shoot forth, ye seeing them, know of your own selves, that summer is then near.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> So likewise ye, when ye see these things come to pass, know ye that the kingdom of God is near.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Verily I say unto you, This age shall not pass, till all these things be done:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Take heed to your selves, lest at any time your hearts be oppressed with surfeiting and drunkenness, and cares of this life, and least that day come on you at unwares.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> For as a snare shall it come on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Watch therefore, and pray continually, that ye may be counted worthy to escape all these things that shall come to pass, and that ye may stand before the Son of man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> Now in the day time he taught in the Temple, and at night he went out, and abode in the mount that is called the mount of Olives.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And all the people came in the morning to him, to hear him in the Temple.</p>
