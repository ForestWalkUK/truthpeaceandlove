<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">12</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> In the mean time, there gathered together an innumerable multitude of people, so that they trod one another: and he began to say unto his disciples first, Take heed to your selves of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> For there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed: neither hid, that shall not be known.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Wherefore whatsoever ye have spoken in darkness, it shall be heard in the light: and that which ye have spoken in the ear, in secret places, shall be preached on the houses.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And I say unto you, my friends, be not afraid of them that kill the body, and after that are not able to do any more.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> But I will forewarn you, who ye shall fear: fear him which after he hath killed, hath power to cast into hell: yea, I say unto you, him fear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Are not five sparrows bought for two farthings, and yet not one of them is forgotten before God?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Yea, and all the hears of your head are numbered: fear not therefore: ye are more of value then many sparrows.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Also I say unto you, Whosoever shall confess me before men, him shall the Son of man confess also before the Angels of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> But he that shall deny me before men, shall be denied before the Angels of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And whosoever shall speak a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him: but unto him, that shall blaspheme the holy Ghost, it shall not be forgiven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And when they shall bring you unto the Synagogues, and unto the rulers and Princes, take no thought how, or what thing ye shall answer, or what ye shall speak.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> For the holy Ghost shall teach you in the same hour, what ye ought to say.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And one of the company said unto him, Master, bid my brother divide the inheritance with me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And he said unto him, Man, who made me a judge, or a divider over you?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Wherefore he said unto them, Take heed, and beware of covetousness: for though a man have abundance, yet his life standeth not in his riches.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And he put forth a parable unto them, saying, The ground of a certain rich man brought forth fruits plenteously.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Therefore he thought with himself, saying, What shall I do, because I have no room, where I may lay up my fruits?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And he said, This will I do, I will pul down my barns, and build greater, and therein will I gather all my fruits, and my goods.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And I will say to my soul, Soul, thou hast much goods laid up for many years: live at ease, eat, drink and take thy pastime.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> But God said unto him, O fool, this night will they fetch away thy soul from thee: then whose shall those things be which thou hast provided?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> So is he that gathereth riches to himself, and is not rich in God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And he spake unto his disciples, Therefore I say unto you, Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat: neither for your body, what ye shall put on.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> The life is more than meat: and the body more than the raiment.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Consider the ravens: for they neither sow nor reap: which neither have storehouse nor barn, and yet God feedeth them: how much more are ye better than fowls?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And which of you with taking thought, can add to his stature one cubit?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> If ye then be not able to do the least thing, why take ye thought for the remnant?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Consider the lilies how they grow: they labour not, neither spin they: yet I say unto you, that Solomon himself in all his royalty was not clothed like one of these.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> If then God so clothe the grass which is to day in the field, and to morrow is cast into the oven, how much more will he clothe you, O ye of little faith?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Therefore ask not what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink, neither hag you in suspense.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> For all such things the people of the world seek for: and your Father knoweth that ye have need of these things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> But rather seek ye after the kingdom of God, and all these things shall be cast upon you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Fear not, little flock: for it is your Fathers pleasure, to give you the kingdom.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Sell that ye have, and give alms: make you bags, which wax not old, a treasure that can never fail in heaven, where no thief cometh, neither moth corrupteth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> For where your treasure is, there will your hearts be also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Let your loins be gird about and your lights burning,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And ye your selves like unto men that wait for their master, when he will return from the wedding, that when he cometh and knocketh, they may open unto him immediately.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> Blessed are those servants, whom the Lord when he cometh shall find waking: verily I say unto you, he will gird himself about, and make them to sit down at table, and will come forth, and serve them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And if he come in the second watch, or come in the third watch, and shall find them so, blessed are those servants.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Now understand this, that if the good man of the house had known at what hour the thief would have come, he would have watched, and would not have suffered his house to be digged through.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> Be ye also prepared therefore: for the Son of man will come at an hour when ye think not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Then Peter said unto him, Master, tellest thou this parable unto us, or even to all?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And the Lord said, Who is a faithful steward and wise, whom the master shall make ruler over his household, to give them their portion of meat in season?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Blessed is that servant, whom his master when he cometh, shall find so doing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> Of a truth I say unto you, that he will make him ruler over all that he hath.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> But if that servant say in his heart, My master doeth defer his coming, and gin to smite the servants, and maidens, and to eat, and drink, and to be drunken,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> The master of that servant will come in a day when he thinketh not, and at an hour when he is not ware of, and will cut him off, and give him his portion with the unbelievers.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And that servant that knew his masters will, and prepared not himself, neither did according to his will, shall be beaten with many stripes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> But he that knew it not, and yet did commit things worthy of stripes, shall be beaten with few stripes: for unto whomsoever much is given, of him shall be much required, and to whom men much commit, the more of him will they ask.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> I am come to put fire on the earth, and what is my desire, if it be already kindled?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> Notwithstanding I must be baptized with a baptism, and how am I grieved, till it be ended?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> Think ye that I am come to give peace on earth? I tell you, nay, but rather debate.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> For from henceforth there shall be five in one house divided, three against two, and two against three.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> The father shall be divided against the son, and the son against the father: the mother against the daughter, and the daughter against the mother: the mother in law against her daughter in law, and the daughter in law against her mother in law.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> Then said he to the people, When ye see a cloud rise out of the West, straightway ye say, A shower cometh: and so it is.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> And when ye see the South wind blow, ye say, that it will be hot: and it cometh to pass.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> Hypocrites, ye can discern the face of the earth, and of the sky: but why discern ye not this time?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">57</span> Yea, and why judge ye not of your selves what is right?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">58</span> While thou goest with thine adversary to the ruler, as thou art in the way, give diligence in the way, that thou mayest be delivered from him, least he draw thee to the judge, and the judge deliver thee to the jailer, and the jailer cast thee into prison.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">59</span> I tell thee, thou shalt not depart thence, till thou hast paid the utmost mite.</p>
</div>
