# Luke 17

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Then said he to his disciples, It can not be avoided, but that offences will come, but woe be to him by whom they come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> It is better for him that a great millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were cast into the sea, then that he should offend one of these little ones.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Take heed to your selves: if thy brother trespass against thee, rebuke him: and if he repent, forgive him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And though he sin against thee seven times in a day, and seven times in a day turn again to thee, saying, It repenteth me, thou shalt forgive him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And the Apostles said unto the Lord, Increase our faith.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And the Lord said, If ye had faith, as much as is a grain of mustard seed, and should say unto this mulberry tree, Pluck thy self up by the roots, and plant thy self in the sea, it should even obey you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Who is it also of you, that having a servant plowing or feeding cattle, would say unto him by and by, when he were come from the field, Go, and sit down at table?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And would not rather say to him, Dress wherewith I may sup, and gird thy self, and serve me, till I have eaten and drunken, and afterward eat thou, and drink thou?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Doeth he thank that servant, because he did that which was commanded unto him? I trow not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> So likewise ye, when ye have done all those things, which are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And so it was when he went to Jerusalem, that he passed through the midst of Samaria, and Galilee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And as he entered into a certain town, there met him ten men that were lepers, which stood a far off.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And they lift up their voices and said, Jesus, Master, have mercy on us.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And when he saw them, he said unto them, Go, show your selves unto the Priests. And it came to pass, that as they went, they were cleansed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Then one of them, when he saw that he, was healed, turned back, and with a loud voice praised God,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And fell down on his face at his feet, and gave him thanks: and he was a Samaritan.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And Jesus answered, and said, Are there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> There is none found that returned to give God praise, save this stranger.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And he said unto him, Arise, go thy way, thy faith hath saved thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And when he was demanded of the Pharisees, when the kingdom of God should come, he answered them, and said, The kingdom of God cometh not with observation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Neither shall men say, Lo here, or lo there: for behold, the kingdom of God is within you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And he said unto the disciples, The days will come, when ye shall desire to see one of the days of the Son of man, and ye shall not see it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Then they shall say to you, Behold here, or behold there: but go not thither, neither follow them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> For as the lightening that lighteneth out of the one part under heaven, shineth unto the other part under heaven, so shall the Son of man be in his day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> But first must he suffer many things, and be reproved of this generation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And as it was in the days of Noe, so shall it be in the days of the Son of man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> They ate, they drank, they married wives, and gave in marriage unto the day that Noe went into the Ark: and the flood came, and destroyed them all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Likewise also, as it was in the days of Lot: they ate, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they built.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> But in the day that Lot went out of Sodom, it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> After these ensamples shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> At that day he that is upon the house, and his stuff in the house, let him not come down to take it out: and he that is in the field likewise, let him not turn back to that he left behind.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Remember Lots wife.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Whosoever will seek to save his soul, shall loose it: and whosoever shall loose it, shall get it life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> I tell you, in that night there shall be two in one bed: the one shall be received, and the other shall be left.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Two women shall be grinding together: the one shall be taken, and the other shall be left.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Two shall be in the field: one shall be received, and another shall be left.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And they answered, and said to him, Where, Lord? And he said unto them, Wheresoever the body is, thither shall also the eagles be gathered together.</p>
