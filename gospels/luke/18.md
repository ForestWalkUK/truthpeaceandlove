# Luke 18

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">18</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And he spake also a parable unto them, to this end, that they ought always to pray, and not to wax faint,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Saying, There was a judge in a certain city, which feared not God, neither reverenced man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And there was a widow in that city, which came unto him, saying, Do me justice against mine adversary.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And he would not of a long time: but afterward he said with himself, Though I fear not God, nor reverence man,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Yet because this widow troubleth me, I will do her right, lest at the last she come and make me weary.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And the Lord said, Hear what the unrighteous judge saith.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Now shall not God avenge his elect, which cry day and night unto him, yea, though he suffer long for them?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> I tell you he will avenge them quickly: but when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on the earth?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> He spake also this parable unto certain which trusted in themselves that they were just, and despised other.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Two men went up into the Temple to pray: the one a Pharisee, and the other a Publican.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> The Pharisee stood and prayed thus with himself, O God, I thank thee that I am not as other men, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, or even as this Publican.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> I fast twice in the week: I give tithe of all that ever I possess.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> But the Publican standing a far off, would not lift up so much as his eyes to heaven, but smote his brest, saying, O God, be merciful to me a sinner.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> I tell you, this man departed to his house justified, rather than the other: for every man that exalteth himself, shall be brought low, and he that humbleth himself, shall be exalted.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> They brought unto him also babes that he should touch them. And when his disciples saw it, they rebuked them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> But Jesus called them unto him, and said, Suffer the babes to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Verily I say unto you, whosoever receiveth not the kingdom of God as a babe, he shall not enter therein.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Then a certain ruler asked him, saying, Good Master, what ought I to do, to inherit eternal life?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And Jesus said unto him, Why callest thou me good? none is good, save one, even God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Thou knowest the commandments, Thou shalt not commit adultery: Thou shalt not kill: Thou shalt not steal: Thou shalt not bear false witness: Honour thy father and thy mother.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And he said, All these have I kept from my youth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Now when Jesus heard that, he said unto him, Yet lackest thou one thing. Sell all that ever thou hast, and distribute unto the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven, and come follow me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> But when he heard those things, he was very heavy: for he was marvellous rich.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And when Jesus saw him very sorrowful, he said, With what difficulty shall they that have riches, enter into the kingdom of God!</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Surely it is easier for a camel to go through a needles eye, then for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Then said they that heard it, And who then can be saved?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And he said, The things which are impossible with men, are possible with God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then Peter said, Lo, we have left all, and have followed thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And he said unto them, Verily I say unto you, there is no man that hath left house, or parents, or brethren, or wife, or children for the kingdom of Gods sake,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Which shall not receive much more in this world, and in the world to come life everlasting.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Then Jesus took unto him the twelve, and said unto them, Behold, we go up to Jerusalem, and all things shall be fulfilled to the Son of man, that are written by the Prophets.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> For he shall be delivered unto the Gentiles, and shall be mocked, and shall be spitefully entreated, and shall be spitted on.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And when they have scourged him, they will put him to death: but the third day he shall rise again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> But they understood none of these things, and this saying was hid from them, neither perceived they the things, which were spoken.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And it came to pass, that as he was come near unto Jericho, a certain blind man sat by the way side, begging.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And when he heard the people pass by, he asked what it meant.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And they said unto him, that Jesus of Nazareth passed by.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Then he cried, saying, Jesus the Son of David, have mercy on me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And they which went before, rebuked him that he should hold his peace, but he cried much more, O Son of David, have mercy on me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And Jesus stood still, and commanded him to be brought unto him. And when he was come near, he asked him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Saying, What wilt thou that I do unto thee? And he said, Lord, that I may receive my sight.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And Jesus said unto him, Receive thy sight: thy faith hath saved thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Then immediately he received his sight, and followed him, praising God: and all the people, when they saw this, gave praise to God.</p>
</div>
