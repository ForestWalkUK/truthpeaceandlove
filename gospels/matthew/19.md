# Matthew 19

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">19</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And it came to pass, that when Jesus had finished these sayings, he departed from Galilee, and came into the coasts of Judea beyond Jordan.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And great multitudes followed him, and he healed them there.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then came unto him the Pharisees tempting him, and saying to him, Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife upon every occasion?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And he answered and said unto them, Have ye not read, that he which made them at the beginning, made them male and female,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And said, For this cause, shall a man leave father and mother, and cleave unto his wife, and they which were two shall be one flesh.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Wherefore they are no more twain, but one flesh. Let not man therefore put asunder that, which God hath coupled together.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> They said to him, Why did then Moses command to give a bill of divorcement, and to put her away?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> He said unto them, Moses, because of the hardness of your heart, suffered you to put away your wives: but from the beginning it was not so.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> I say therefore unto you, that whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for whoredom, and marry another, committeth adultery: and whosoever marieth her which is divorced, doeth commit adultery.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Then said his disciples to him, If the matter be so between man and wife, it is not good to marry.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> But he said unto them, All men cannot receive this thing, save they to whom it is given.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> For there are some eunuchs, which were so born of their mothers belly: and there be some eunuchs, which be gelded by men: and there be some eunuchs, which have gelded them selves for the kingdom of heaven. He that is able to receive this, let him receive it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then were brought to him little children, that he should put his hands on them, and pray: and the disciples rebuked them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> But Jesus said, Suffer the little children, and forbid them not to come to me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And when he had put his hands on them, he departed thence.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And behold, one came and said unto him, Good Master, what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And he said unto him, Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, even God: but if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> He said to him, Which? And Jesus said, These, Thou shalt not kill: Thou shalt not commit adultery: Thou shalt not steal: Thou shalt not bear false witness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Honour thy father and mother: and thou shalt love thy neighbour as thy self.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> The young man said unto him, I have observed all these things from my youth: what lack I yet?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Jesus said unto him, If thou wilt be perfect, go, sell that thou hast, and give it to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven, and come, and follow me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And when the young man heard that saying, he went away sorrowful: for he had great possessions.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Then Jesus said unto his disciples, Verily I say unto you, that a rich man shall hardly enter into the kingdom of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And again I say unto you, It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle, then for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And when his disciples heard it, they were exceedingly amazed, saying, Who then can be saved?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And Jesus beheld them, and said unto them, With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Then answered Peter, and said to him, Behold, we have forsaken all, and followed thee: what therefore shall we have?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And Jesus said unto them, Verily I say to you, that when the Son of man shall sit in the throne of his majesty, ye which followed me in the regeneration, shall sit also upon twelve thrones and judge the twelve tribes of Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And whosoever shall forsake houses, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands, for my Names sake, he shall receive an hundred fold more, and shall inherit everlasting life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> But many that are first, shall be last, and the last shall be first.</p>
