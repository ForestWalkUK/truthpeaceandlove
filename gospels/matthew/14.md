# Matthew 14

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> At that time Herod the Tetrarch heard of the fame of Jesus,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And said unto his servants, This is that John Baptist, he is risen again from the dead, and therefore great works are wrought by him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> For Herod had taken John, and bound him, and put him in prison for Herodias sake, his brother Philips wife.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> For John said unto him, It is not lawful for thee to have her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And when he would have put him to death, he feared the multitude, because they counted him as a Prophet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> But when Herods birth day was kept, the daughter of Herodias danced before them, and pleased Herod.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Wherefore he promised with an oath, that he would give her whatsoever she would ask.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And she being before instructed of her mother, said, Give me here John Baptists head in a platter.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And the King was sorry: nevertheless because of the oath, and them that sat with him at the table, he commanded it to be given her,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And sent, and beheaded John in the prison.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And his head was brought in a platter, and given to the maid, and she brought it unto her mother.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And his disciples came, and took up the body, and buried it, and went, and told Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And when Jesus heard it, he departed thence by ship into a desert place apart. And when the multitude had heard it, they followed him on foot out of the cities.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And Jesus went forth and saw a great multitude, and was moved with compassion toward them, and he healed their sick.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And when even was come, his disciples came to him, saying, This is a desert place, and the time is already past: let the multitude depart, that they may go into the towns, and buy them victuals.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> But Jesus said to them, They have no need to go away: give ye them to eat.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then said they unto him, We have here but five loaves, and two fishes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And he said, Bring them hither to me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And he commanded the multitude to sit down on the grass, and took the five loaves and the two fishes, and looked up to heaven and blessed, and brake, and gave the loaves to his disciples, and the disciples to the multitude.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And they did all eat, and were sufficed, and they took up of the fragments that remained, twelve baskets full.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And they that had eaten, were about five thousand men, beside women and little children.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And straightway Jesus compelled his disciples to enter into a ship, and to go over before him, while he sent the multitude away.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And as soon as he had sent the multitude away, he went up into a mountain alone to pray: and when the evening was come, he was there alone.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And the ship was now in the midst of the sea, and was tossed with waves: for it was a contrary wind.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And in the fourth watch of the night, Jesus went unto them, walking on the sea.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And when his disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, It is a spirit, and cried out for fear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> But straight way Jesus spake unto them, saying, Be of good comfort, It is I: be not afraid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then Peter answered him, and said, Master, if it be thou, bid me come unto thee on the water.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And he said, Come. And when Peter was come down out of the ship, he walked on the water, to go to Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> But when he saw a mighty wind, he was afraid: and as he began to sink, he cried, saying, Master, save me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> So immediately Jesus stretched forth his hand, and caught him, and said to him, O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And as soon as they were come into the ship, the wind ceased.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Then they that were in the ship, came and worshipped him, saying, Of a truth thou art the Son of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And when they were come over, they came into the land of Gennesaret.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And when the men of that place knew him, they sent out into all that country round about, and brought unto him all that were sick,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And besought him, that they might touch the hem of his garment only: and as many as touched it, were made whole.</p>
</div>
