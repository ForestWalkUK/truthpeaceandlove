# Matthew 11

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And it came to pass that when Jesus had made an end of commanding his twelve disciples, he departed thence to teach and to preach in their cities.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And when John heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent two of his disciples, and said unto him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Art thou he that should come, or shall we look for another?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And Jesus answering, said unto them, Go, and show John, what things ye hear, and see.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> The blind receive sight, and the halt do walk: the lepers are cleansed, and the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor receive the Gospel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And blessed is he that shall not be offended in me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And as they departed, Jesus began to speak unto the multitude, of John, What went ye out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken with the wind?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> But what went ye out to see? A man clothed in soft raiment? Behold, they that wear soft clothing, are in Kings houses.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> But what went ye out to see? A Prophet? Yea, I say unto you, and more than a Prophet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> For this is he of whom it is written, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Verily I say unto you, among them which are begotten of women, arose there not a greater than John Baptist: notwithstanding, he that is the least in the kingdom of heaven, is greater than he.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And from the time of John Baptist hitherto, the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> For all the Prophets and the Law prophesied unto John.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And if ye will receive it, this is that Elias, which was to come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> But whereunto shall I liken this generation? It is like unto little children which sit in the markets, and call unto their fellows,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And say, We have piped unto you, and ye have not danced, we have mourned unto you, and ye have not lamented.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> For John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, He hath a devil.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> The son of man came eating and drinking, and they say, Behold a glutton and a drinker of wine, a friend unto Publicans and sinners: but wisdom is justified of her children.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Then began he to upbraid the cities, wherein most of his great works were done, because they repented not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Woe be to thee, Chorazin: Woe be to thee, Bethsaida: for if the great works, which were done in you, had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they had repented long agone in sackcloth and ashes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> But I say to you, It shall be easier for Tyre and Sidon at the day of judgement, then for you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And thou, Capernaum, which art lifted up unto heaven, shalt be brought down to hell: for if the great works, which have been done in thee, had been done among them of Sodom, they had remained to this day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> But I say unto you, that it shall be easier for them of the land of Sodom in the day of judgement, then for thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> At that time Jesus answered, and said, I give thee thanks, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because thou hast hid these things from the wise and men of understanding, and hast opened them unto babes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> It is so, O Father, because thy good pleasure was such.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> All things are given unto me of my Father: and no man knoweth the Son, but the Father: neither knoweth any man the Father, but the Son, and he to whom the Son will reveal him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Come unto me, all ye that are weary and laden, and I will ease you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Take my yoke on you, and learn of me that I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> For my yoke is easy, and my burden light.</p>
</div>
