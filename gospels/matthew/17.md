# Matthew 17

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And after six days, Jesus took Peter, and James and John his brother, and brought them up into an high mountain apart,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And was transfigured before them: and his face did shine as the Sun, and his clothes were as white as the light.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And behold, there appeared unto them Moses, and Elias, talking with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Then answered Peter, and said to Jesus, Master, it is good for us to be here: if thou wilt, let us make here three tabernacles, one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elias.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> While he yet spake, behold, a bright cloud shadowed them: and behold, there came a voice out of the cloud, saying, This is that my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased: hear him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And when the disciples heard that, they fell on their faces, and were sore afraid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then Jesus came and touched them, and said, Arise, and be not afraid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And when they lifted up their eyes, they saw no man, save Jesus only.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And as they came down from the mountain, Jesus charged them, saying, Show the vision to no man, until the Son of man rise again from the dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And his disciples asked him, saying, Why then say the Scribes that Elias must first come?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And Jesus answered, and said unto them, Certainly Elias must first come, and restore all things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> But I say unto you that Elias is come already, and they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they would: likewise shall also the Son of man suffer of them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then the disciples perceived that he spake unto them of John Baptist.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And when they were come to the multitude, there came to him a certain man, and fell down at his feet,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And said, Master, have pity on my son: for he is lunatic, and is sore vexed: for oft times he falleth into the fire, and oft times into the water.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And I brought him to thy disciples, and they could not heal him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then Jesus answered, and said, O generation faithless, and crooked, how long now shall I be with you! how long now shall I suffer you! bring him hither to me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And Jesus rebuked the devil, and he went out of him: and the child was healed at that hour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Then came the disciples to Jesus apart, and said, Why could not we cast him out?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And Jesus said unto them, Because of your unbelief: for verily I say unto you, if ye have faith as much as is a grain of mustard seed, ye shall say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place, and it shall remove: and nothing shall be impossible unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Howbeit this kind goeth not out, but by prayer and fasting.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And they being in Galilee, Jesus said unto them, The Son of man shall be delivered into the hands of men,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And they shall kill him, but the third day shall he rise again: and they were very sorry.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And when they were come to Capernaum, they that received poll money, came to Peter, and said, Doeth not your Master pay poll money?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> He said, Yes. And when he was come into the house, Jesus prevented him, saying, What thinkest thou, Simon? Of whom do the Kings of the earth take tribute, or poll money? of their children, or of strangers?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Peter said unto him, Of strangers. Then said Jesus unto him, Then are the children free.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Nevertheless, lest we should offend them: go to the sea, and cast in an angle, and take the first fish that cometh up, and when thou hast opened his mouth, thou shalt find a piece of twenty pence: that take, and give it unto them for me and thee.</p>
</div>
