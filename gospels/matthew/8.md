# Matthew 8

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">8</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Now when he was come down from the mountain, great multitudes followed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And lo, there came a Leper and worshipped him, saying, Master, if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And Jesus putting forth his hand, touched him, saying, I will, be thou clean: and immediately his leprosy was cleansed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Then Jesus said unto him, See thou tell no man, but go, and show thy self unto the Priest, and offer the gift that Moses commanded, for a witness to them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> When Jesus was entered into Capernaum, there came unto him a Centurion, beseeching him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And said, Master, my servant lieth sick at home of the palsy, and is grievously pained.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And Jesus said unto him, I will come and heal him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> But the Centurion answered, saying, Master, I am not worthy that thou shouldest come under my roof: but speak the word only, and my servant shall be healed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> For I am a man also under the authority of an other, and have soldiers under me: and I say to one, Go, and he goeth: and to another, Come, and he cometh: and to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> When Jesus heard that, he marvelled, and said to them that followed him, Verily, I say unto you, I have not found so great faith, even in Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> But I say unto you, that many shall come from the East and West, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, in the kingdom of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into utter darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then Jesus said unto the Centurion, Go thy way, and as thou hast believed, so be it unto thee, And his servant was healed the same hour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And when Jesus came to Peters house, he saw his wives mother laid down, and sick of a fever.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And he touched her hand, and the fever left her: so she arose, and ministered unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> When the Even was come, they brought unto him many that were possessed with devils: and he cast out the spirits with his word, and healed all that were sick,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> That it might be fulfilled, which was spoken by Esaias the Prophet, saying, He took our infirmities, and bare our sicknesses.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And when Jesus saw great multitudes of people about him, he commanded them to go over the water.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Then came there a certain Scribe, and said unto him, Master, I will follow thee whithersoever thou goest.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> But Jesus said unto him, The foxes have holes, and the birds of the heaven have nests, but the Son of man hath not whereon to rest his head.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And another of his disciples said unto him, Master, suffer me first to go, and bury my father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> But Jesus said unto him, Follow me, and let the dead bury their dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And when he was entered into the ship, his disciples followed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And behold, there arose a great tempest in the sea, so that the ship was covered with waves: but he was a sleep.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then his disciples came, and awoke him, saying, Master, save us: we perish.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And he said unto them, Why are ye fearful, O ye of little faith? Then he arose, and rebuked the winds and the sea: and so there was a great calm.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And the men marvelled, saying, What man is this, that both the winds and the sea obey him!</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And when he was come to the other side into the country of the Gergesenes, there met him two possessed with devils, which came out of the graves very fierce, so that no man might go by that way.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And behold, they cried out, saying, Jesus the son of God, what have we to do with thee? Art thou come hither to torment us before the time?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Now there was, afar off from them, a great heard of swine feeding.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And the devils besought him, saying, If thou cast us out, suffer us to go into the heard of swine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And he said unto them, Go. So they went out and departed into the heard of swine: and behold, the whole heard of swine ran headlong into the sea, and died in the water.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Then the herdmen fled: and when they were come into the city, they told all things, and what was become of them that were possessed with the devils.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And behold, all the city came out to meet Jesus: and when they saw him, they besought him to depart out of their coasts.</p>
