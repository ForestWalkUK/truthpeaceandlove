# Matthew 28

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Now in the end of the Sabbath, when the first day of the week began to dawn, Mary Magdalene, and the other Mary came to see the sepulchre,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And behold, there was a great earthquake: for the Angel of the Lord descended from heaven, and came and rolled back the stone from the door, and sat upon it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And his countenance was like lightning, and his raiment white as snow.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And for fear of him, the keepers were astonied, and became as dead men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> But the Angel answered, and said to the women, Fear ye not: for I know that ye seek Jesus which was crucified:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> He is not here, for he is risen; as he said: come, see the place where the Lord was laid,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And go quickly, and tell his disciples that he is risen from the dead: and behold, he goeth before you into Galilee: there ye shall see him: lo, I have told you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> So they departed quickly from the sepulchre, with fear and great joy, and did run to bring his disciples word.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And as they wet to tell his disciples, behold, Jesus also met the, saying, God save you. And they came, and took him by the feet, and worshipped him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Then said Jesus unto them, Be not afraid. Go, and tell my brethren, that they go into Galilee, and there shall they see me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Now when they were gone, behold, some of the watch came into the city, and showed unto the high Priests all the things that were done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And they gathered them together with the Elders, and took counsel, and gave large money unto the soldiers,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Saying, Say, His disciples came by night, and stole him away while we slept.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And if this matter come before the governor to be heard, we will persuade him, and so use the matter that you shall not need to care.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> So they took the money, and did as they were taught: and this saying is noised among the Jews unto this day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Then the eleven disciples wet into Galilee, into a mountain, where Jesus had appointed the.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And when they saw him, they worshipped him: but some doubted.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And Jesus came, and spake unto them, saying, All power is given unto me, in heaven, and in earth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Go therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the Name of the Father, and the Son, and the holy Ghost,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Teaching them to observe all things, whatsoever I have commanded you: and lo, I am with you always, until the end of the world, Amen.</p>
</div>
