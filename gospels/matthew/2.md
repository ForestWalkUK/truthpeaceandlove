# Matthew 2

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> When Jesus then was born at Bethlehem in Judea, in the days of Herod the King, behold, there came Wise men from the East to Jerusalem,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Saying, Where is that King of the Jews that is born? for we have seen his star in the East, and are come to worship him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> When King Herod heard this, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And gathering together all the chief Priests and Scribes of the people, he asked of them, where Christ should be born.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And they said unto him, At Bethlehem in Judea: for so it is written by the Prophet,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And thou Bethlehem in the land of Juda, art not the least among the Princes of Juda: For out of thee shall come the governor that shall feed that my people Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then Herod privily called the Wise men, and diligently inquired of them the time of the star that appeared,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And sent them to Bethlehem, saying, Go, and search diligently for the babe: and when ye have found him, bring me word again, that I may come also, and worship him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> So when they had heard the King, they departed: and lo, the star which they had seen in the East, went before them, till it came and stood over the place where the babe was.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And when they saw the star, they rejoiced with an exceeding great joy,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And went into the house, and found the babe with Mary his mother, and fell down, and worshipped him, and opened their treasures, and presented unto him gifts, even gold, and frankincense, and myrrh.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And after they were warned of God in a dream, that they should not go again to Herod, they returned into their country another way.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> After their departure, behold, the Angel of the Lord appeareth to Joseph in a dream, saying, Arise, and take the babe and his mother, and flee into Egypt, and be there till I bring thee word: for Herod will seek the babe, to destroy him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> So he arose and took the babe and his mother by night, and departed into Egypt,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And was there unto the death of Herod, that that might be fulfilled, which is spoken of the Lord by the Prophet, saying, Out of Egypt have I called my son.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Then Herod, seeing that he was mocked of the Wise men, was exceeding wroth, and sent forth, and slew all the male children that were in Bethlehem, and in all the coasts thereof, from two year old and under, according to the time which he had diligently searched out of the Wise men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then was that fulfilled which is spoken by the Prophet Jeremias, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> In Rhama was a voice heard, mourning, and weeping, and great howling: Rachel weeping for her children, and would not be comforted, because they were not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And when Herod was dead, behold, an Angel of the Lord appeareth in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Saying, Arise, and take the babe and his mother, and go into the land of Israel: for they are dead which sought the babes life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then he arose up and took the babe and his mother, and came into the land of Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> But when he heard that Archelaus did reign in Judea in stead of his father Herod, he was afraid to go thither: yet after he was warned of God in a dream, he turned aside into the parts of Galilee,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And went and dwelt in a city called Nazareth, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the Prophets, which was, That he should be called a Nazarite.</p>
</div>
