# Matthew 9

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Then he entered into a ship, and passed over, and came into his own city.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And lo, they brought to him a man sick of the palsy, laid on a bed. And Jesus seeing their faith, said to the sick of the palsy, Son, be of good comfort: thy sins are forgiven thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And behold, certain of the Scribes said with themselves, This man blasphemeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> But when Jesus saw their thoughts, he said, Wherefore think ye evil things in your hearts?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> For whether is it easier to say, Thy sins are forgiven thee, or to say, Arise, and walk?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And that ye may know that the Son of man hath authority in earth to forgive sins, (then said he unto the sick of the palsy,) Arise, take up thy bed, and go to thine house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And he arose, and departed to his own house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> So when the multitude saw it, they marvelled, and glorified God, which had given such authority to men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And as Jesus passed forth from thence, he saw a man sitting at the custom, named Matthew, and said to him, Follow me. And he arose, and followed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And it came to pass, as Jesus sat at meat in his house, behold, many Publicans and sinners, that came thither, sat down at the table with Jesus and his disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And when the Pharisees saw that, they said to his disciples, Why eateth your master with Publicans and sinners?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Now when Jesus heard it, he said unto them, The whole need not a Physician, but they that are sick.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> But go ye and learn what this is, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: for I am not come to call the righteous, but the sinners to repentance.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Then came the disciples of John to him, saying, Why do we and the Pharisees fast oft, and thy disciples fast not?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And Jesus said unto them, Can the children of the marriage chamber mourn as long as the bridegroom is with them? But the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then shall they fast.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Moreover no man pieceth an old garment with a piece of new cloth: for that that should fill it up, taketh away from the garment, and the breach is worse.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Neither do they put new wine into old vessels: for then the vessels would break, and the wine would be spilt, and the vessels should perish: but they put new wine into new vessels, and so are both preserved.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> While he thus spake unto them, behold, there came a certain ruler, and worshipped him, saying, My daughter is now deceased, but come and lay thine hand on her, and she shall live.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And Jesus arose and followed him with his disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> (And behold, a woman which was diseased with an issue of blood twelve years, came behind him, and touched the hem of his garment.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> For she said in her self, If I may touch but his garment only, I shall be whole.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Then Jesus turned him about, and seeing her, did say, Daughter, be of good comfort: thy faith hath made thee whole. And the woman was made whole at that same moment.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Now when Jesus came into the Rulers house, and saw the minstrels and the multitude making noise,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> He said unto them, Get you hence: for the maid is not dead, but sleepeth. And they laughed him to scorn.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And when the multitude were put forth, he went in and took her by the hand, and the maid arose.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And this bruit went throughout all that land.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And as Jesus departed thence, two blind men followed him, crying, and saying, O son of David, have mercy upon us.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And when he was come into the house, the blind came to him, and Jesus said unto them, Believe ye that I am able to do this? And they said unto him, Yea, Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Then touched he their eyes, saying, According to your faith be it unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And their eyes were opened, and Jesus gave them great charge, saying, See that no man know it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> But when they were departed, they spread abroad his fame throughout all that land.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And as they went out, behold, they brought to him a dumb man possessed with a devil.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And when the devil was cast out, the dumb spake: then the multitude marvelled, saying, The like was never seen in Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> But the Pharisees said, He casteth out devils, through the prince of devils.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And Jesus went about all cities and towns, teaching in their Synagogues, and preaching the Gospel of the kingdom, and healing every sickness and every disease among the people.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> But when he saw the multitude, he had compassion upon them, because they were dispersed, and scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> Then said he to his disciples, Surely the harvest is great, but the labourers are few.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Wherefore pray the Lord of the harvest, that he would send forth labourers into his harvest.</p>
</div>
