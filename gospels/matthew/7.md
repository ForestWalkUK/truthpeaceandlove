# Matthew 7

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Judge not, that ye be not judged.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> For with what judgement ye judge, ye shall be judged, and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And why seest thou the mote, that is in thy brothers eye, and perceivest not the beam that is in thine own eye?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Or how sayest thou to thy brother, Suffer me to cast out the mote out of thine eye, and behold, a beam is in thine own eye?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Hypocrite, first cast out that beam out of thine own eye, and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brothers eye.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Give ye not that which is holy, to dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine, lest they tread them under their feet, and turning again, all to rent you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Ask, and it shall be given you: seek, and ye shall find: knock, and it shall be opened unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> For whosoever asketh, receiveth: and he, that seeketh, findeth: and to him that knocketh, it shall be opened.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> For what man is there among you, which if his son ask him bread, would give him a stone?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Or if he ask fish, will he give him a serpent?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> If ye then, which are evil, can give to your children good gifts, how much more shall your Father which is in heaven, give good things to them that ask him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Therefore whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, even so do ye to them: for this is the Law and the Prophets.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Enter in at the straight gate: for it is the wide gate, and broad way that leadeth to destruction: and many there be which go in thereat,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Because the gate is straight, and the way narrow that leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Beware of false prophets, which come to you, in sheeps clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns? or figs of thistles?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> So every good tree bringeth forth good fruit, and a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> A good tree can not bring forth evil fruit: neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit, is hewn down, and cast into the fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Therefore by their fruits ye shall know them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Not every one that sayeth unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven, but he that doeth my Fathers will which is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not by thy Name prophesied? and by thy name cast out devils? and by thy name done many great works?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And then will I profess to them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Whosoever then heareth of me these words, and doeth the same, I will liken him to a wise man, which hath build his house on a rock:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And the rain fell, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house, and it fell not: for it was grounded on a rock.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> But whosoever heareth these my words, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which hath build his house upon the sand:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And the rain fell, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house, and it fell, and the fall thereof was great.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And it came to pass, when Jesus had ended these words, the people were astonied at his doctrine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> For he taught them as one having authority, and not as the Scribes.</p>
</div>
