<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">21</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And when they drew near to Jerusalem, and were come to Bethphage, unto the mount of the Olives, then sent Jesus two disciples,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Saying to them, Go into the town that is over against you, and anon ye shall find an ass bound, and a colt with her: loose them, and bring them unto me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And if any man say ought unto you, say ye, that the Lord hath need of them, and straightway he will let them go.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> All this was done that it might be fulfilled, which was spoken by the Prophet, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Tell ye the daughter of Sion, Behold, thy King cometh unto thee, meek and sitting upon an ass, and a colt, the foal of an ass used to the yoke.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> So the disciples went, and did as Jesus had commanded them,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And brought the ass and the colt, and put on them their clothes, and set him thereon.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And a great multitude spread their garments in the way: and other cut down branches from the trees, and strawed them in the way.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Moreover, the people that went before, and they also that followed, cried, saying, Hosanna to the Son of David, Blessed be he that cometh in the Name of the Lord, Hosanna thou which art in the highest heavens.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And when he was come into Jerusalem, all the city was moved, saying, Who is this?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And the people said, This is Jesus that Prophet of Nazareth in Galilee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And Jesus went into the Temple of God, and cast out all them that sold and bought in the Temple, and overthrew the tables of the money changers, and the seats of them that sold doves,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And said to them, it is written, My house shall be called the house of prayer: but ye have made it a den of thieves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Then the blind, and the halt came to him in the Temple, and he healed them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> But when the chief priests and Scribes saw the marvels that he did, and the children crying in the Temple, and saying, Hosanna to the Son of David, they disdained,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And said unto him, Hearest thou what these say? And Jesus said unto them, Yea: read ye never, By the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast made perfect the praise?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> So he left them, and went out of the city unto Bethany, and lodged there.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And in the morning, as he returned into the city, he was hungry,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And seeing a fig tree in the way, he came to it, and found nothing thereon, but leaves only, and said to it, Never fruit grow on thee henceforwards. And anon the fig tree withered.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And when his disciples saw it, they marvelled, saying, How soon is the fig tree withered!</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Verily I say unto you, if ye have faith, and doubt not, ye shall not only do that, which I have done to the fig tree, but also if ye say unto this mountain, Take thy self away, and cast thy self into the sea, it shall be done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, if ye believe, ye shall receive it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And when he was come into the Temple, the chief Priests, and the Elders of the people came unto him, as he was teaching, and said, By what authority doest thou these things? and who gave thee this authority?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Then Jesus answered and said unto them, I also will ask of you a certain thing, which if ye tell me, I likewise will tell you by what authority I do these things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> The baptism of John, whence was it? from heaven, or of men? Then they reasoned among themselves, saying, If we shall say, From heaven, he will say unto us, Why did ye not then believe him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And if we say, Of men, we fear the multitude, for all hold John as a Prophet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Then they answered Jesus, and said, We can not tell. And he said unto them, Neither tell I you by what authority I do these things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> But what think ye? A certain man had two sons, and came to the elder, and said, Son, go and work to day in my vineyard.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> But he answered, and said, I will not: yet afterward he repented himself, and went.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Then came he to the second, and said likewise. And he answered, and said, I will, Sir: yet he went not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Whether of them twain did the will of the father? They said unto him, The first. Jesus said unto them, Verily I say unto you, that the Publicans and the harlots go before you into the kingdom of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> For John came unto you in the way of righteousness, and ye believed him not: but the Publicans, and the harlots believed him, and ye, though ye saw it, were not moved with repentance afterward, that ye might believe him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Hear another parable, There was a certain householder, which planted a vineyard, and hedged it round about, and made a winepress therein, and built a tower, and let it out to husbandmen, and went into a strange country.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And when the time of the fruit drew near, he sent his servants to the husbandmen to receive the fruits thereof.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And the husbandmen took his servants and beat one, and killed another, and stoned another.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Again he sent other servants, more than the first: and they did the like unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> But last of all he sent unto them his own son, saying, They will reverence my son.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> But when the husbandmen saw the son, they said among themselves, This is the heir: come, let us kill him, and let us take his inheritance.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> So they took him, and cast him out of the vineyard, and slew him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> When therefore the Lord of the vineyard shall come, what will he do to those husbandmen?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> They said unto him, He will cruelly destroy those wicked men, and will let out his vineyard unto other husbandmen, which shall deliver him the fruits in their seasons.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> Jesus said unto them, Read ye never in the Scriptures, The stone which the builders refused, the same is made the head of the corner? This was the Lords doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Therefore say I unto you, The kingdom of God shall be taken from you, and shall be given to a nation, which shall bring forth the fruits thereof.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> And whosoever shall fall on this stone, he shall be broken: but on whomsoever it shall fall, it will dash him a pieces.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> And when the chief Priests and Pharisees had heard his parables, they perceived that he spake of them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> And they seeking to lay hands on him, feared the people, because they took him as a Prophet.</p>
