# Matthew 25

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">25</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Then the kingdom of heaven shall be likened unto ten virgins, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And five of them were wise, and five foolish.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> The foolish took their lamps, but took none oil with them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> But the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Now while the bridegroom tarried long, all slumbered and slept.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And at midnight there was a cry made, Behold, the bridegroom cometh: go out to meet him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And the foolish said to the wise, Give us of your oil, for our lamps are out.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> But the wise answered, saying, Not so, lest there will not be enough for us and you: but go ye rather to them that sell, and buy for your selves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And while they went to buy, the bridegroom came: and they that were ready, went in with him to the wedding, and the gate was shut.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Afterwards came also the other virgins, saying, Lord, Lord, open to us.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> But he answered, and said, Verily I say unto you, I know you not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Watch therefore: for ye know neither the day, nor the hour, when the son of man will come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> For the kingdom of heaven is as a man that going into a strange country, called his servants, and delivered to them his goods.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And unto one he gave five talents, and to an other two, and to another one, to every man after his own ability, and straightway went from home.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Then he that had received the five talents, went and occupied with them, and gained other five talents.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Likewise also, he that received two, he also gained other two.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> But he that received that one, went and digged it in the earth, and hid his masters money.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> But after a long season, the master of those servants came, and reckoned with them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Then came he that had received five talents, and brought other five talents, saying, Master, thou deliveredst unto me five talents: behold, I have gained with them other five talents.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then his master said unto him, It is well done good servant and faithful, Thou hast been faithful in little, I will make thee ruler over much: enter into thy masters joy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Also he that had received two talents, came, and said, Master, thou deliveredst unto me two talents: behold, I have gained two other talents more.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> His master said unto him, It is well done good servant, and faithful, Thou hast been faithful in little, I will make thee ruler over much: enter into thy masters joy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Then he which had received the one talent, came, and said, Master, I knew that thou wast an hard man, which reapest where thou sowedst not, and gatherest where thou strawedst not:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> I was therefore afraid, and went, and hid thy talent in the earth: behold, thou hast thine own.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And his master answered, and said unto him, Thou evil servant, and slothful, thou knewest that I reap where I sowed not, and gather where I strawed not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Thou oughtest therefore to have put my money to the exchangers, and then at my coming should I have received mine own with vantage.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Take therefore the talent from him, and give it unto him which hath ten talents.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> For unto every man that hath, it shall be given, and he shall have abundance, and from him that hath not, even that he hath, shall be taken away.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Cast therefore that unprofitable servant into utter darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And when the Son of man cometh in his glory, and all the holy Angels with him, then shall he sit upon the throne of his glory,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And before him shall be gathered all nations, and he shall separate them one from another, as a shepherd separateth the sheep from the goats.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, and the goats on the left.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Then shall the king say to them on his right hand, Come ye blessed of my father: take the inheritance of the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> For I was an hungered, and ye gave me meat: I thirsted, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> I was naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> Then shall the righteous answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungered, and fed thee? or a thirst, and gave thee drink?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And when saw we thee a stranger, and took thee in unto us? or naked, and clothed thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Or when saw we thee sick, or in prison, and came unto thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And the King shall answer, and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, in as much as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it to me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Then shall he say unto them on the left hand, Depart from me ye cursed, into everlasting fire, which is prepared for the devil and his angels.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> For I was an hungered, and ye gave me no meat: I thirsted, and ye gave me no drink:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> I was a stranger, and ye took me not in unto you: I was naked, and ye clothed me not: sick, and in prison, and ye visited me not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> Then shall they also answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungered, or a thirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister unto thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Then shall he answer them, and say, Verily I say unto you, in as much as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> And these shall go into everlasting paine, and the righteous into life eternal.</p>
</div>
