# Matthew 26

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And it came to pass, when Jesus had finished all these sayings, he said unto his disciples,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Ye know that after two days is the Passover, and the Son of man shall be delivered to be crucified.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then assembled together the chief Priests, and the Scribes, and the Elders of the people into the hall of the high Priest called Caiaphas:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And consulted together that they might take Jesus by subtlety, and kill him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> But they said, Not on the feast day, least any uproar be among the people.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And when Jesus was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> There came unto him a woman, which had a box of very costly ointment, and poured it on his head, as he sat at the table.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And when his disciples saw it, they had indignation, saying, What needed this waste?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> For this ointment might have been sold for much, and been given to the poor.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And Jesus knowing it, said unto them, Why trouble ye the woman? for she hath wrought a good work upon me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> For ye have the poor always with you, but me shall ye not have always.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> For in that she poured this ointment on my body, she did it to bury me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Verily I say unto you, wheresoever this Gospel shall be preached throughout all the world, there shall also this that she hath done, be spoken of for a memorial of her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Then one of the twelve, called Judas Iscariot, went unto the chief Priests,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And said, What will ye give me, and I will deliver him unto you? and they appointed unto him thirty pieces of silver.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And from that time, he sought opportunity to betray him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Now on the first day of the feast of unleavened bread the disciples came to Jesus, saying unto him, Where wilt thou that we prepare for thee to eat the Passover?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And he said, Go ye into the city to such a man, and say to him, The master saith, My time is at hand: I will keep the Passover at thine house with my disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And the disciples did as Jesus had given them charge, and made ready the Passover.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> So when the Even was come, he sat down with the twelve.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And as they did eat, he said, Verily I say unto you, that one of you shall betray me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And they were exceeding sorrowful, and began every one of them to say unto him, Is it I, Master?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And he answered and said, He that dippeth his hand with me in the dish, he shall betray me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Surely the Son of man goeth his way, as it is written of him: but woe be to that man, by whom the Son of man is betrayed: it had been good for that man, if he had never been born.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then Judas which betrayed him, answered and said, Is it I, Master? He said unto him, Thou hast said it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And as they did eat, Jesus took the bread, and when he had blessed, he brake it, and gave it to the disciples, and said, Take, eat: this is my body.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Also he took the cup, and when he had given thanks, he gave it them, saying, Drink ye all of it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> For this is my blood of the New Testament, that is shed for many, for the remission of sins.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> I say unto you, that I will not drink henceforth of this fruit of the vine until that day, when I shall drink it new with you in my Fathers kingdom.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And when they had sung a Psalm, they went out into the mount of Olives.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Then said Jesus unto them, All ye shall be offended by me this night: for it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock shall be scattered.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> But after I am risen again, I will go before you into Galilee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> But Peter answered, and said unto him, Though that all men should be offended by thee, yet will I never be offended.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Jesus said unto him, Verily I say unto thee, that this night, before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Peter said unto him, Though I should die with thee, I will in no case deny thee. Likewise also said all the disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Then went Jesus with them into a place which is called Gethsemane, and said unto his disciples, Sit ye here, while I go, and pray yonder.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And he took unto him Peter, and the two sons of Zebedee, and began to wax sorrowful, and grievously troubled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Then said Jesus unto them, My soul is very heavy, even unto the death: tarry ye here, and watch with me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> So he went a little further, and fell on his face, and prayed, saying, O my Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me: nevertheless, not as I will, but as thou wilt.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> After, he came unto the disciples, and found them a sleep, and said to Peter, What? could ye not watch with me one hour?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Watch, and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit in deed is ready, but the flesh is weak.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> Again he went away the second time, and prayed, saying, O my Father, if this cup can not pass away from me, but that I must drink it, thy will be done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> And he came, and found them a sleep again, for their eyes were heavy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> So he left them and went away again, and prayed the third time, saying the same words.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Then came he to his disciples, and said unto them, Sleep henceforth, and take your rest: behold, the hour is at hand, and the Son of man is given into the hands of sinners.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> Rise, let us go: behold, he is at hand that betrayeth me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And while he yet spake, lo Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great multitude with swords and staves, from the high Priests and Elders of the people.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> Now he that betrayed him, had given them a token, saying, Whomsoever I shall kiss, that is he, lay hold on him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> And forthwith he came to Jesus, and said, God save thee, Master, and kissed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> Then Jesus said unto him, Friend, wherefore art thou come? Then came they, and laid hands on Jesus, and took him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> And behold, one of them which were with Jesus, stretched out his hand, and drew his sword, and struck a servant of the high Priest, and smote off his ear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> Then said Jesus unto him, Put up thy sword into his place: for all that take the sword, shall perish with the sword.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> Either thinkest thou, that I can not now pray to my Father, and he will give me more than twelve legions of Angels?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> How then should the Scriptures be fulfilled, which say, that it must be so?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> The same hour said Jesus to the multitude, Ye be come out as it were against a thief, with swords and staves to take me: I sat daily teaching in the Temple among you, and ye took me not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> But all this was done, that the Scriptures of the Prophets might be fulfilled. Then all the disciples forsook him, and fled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">57</span> And they took Jesus, and led him to Caiaphas the high Priest, where the Scribes and the Elders were assembled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">58</span> And Peter followed him a far off unto the high Priests hall, and went in, and sat with the servants to see the end.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">59</span> Now the chief Priests and the Elders, and all the whole council sought false witness against Jesus, to put him to death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">60</span> But they found none, and though many false witnesses came, yet found they none: but at the last came two false witnesses,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">61</span> And said, This man said, I can destroy the Temple of God, and build it in three days.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">62</span> Then the chief Priest arose, and said to him, Answerest thou nothing? What is the matter that these men witness against thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">63</span> But Jesus held his peace. Then the chief Priest answered, and said to him, I charge thee swear unto us by the living God, to tell us, If thou be that Christ the Son of God, or no.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">64</span> Jesus said to him, Thou hast said it: nevertheless I say unto you, hereafter shall ye see the Son of man, sitting at the right hand of the power of God, and come in the clouds of the heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">65</span> Then the high Priest rent his clothes, saying, He hath blasphemed, what have we any more need of witnesses? behold: now ye have heard his blasphemy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">66</span> What think ye? They answered, and said, He is guilty of death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">67</span> Then spat they in his face, and buffeted him, and other smote him with rods,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">68</span> Saying, Prophecy to us, O Christ, Who is he that smote thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">69</span> Peter sat without in the hall, and a maid came to him, saying, Thou also wast with Jesus of Galilee:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">70</span> But he denied before them all, saying, I wot not what thou sayest.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">71</span> And when he went out into the porch, another maid saw him, and said unto them that were there, This man was also with Jesus of Nazareth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">72</span> And again he denied with an oath, saying, I know not the man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">73</span> So after a while, came unto him they that stood by, and said unto Peter, Surely thou art also one of them: for even thy speech bewrayeth thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">74</span> Then began he to curse himself, and to swear, saying, I know not the man. And immediately the cock crew.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">75</span> Then Peter remembered the words of Jesus, which had said unto him, Before the cock crow thou shalt deny me thrice. So he went out, and wept bitterly.</p>
