# Matthew 18

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">18</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> The same time the disciples came unto Jesus, saying, Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And Jesus called a little child unto him, and set him in the midst of them,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And said, Verily I say unto you, except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And whosoever shall receive one such little child in my name, receiveth me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> But whosoever shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him, that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Woe be unto the world because of offences: for it must needs be that offences shall come, but woe be to that man by whom the offence cometh.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Wherefore, if thy hand or thy foot cause thee to offend, cut them off, and cast them from thee: it is better for thee to enter into life, halt, or maimed, then having two hands, or two feet, to be cast into everlasting fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And if thine eye cause thee to offend, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: it is better for thee to enter into life with one eye, then having two eyes to be cast into hell fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> See that ye despise not one of these little ones: for I say unto you, that in heaven their Angels always behold the face of my Father which is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> For the Son of man is come to save that which was lost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> How think ye? If a man have an hundred sheep, and one of them be gone astray, doeth he not leave ninety and nine, and go into the mountains, and seek that which is gone astray?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And if so be that he find it, verily I say unto you, he rejoiceth more of that sheep, then of the ninety and nine which went not astray:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> So is it not ye will of your Father which is in heaven, that one of these little ones should perish.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Moreover, if thy brother trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone: if he hear thee, thou hast won thy brother.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> But if he hear thee not, take yet with thee one or two, that by the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be confirmed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And if he refuse to hear them, tell it unto the Church: and if he refuse to hear the Church also, let him be unto thee as an heathen man, and a Publican.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Verily I say unto you, Whatsoever ye bind on earth, shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye loose on earth, shall be loosed in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Again, verily I say unto you, that if two of you shall agree in earth upon any thing, whatsoever they shall desire, it shall be given them of my Father which is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> For where two or three are gathered together in my Name, there am I in the midst of them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then came Peter to him, and said, Master, how oft shall my brother sin against me, and I shall forgive him? unto seven times?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Jesus said unto him, I say not to thee, Unto seven times, but, Unto seventy times seven times.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Therefore is the kingdom of heaven likened unto a certain King, which would take an account of his servants.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> And when he had begun to reckon, one was brought unto him, which ought him ten thousand talents.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And because he had nothing to pay, his Lord commanded him to be sold, and his wife, and his children, and all that he had, and the debt to be paid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> The servant therefore fell down, and worshipped him, saying, Lord, refrain thine anger toward me, and I will pay thee all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Then that servants Lord had compassion, and loosed him, and forgave him the debt.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> But when the servant was departed, he found one of his fellow servants, which ought him an hundred pence, and he laid hands on him, and throttled him, saying, Pay me that thou owest.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Then his fellow servant fell down at his feet, and besought him, saying, Refrain thine anger towards me, and I will pay thee all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Yet he would not, but went and cast him into prison, till he should pay the debt.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And when his other fellow servants saw what was done, they were very sorry, and came, and declared unto their Lord all that was done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Then his Lord called him unto him, and said to him, O evil servant, I forgave thee all that debt, because thou prayedst me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Oughtest not thou also to have had pity on thy fellow servant, even as I had pity on thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> So his Lord was wroth, and delivered him to the tormentors, till he should pay all that was due to him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> So likewise shall mine heavenly Father do unto you, except ye forgive from your hearts, each one to his brother their trespasses.</p>
