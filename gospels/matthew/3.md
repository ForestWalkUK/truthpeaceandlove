<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">3</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And in those days, John the Baptist came and preached in the wilderness of Judea,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And said, Repent: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> For this is he of whom it is spoken by the Prophet Esaias, saying, The voice of him that crieth in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord: make his paths straight.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And this John had his garment of camels hear, and a girdle of a skin about his loins: his meat was also locusts and wild honey.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Then went out to him Jerusalem and all Judea, and all the region round about Jordan.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And they were baptized of him in Jordan, confessing their sins.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Now when he saw many of the Pharisees, and of the Sadducees come to his baptism, he said unto them, O generations of vipers, who hath forewarned you to flee from the anger to come?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Bring forth therefore fruit worthy amendment of life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And think not to say with your selves, We have Abraham to our father: for I say unto you, that God is able even of these stones to raise up children unto Abraham.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And now also is the axe put to the root of the trees: therefore every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit, is hewn down, and cast into the fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> In deed I baptize you with water to amendment of life, but he that cometh after me, is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he will baptize you with the holy Ghost, and with fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Which hath his fan in his hand, and will make clean his floor, and gather his wheat into his garner, but will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then came Jesus from Galilee to Jordan unto John, to be baptized of him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> But John earnestly put him back, saying, I have need to be baptized of thee, and comest thou to me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Then Jesus answering, said to him, Let be now: for thus it becometh us to fulfill all righteousness. So he suffered him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And Jesus when he was baptized, came straight out of the water. And lo, the heaves were opened unto him, and John saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, and lighting upon him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And lo, a voice came from heaven, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.</p>
</div>
