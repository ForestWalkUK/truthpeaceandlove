# Matthew 5

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And when he saw the multitude, he went up into a mountain: and when he was set, his disciples came to him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And he opened his mouth and taught them, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Blessed are they which hunger and thirst for righteousness: for they shall be filled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Blessed are the pure in heart: for they shall see God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Blessed are the peace makers: for they shall be called the children of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Blessed are they which suffer persecution for righteousness sake: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Blessed shall ye be when men revile you, and persecute you, and say all manner of evil against you for my sake, falsely.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Rejoice and be glad, for great is your reward in heaven: for so persecuted they the Prophets which were before you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Ye are the salt of the earth: but if the salt have lost his savour, wherewith shall it be salted? It is thenceforth good for nothing, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill, cannot be hid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick, and it giveth light unto all that are in the house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Think not that I am come to destroy the Law, or the Prophets. I am not come to destroy them, but to fulfill them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> For truly I say unto you, Till heaven, and earth perish, one jot or one title of the Law shall not scape, till all things be fulfilled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Whosoever therefore shall break one of these least commandments, and teach men so, he shall be called the least in the kingdom of heaven: but whosoever shall observe and teach them, the same shall be called great in the kingdom of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> For I say unto you, except your righteousness exceed the righteousness of the Scribes and Pharisees, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Ye have heard that it was said unto them of the old time, Thou shalt not kill: for whosoever killeth shall be culpable of judgement.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> But I say unto you, whosoever is angry with his brother unadvisedly, shall be culpable of judgment. And whosoever saith unto his brother, Raca, shall be worthy to be punished by the Council. And whosoever shall say, Fool, shall be worthy to be punished with hell fire.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> If then thou bring thy gift to the altar, and there rememberest that thy brother hath ought against thee,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Leave there thine offering before the altar, and go thy way: first be reconciled to thy brother, and then come and offer thy gift.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Agree with thine adversary quickly, whiles thou art in the way with him, lest thine adversary deliver thee to the Judge, and the Judge deliver thee to the sergeant, and thou be cast into prison.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Verily I say unto thee, thou shalt not come out thence, till thou hast paid the utmost farthing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Ye have heard that it was said to them of old time, Thou shalt not commit adultery.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> But I say unto you, that whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her, hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Wherefore if thy right eye cause thee to offend, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: for better it is for thee, that one of thy members perish, then that thy whole body should be cast into hell.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Also if thy right hand make thee to offend, cut it off, and cast it from thee: for better it is for thee that one of thy members perish, then that thy whole body should be cast into hell.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> It hath been said also, Whosoever shall put away his wife, let him give her a bill of divorcement.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> But I say unto you, whosoever shall put away his wife (except it be for fornication) causeth her to commit adultery: and whosoever shall marry her that is divorced, committeth adultery.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Again, ye have heard that it was said to them of old time, Thou shalt not forswear thy self, but shalt perform thine oaths to the Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> But I say unto you, Swear not at all, neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Nor yet by the earth: for it is his footstool: neither by Jerusalem: for it is the city of the great King.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Neither shalt thou swear by thine head, because thou canst not make one hear white or black.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> But let your communication be Yea, yea: Nay, nay. For whatsoever is more than these, cometh of evil.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Ye have heard that it hath been said, An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> But I say unto you, Resist not evil: but whosoever shall smite thee on thy right cheek, turn to him the other also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And if any man will sue thee at the law, and take away thy coat, let him have thy cloak also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And whosoever will compel thee to go a mile, go with him twain.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> Give to him that asketh, and from him that would borrow of thee, turn not away.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Ye have heard that it hath been said, Thou shalt love thy neighbour, and hate your enemy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> But I say unto you, Love your enemies: bless them that curse you: do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which hurt you, and persecute you,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> That ye may be the children of your father that is in heaven: for he maketh his sun to arise on the evil, and the good, and sendeth rain on the just, and unjust.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> For if ye love them, which love you, what reward shall you have? Do not the Publicans even the same?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And if ye be friendly to your brethren only, what singular thing do ye? do not even the Publicans likewise?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> Ye shall therefore be perfect, as your Father which is in heaven, is perfect.</p>
</div>
