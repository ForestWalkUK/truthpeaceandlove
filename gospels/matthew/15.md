# Matthew 15

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Then came to Jesus the Scribes and Pharisees, which were of Jerusalem, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the Elders? for they wash not their hands when they eat bread.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> But he answered and said unto them, Why do ye also transgress the commandment of God by your tradition?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> For God hath commanded, saying, Honour thy father and mother: and he that curseth father or mother, let him die the death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> But ye say, Whosoever shall say to father or mother, By the gift that is offered by me, thou mayest have profit,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Though he honour not his father, or his mother, shall be free: thus have ye made the commandment of God of no authority by your tradition.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> O hypocrites, Esaias prophesied well of you, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> This people draweth near unto me with their mouth, and honoureth me with the lips, but their heart is far off from me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> But in vain they worship me, teaching for doctrines, mens precepts.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Then he called the multitude unto him, and said to them, Hear and understand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> That which goeth into the mouth, defileth not the man, but that which cometh out of the mouth, that defileth the man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then came his disciples, and said unto him, Perceivest thou not, that the Pharisees are offended in hearing this saying?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> But he answered and said, Every plant which mine heavenly Father hath not planted, shall be rooted up.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Let them alone, they be the blind leaders of the blind: and if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Then answered Peter, and said to him, Declare unto us this parable.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Then said Jesus, Are ye yet without understanding?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Perceive ye not yet, that whatsoever entereth into the mouth, goeth into the belly, and is cast out into the draught?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> But those things which proceed out of the mouth, come from the heart, and they defile the man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> For out of the heart come evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false testimonies, slanders.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> These are the things, which defile the man: but to eat with unwashen hands, defileth not the man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And Jesus went thence, and departed into the coasts of Tyre and Sidon.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And behold, a woman a Canaanite came out of the same coasts, and cried, saying unto him, Have mercy on me, O Lord, the son of David: my daughter is miserably vexed with a devil.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> But he answered her not a word. Then came to him his disciples, and besought him, saying, Send her away, for she crieth after us.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> But he answered, and said, I am not sent, but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Yet she came, and worshipped him, saying, Lord, help me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And he answered, and said, It is not good to take the childrens bread, and to cast it to whelps.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> But she said, Truth, Lord: yet in deed the whelps eat of the crumbs, which fall from their masters table.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then Jesus answered, and said unto her, O woman, great is thy faith: be it to thee, as thou desirest. And her daughter was made whole at that hour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> So Jesus went away from thence, and came near unto the sea of Galilee, and went up into a mountain and sat down there.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And great multitudes came unto him, having with them, halt, blind, dumb, maimed, and many other, and cast them down at Jesus feet, and he healed them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> In so much that the multitude wondered, to see the dumb speak, the maimed whole, the halt to go, and the blind to see: and they glorified the God of Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Then Jesus called his disciples unto him, and said, I have compassion on this multitude, because they have continued with me already three days, and have nothing to eat: and I will not let them depart fasting, least they faint in the way.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And his disciples said unto him, Whence should we get so much bread in the wilderness, as should suffice so great a multitude!</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And Jesus said unto them, How many loaves have ye? And they said, Seven, and a few little fishes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Then he commanded the multitude to sit down on the ground,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And took the seven loaves, and the fishes, and gave thanks, and brake them, and gave to his disciples, and the disciples to the multitude.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And they did all eat, and were sufficed: and they took up of the fragments that remained, seven baskets full.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And they that had eaten, were four thousand men, beside women, and little children.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Then Jesus sent away the multitude, and took ship, and came into the parts of Magdala.</p>
</div>
