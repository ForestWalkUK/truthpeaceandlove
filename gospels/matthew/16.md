# Matthew 16

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Then came the Pharisees and Sadducees, and did tempt him, desiring him to show them a sign from heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> But he answered, and said unto them, When it is evening, ye say, Fair wether: for the sky is red.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And in the morning ye say, To day shall be a tempest: for the sky is red and lowering. O hypocrites, ye can discern the face of the sky, and can ye not discern the signs of the times?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> The wicked generation, and adulterous seeketh a sign, but there shall no sign be given it, but that sign of the Prophet Jonas: so he left them, and departed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And when his disciples were come to the other side, they had forgotten to take bread with them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Then Jesus said unto them, Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And they reasoned among themselves, saying, It is because we have brought no bread.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> But Jesus knowing it, said unto them, O ye of little faith, why reason you thus among your selves, because ye have brought no bread?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Do ye not yet perceive, neither remember the five loaves, when there were five thousand men, and how many baskets took ye up?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Neither the seven loaves when there were four thousand men, and how many baskets took ye up?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Why perceive ye not that I said not unto you concerning bread, that ye should beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then understood they that he had not said that they should beware of the leaven of bread, but of the doctrine of the Pharisees, and Sadducees.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Now when Jesus came into the coasts of Caesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, saying, Whom do men say that I, the son of man am?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And they said, Some say, John Baptist: and some, Elias: and others, Jeremias, or one of the Prophets.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> He said unto them, But whom say ye that I am?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Then Simon Peter answered, and said, Thou art that Christ, the Son of the living God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And Jesus answered, and said to him, Blessed art thou, Simon, the son of Jonas: for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And I say also unto thee, that thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my Church: and the gates of hell shall not overcome it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven, and whatsoever thou shalt bind upon earth, shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth, shall be loosed in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Then he charged his disciples, that they should tell no man that he was Jesus that Christ.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> From that time forth Jesus began to show unto his disciples, that he must go unto Jerusalem, and suffer many things of the Elders, and of the high Priests, and Scribes, and be slain, and be raised again the third day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Then Peter took him aside, and began to rebuke him, saying, Master, pity thy self: this shall not be unto thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Then he turned back, and said unto Peter, Get thee behind me, Satan: thou art an offence unto me, because thou understandest not the things that are of God, but the things that are of men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Jesus then said to his disciples, If any man will follow me, let him forsake himself: and take up his cross, and follow me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> For whosoever will save his life, shall lose it: and whosoever shall lose his life for my sake, shall find it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> For what shall it profit a man though he should win the whole world, if he lose his own soul? or what shall a man give for recompense of his soul?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> For the Son of man shall come in the glory of his Father with his Angels, and then shall he give to every man according to his deeds.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Verily I say unto you, there be some of them that stand here, which shall not taste of death, till they have seen the Son of man come in his kingdom.</p>
</div>
