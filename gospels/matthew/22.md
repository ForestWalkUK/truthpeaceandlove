# Matthew 22

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Then Jesus answered, and spake unto them again in parables, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> The kingdom of heaven is like unto a certain King which married his son,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And sent forth his servants, to call them that were bid to the wedding, but they would not come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Again he sent forth other servants, saying. Tell them which are bidden, Behold, I have prepared my dinner: mine oxen and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready: come unto the marriage.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> But they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his farm, and another about his merchandise.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And the remnant took his servants, and entreated them sharply, and slew them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> But when the King heard it, he was wroth, and sent forth his warriors, and destroyed those murderers, and burnt up their city.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Then said he to his servants, Truly the wedding is prepared: but they which were bidden, were not worthy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Go ye therefore out into the high ways, and as many as ye find, bid them to the marriage.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> So those servants went out into the high ways, and gathered together all that ever they found, both good and bad: so the wedding was furnished with guests.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Then the King came in, to see the guests, and saw there a man which had not on a wedding garment.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And he said unto him, Friend, how camest thou in hither, and hast not on a wedding garment? And he was speechless.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then said the King to the servants, Bind him hand and foot: take him away, and cast him into utter darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> For many are called, but few chosen.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Then went the Pharisees and took counsel how they might tangle him in talk.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And they sent unto him their disciples with the Herodians, saying, Master, we know that thou art true, and teachest the way of God truly, neither carest for any man: for thou considerest not the person of men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Tell us therefore, how thinkest thou? Is it lawful to give tribute unto Caesar, or not?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> But Jesus perceived their wickedness, and said, Why tempt ye me, ye hypocrites?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Show me the tribute money. And they brought him a penny.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And he said unto them, Whose is this image and superscription?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> They said unto him, Caesars. Then said he unto them, Give therefore to Caesar, the things which are Caesars, and give unto God, those things which are Gods.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And when they heard it, they marvelled, and left him, and went their way.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> The same day the Sadducees came to him (which say that there is no resurrection) and asked him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Saying, Master, Moses said, If a man die, having no children, his brother shall mary his wife by the right of alliance, and raise up seed unto his brother.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Now there were with us seven brethren, and the first married a wife, and deceased: and having none issue, left his wife unto his brother.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Likewise also the second, and the third, unto the seventh.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And last of all the woman died also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Therefore in the resurrection, whose wife shall she be of the seven? for all had her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Then Jesus answered, and said unto them, Ye are deceived, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> For in the resurrection they neither mary wives, nor wives are bestowed in marriage, but are as the Angels of God in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And concerning the resurrection of the dead, have ye not read what is spoken unto you of God, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob? God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And when the multitude heard it, they were astonied at his doctrine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> But when the Pharisees had heard, that he had put the Sadducees to silence, they assembled together.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And one of them, which was an expounder of the Law, asked him a question, tempting him, and saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Master, which is the great commandment in the Law?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> Jesus said to him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thine heart, with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> This is the first and the great commandment.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And the second is like unto this, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thy self.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> On these two commandments hangeth the whole Law, and the Prophets.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> While the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> Saying, What think ye of Christ? whose son is he? They said unto him, Davids.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> He said unto them, How then doeth David in spirit call him Lord, saying,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at my right hand, till I make thine enemies thy footstool?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> If then David call him Lord, how is he his son?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> And none could answer him a word, neither durst any from that day forth ask him any more questions.</p>
</div>
