# Matthew 27

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">27</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> When the morning was come, all the chief Priests, and the elders of the people took counsel against Jesus, to put him to death,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And led him away bound, and delivered him unto Pontius Pilate the governor.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then when Judas which betrayed him, saw that he was condemned, he repented himself, and brought again the thirty pieces of silver to the chief Priests, and Elders,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Saying, I have sinned, betraying the innocent blood. But they said, What is that to us? see thou to it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And when he had cast down the silver pieces in the Temple, he departed, and went, and hanged himself.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And the chief Priests took the silver pieces, and said, It is not lawful for us to put them into the treasure, because it is the price of blood.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And they took counsel, and bought with them a potters field, for the burial of strangers.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Wherefore that field is called, The field of blood, until this day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> (Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremias the Prophet, saying, And they took thirty silver pieces, the price of him that was valued, whom they of the children of Israel valued.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And they gave them for the potters field, as the Lord appointed me.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, saying, Art thou that King of the Jews? Jesus said unto him, Thou sayest it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And when he was accused of the chief Priests, and Elders, he answered nothing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then said Pilate unto him, Hearest thou not how many things they lay against thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> But he answered him not to one word, in so much that the governor marvelled greatly.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Now at the feast, the governor was wont to deliver unto the people a prisoner whom they would.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And they had then a notable prisoner, called Barabbas.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> When they were then gathered together, Pilate said unto the, Whether will ye that I let loose unto you Barabbas, or Jesus which is called Christ?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> (For he knew well, that for envy they had delivered him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Also when he was set down upon the judgement seat, his wife sent to him, saying, Have thou nothing to do with that just man: for I have suffered many things this day in a dream by reason of him.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> But the chief Priests and the Elders had persuaded the people that they should ask Barabbas, and should destroy Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then the governor answered, and said unto them, Whether of the twain will ye that I let loose unto you? And they said, Barabbas.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Pilate said unto them, What shall I do then with Jesus, which is called Christ? They all said to him, Let him be crucified.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Then said the governor, But what evil hath he done? Then they cried the more, saying, Let him be crucified.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> When Pilate saw that he availed nothing, but that more tumult was made, he took water and washed his hands before the multitude, saying, I am innocent of the blood of this just man: look you to it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then answered all the people, and said, His blood be on us, and on our children.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Thus let he Barabbas loose unto them, and scourged Jesus, and delivered him to be crucified.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Then the soldiers of the governor took Jesus into the common hall, and gathered about him the whole band,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And they stripped him, and put about him a scarlet robe,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And platted a crown of thorns, and put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand, and bowed their knees before him, and mocked him, saying, God save thee King of the Jews,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And spitted upon him, and took a reed, and smote him on the head.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Thus when they had mocked him, they took the robe from him, and put his own raiment on him, and led him away to crucify him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And as they came out, they found a man of Cyrene, named Simon: him they compelled to bear his cross.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And when they came unto the place called Golgotha, (that is to say, the place of dead mens skulls)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> They gave him vinegar to drink, mingled with gall: and when he had tasted thereof, he would not drink.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And when they had crucified him, they parted his garments, and did cast lots, that it might be fulfilled, which was spoken by the Prophet, They divided my garments among them, and upon my vesture did cast lots.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And they sat, and watched him there.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> They set up also over his head his cause written, THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And there were two thieves crucified with him, one on the right hand, and another on the left.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And they that passed by, reviled him, wagging their heads,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And saying, Thou that destroyest the Temple, and buildest it in three days, save thy self: if thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Likewise also the high Priests mocking him, with the Scribes, and Elders, and Pharisees, said,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> He saved others, but he cannot save him self: if he be the King of Israel, let him now come down from the cross, and we will believe in him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> He trusted in God, let him deliver him now, if he will have him: for he said, I am the Son of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> The self same thing also the thieves which were crucified with him, cast in his teeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Now from the sixth hour was there darkness over all the land, unto the ninth hour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> And about the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani? that is, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And some of them that stood there, when they heard it, said, This man calleth Elias.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> And straightway one of them ran, and took a sponge, and filled it with vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> Other said, Let be: let us see, if Elias will come and save him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> Then Jesus cried again with a loud voice, and yielded up the ghost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> And behold, the veil of the Temple was rent in twain, from the top to the bottom, and the earth did quake, and the stones were clove.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> And the graves did open themselves, and many bodies of the Saints, which slept, arose,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> And came out of the graves after his resurrection, and went into the holy city, and appeared unto many.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> When the Centurion, and they that were with him watching Jesus, saw the earthquake, and the things that were done, they feared greatly, saying, Truly this was the Son of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> And many women were there, beholding him a far off, which had followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering unto him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> Among whom was Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James, and Joses, and the mother of Zebedee sons.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">57</span> And when the even was come, there came a rich man of Arimathea, named Joseph, who had also himself been Jesus disciple.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">58</span> He went to Pilate, and asked the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded the body to be delivered.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">59</span> So Joseph took the body, and wrapped it in a clean linen cloth,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">60</span> And put it in his new tomb, which he had hewn out in a rock, and rolled a great stone to the door of the sepulchre, and departed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">61</span> And there was Mary Magdalene, and the other Mary sitting over against the sepulchre.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">62</span> Now the next day that followed the Preparation of the Sabbath, the high Priests and Pharisees assembled to Pilate,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">63</span> And said, Sir, we remember that that deceiver said, while he was yet alive, Within three days I will rise.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">64</span> Command therefore, that the sepulchre be made sure until the third day, lest his disciples come by night, and steal him away, and say unto the people, He is risen from the dead: so shall the last error be worse than the first.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">65</span> Then Pilate said unto them, Ye have a watch: go, and make it sure as ye know.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">66</span> And they went, and made the sepulchre sure with the watch, and sealed the stone.</p>
