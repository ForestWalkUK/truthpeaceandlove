# Matthew 23

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">23</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Then spake Jesus to the multitude, and to his disciples,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Saying, The Scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses seat.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do: but after their works do not: for they say, and do not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> For they bind heavy burdens, and grievous to be borne, and lay them on mens shoulders, but they themselves will not move them with one of their fingers.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> All their works they do for to be seen of men: for they make their phylacteries broad, and make long the fringes of their garments,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And love the chief place at feasts, and to have the chief seats in the assemblies,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And greetings in the markets, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> But be not ye called, Rabbi: for one is your doctor, to wit, Christ, and all ye are brethren.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And call no man your father upon the earth: for there is but one, your father which is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Be not called doctors: for one is your doctor, even Christ.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> But he that is greatest among you, let him be your servant.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> For whosoever will exalt himself, shall be brought low: and whosoever will humble himself, shall be exalted.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Woe therefore be unto you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites, because ye shut up the kingdom of heaven before men: for ye your selves go not in, neither suffer ye them that would enter, to come in.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Woe be unto you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites: for ye devour widows houses, even under a colour of long prayers: wherefore ye shall receive the greater damnation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Woe be unto you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites: for ye compass sea and land to make one of your profession: and when he is made, ye make him two fold more the child of hell, then you your selves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Woe be unto you blind guides, which say, Whosoever sweareth by the Temple, it is nothing: but whosoever sweareth by the gold of the Temple, he offendeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Ye fools and blind, whether is greater, the gold, or the Temple that sanctifieth the gold?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And whosoever sweareth by the altar, it is nothing: but whosoever sweareth by the offering that is upon it, offendeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Ye fools and blind, whether is greater, the offering, or the altar which sanctifieth the offering?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Whosoever therefore sweareth by the altar, sweareth by it, and by all things thereon.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And whosoever sweareth by the Temple, sweareth by it, and by him that dwelleth therein.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And he that sweareth by heaven, sweareth by the throne of God, and by him that sitteth thereon.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Woe be to you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites: for ye tithe mint, and anise, and cumin, and leave the weightier matters of the law, as judgement, and mercy and fidelity. These ought ye to have done, and not to have left the other.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Ye blind guides, which strain out a gnat, and swallow a camel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Woe be to you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites: for ye make clean the utter side of the cup, and of the platter: but within they are full of bribery and excess.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Thou blind Pharisee, cleanse first the inside of the cup and platter, that the outside of them may be clean also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Woe be to you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites: for ye are like unto whited tombs, which appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead mens bones, and all filthiness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> So are ye also: for outward ye appear righteous unto men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Woe be unto you, Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites: for ye build the tombs of the Prophets, and garnish the sepulchres of the righteous,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partners with them in the blood of the Prophets.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> So then ye be witnesses unto your selves, that ye are the children of them that murdered the Prophets.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Fulfill ye also ye measure of your fathers.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> O serpents, the generation of vipers, how should ye escape the damnation of hell!</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Wherefore behold, I send unto you Prophets, and wise men, and Scribes, and of them ye shall kill and crucify: and of them shall ye scourge in your Synagogues, and persecute from city to city,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> That upon you may come all the righteous blood that was shed upon the earth, from the blood of Abel the righteous, unto the blood of Zacharias the son of Barachias, whom ye slew between the Temple and the altar.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Verily I say unto you, all these things shall come upon this generation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, which killest the Prophets, and stonest them which are sent to thee, how often would I have gathered thy children together, as the hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, and ye would not!</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Behold, your habitation shall be left unto you desolate,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> For I say unto you, ye shall not see me henceforth till that ye say, Blessed is he that cometh in the Name of the Lord.</p>
</div>
