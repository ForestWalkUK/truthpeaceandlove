# Matthew 10

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">10</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And he called his twelve disciples unto him, and gave them power against unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal every sickness, and every disease.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Now the names of the twelve Apostles are these. The first is Simon, called Peter, and Andrew his brother: James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Philip and Bartholomew: Thomas, and Matthew that Publican: James the son of Alpheus, and Lebbeus whose surname was Thaddeus:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Simon the Canaanite, and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> These twelve did Jesus send forth, and commanded them, saying, Go not into the way of of the Gentiles, and into the cities of the Samaritans enter ye not:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> But go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And as ye go, preach, saying, The kingdom of heaven is at hand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Heal the sick: cleanse the lepers: raise up the dead: cast out the devils. Freely ye have received, freely give.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Possess not gold, nor silver, nor money in your girdles,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Nor a scrip for the journey, neither two coats, neither shoes, nor a staff: for the workman is worthy of his meat.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And into whatsoever city or town ye shall come, enquire who is worthy in it, and there abide till ye go thence.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And when ye come into an house, salute the same.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And if the house be worthy, let your peace come upon it: but if it be not worthy, let your peace return to you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear your words, when ye depart out of that house, or that city, shake off the dust of your feet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Truly I say unto you, it shall be easier for them of the land of Sodom and Gomorrha in the day of judgement, then for that city.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Behold, I send you as sheep in the midst of the wolves: be ye therefore wise as serpents, and innocent as doves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> But beware of men, for they will deliver you up to the Councils, and will scourge you in their Synagogues.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And ye shall be brought to the governors and Kings for my sake, in witness to them, and to the Gentiles.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> But when they deliver you up, take no thought how or what ye shall speak: for it shall be given you in that hour, what ye shall say.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> For it is not ye that speak, but the spirit of your father which speaketh in you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And the brother shall betray the brother to death, and the father the son, and the children shall rise against their parents, and shall cause them to die.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And ye shall be hated of all men for my Name: but he that endureth to the end, he shall be saved.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And when they persecute you in this city, flee into another: for verily I say unto you, ye shall not go over all the cities of Israel, till the Son of man be come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> The disciple is not above his master, nor the servant above his Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> It is enough for the disciple to be as his master is, and the servant as his Lord. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, how much more them of his household?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Fear them not therefore: for there is nothing covered, that shall not be disclosed, nor hid, that shall not be known.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> What I tell you in darkness, that speak ye in light: and what ye hear in the ear, that preach ye on the houses.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And fear ye not them which kill the body, but are nor able to kill the soul: but rather fear him, which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Are not two sparrows sold for a farthing, and one of them shall not fall on the ground without your Father?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Yea, and all the hears of your head are numbered.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Fear ye not therefore, ye are of more value then many sparrows.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Whosoever therefore shall confess me before men, him will I confess also before my Father which is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> But whosoever shall deny me before me, him will I also deny before my Father which is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Think not that I am come to send peace into the earth: I came not to send peace, but the sword.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> For I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And a mans enemies shall be they of his own household.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> He that loveth father or mother more than me, is not worthy of me. And he that loveth son, or daughter more than me, is not worthy of me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And he that taketh not his cross, and followeth after me, is not worthy of me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> He that will find his life, shall lose it: and he that loseth his life for my sake, shall find it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> He that receiveth you, receiveth me: and he that receiveth me, receiveth him that hath sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> He that receiveth a Prophet in the name of a Prophet, shall receive a Prophets reward: and he that receiveth a righteous man, in the name of a righteous man, shall receive the reward of a righteous man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And whosoever shall give unto one of these little ones to drink a cup of cold water only, in the name of a disciple, verily I say unto you, he shall not lose his reward.</p>
</div>
