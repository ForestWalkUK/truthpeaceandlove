# Matthew 6

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">6</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Take heed that ye give not your alms before men, to be seen of them, or else ye shall have no reward of your Father which is in heave.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Therefore when thou givest thine alms, thou shalt not make a trumpet to be blown before thee, as the hypocrites do in the Synagogues and in the streets, to be praised of men. Verily I say unto you, they have their reward.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> But when thou doest thine alms, let not thy left hand know what thy right hand doeth,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> That thine alms may be in secret, and thy Father that seeth in secret, he will reward thee openly.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And when thou prayest, be not as the hypocrites: for they love to stand, and pray in the Synagogues, and in the corners of the streets, because they would be seen of men. Verily I say unto you, they have their reward.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> But when thou prayest, enter into thy chamber and when thou hast shut thy door, pray unto thy Father which is in secret, and thy Father which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Also when ye pray, use no vain repetitions as the Heathen: for they think to be heard for their much babbling.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Be ye not like them therefore: for your Father knoweth whereof ye have need, before ye ask of him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> After this manner therefore pray ye, Our father which art in heaven, hallowed be thy name.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Thy Kingdom come. Thy will be done even in earth, as it is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Give us this day our daily bread.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: for thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory for ever. Amen.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> For if ye do forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> But if ye do not forgive men their trespasses,, no more will your father forgive you your trespasses.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Moreover, when ye fast, look not sour as the hypocrites: for they disfigure their faces, that they might seem unto men to fast. Verily I say unto you, that they have their reward.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> But when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> That thou seem not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father which seeth in secret, will reward thee openly.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Lay not up treasures for your selves upon the earth, where the moth and canker corrupt, and where thieves dig through and steal.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> But lay up treasures for your selves in heaven, where neither the moth nor canker corrupteth, and where thieves neither dig through, nor steal.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> The light of the body is the eye: if then thine eye be single, thy whole body shall be light.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> But if thine eye be wicked, then all thy body shall be dark. Wherefore if the light that is in thee, be darkness, how great is that darkness?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> No man can serve two masters: for either he shall hate the one, and love the other, or else he shall lean to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and riches.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Therefore I say unto you, be not careful for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink: nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on. Is not the life more worth then meat? and the body then raiment?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Behold the fowls of the heaven: for they sow not, neither reap, nor carry into the barns: yet your heavenly Father feedeth them. Are ye not much better than they?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Which of you by taking care is able to add one cubit unto his stature?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And why care ye for raiment? Learn how the lilies of the field do grow: they are not wearied, neither spin:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Yet I say unto you, that even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Wherefore if God so clothe the grass of the field which is to day, and to morrow is cast into the oven, shall he not do much more unto you, O ye of little faith?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Therefore take no thought, saying, What shall we eat? or what shall we drink? or where with shall we be clothed?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> (For after all these things seek the Gentiles) for your heavenly Father knoweth, that ye have need of all these things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness, and all these things shall be ministered unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Care not then for the morrow: for the morrow shall care for it self: the day hath enough with his own grief.</p>
</div>
