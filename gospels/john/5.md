# John 5

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> After that, there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And there is at Jerusalem by the place of the sheep, a pool called in Hebrew Bethesda, having five porches:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> In the which lay a great multitude of sick folk, of blind, halt, and withered, waiting for the moving of the water.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> For an Angel went down at a certain season into the pool, and troubled the water: whosoever then first, after the stirring of the water, stepped in, was made whole of whatsoever disease he had.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And a certain man was there, which had been diseased eight and thirty years.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> When Jesus saw him lie, and knew that he now long time had been diseased, he said unto him, Wilt thou be made whole?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> The sick man answered him, Sir, I have no man, when the water is troubled, to put me into the pool: but while I am coming, another steppeth down before me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Jesus said unto him, Rise: take up thy bed, and walk.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And immediately the man was made whole, and took up his bed, and walked: and the same day was the Sabbath.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> The Jews therefore said to him that was made whole, It is the Sabbath day: it is not lawful for thee to carry thy bed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> He answered them, He that made me whole, he said unto me, Take up thy bed, and walk.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then asked they him, What man is that which said unto thee, Take up thy bed and walk?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And he that was healed, knew not who it was: for Jesus had conveyed himself away from the multitude that was in that place.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And after that, Jesus found him in the Temple, and said unto him, Behold, thou art made whole: sin no more, lest a worse thing come unto thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> The man departed and told the Jews that it was Jesus, which had made him whole.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And therefore the Jews did persecute Jesus, and sought to slay him, because he had done these things on the Sabbath day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> But Jesus answered them, My Father worketh hitherto, and I work.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Therefore the Jews sought the more to kill him: not only because he had broken the Sabbath: but said also that God was his Father, and made himself equal with God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Then answered Jesus, and said unto them, Verily, verily I say unto you, The Son can do nothing of himself, save that he seeth the Father do: for whatsoever things he doth, the same things doeth the Son in like manner.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> For the Father loveth the Son, and showeth him all things, whatsoever he himself doeth, and he will show him greater works then these, that ye should marvel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> For likewise as the Father rayseth up the dead, and quickeneth them, so the Son quickeneth whom he will.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> For the Father judgeth no man, but hath committed all judgement unto the Son,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Because that all men should honour the Son, as they honour the Father: he that honoureth not the Son, the same honoureth not the Father, which hath sent him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Verily, verily I say unto you, he that heareth my word, and believeth him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation, but hath passed from death unto life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Verily, verily I say unto you, the hour shall come, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God: and they that hear it, shall live.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> For as the Father hath life in himself, so likewise hath he given to the Son to have life in himself,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And hath given him power also to execute judgement, in that he is the Son of man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Marvel not at this: for the hour shall come, in the which all that are in the graves, shall hear his voice.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And they shall come forth, that have done good, unto the resurrection of life: but they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of condemnation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> I can do nothing of mine own self: as I hear, I judge: and my judgement is just, because I seek not mine own will, but the will of the Father who hath sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> If I should bear witness of my self, my witness were not true.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> There is another that beareth witness of me, and I know that the witness, which he beareth of me, is true.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Ye sent unto John, and he bare witness unto the truth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> But I receive not the record of man: nevertheless these things I say, that ye might be saved.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> He was a burning, and a shining candle: and ye would for a season have rejoiced in his light.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> But I have greater witness then the witness of John: for the works which the Father hath given me to finish, the same works that I do, bear witness of me, that the Father sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And the Father himself, which hath sent me, beareth witness of me. Ye have not heard his voice at any time, neither have ye seen his shape.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And his word have you not abiding in you: for whom he hath sent, him ye believed not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Search the Scriptures: for in them ye think to have eternal life, and they are they which testify of me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> But ye will not come to me, that ye might have life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> I receive not the praise of men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> But I know you, that ye have not the love of God in you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> I am come in my Fathers Name, and ye receive me not: if another shall come in his own name, him will ye receive.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> How can ye believe, which receive honour one of another, and seek not the honour that cometh of God alone?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Do not think that I will accuse you to my Father: there is one that accuseth you, even Moses, in whom ye trust.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> For had ye believed Moses, ye would have believed me: for he wrote of me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> But if ye believe not his writings, how shall ye believe my words?</p>
</div>
