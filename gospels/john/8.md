# John 8

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">8</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And Jesus went unto the mount of Olives,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And early in the morning came again into the Temple, and all the people came unto him, and he sat down, and taught them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then the Scribes, and the Pharisees brought unto him a woman, taken in adultery, and set her in the midst,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And said unto him, Master, we found this woman committing adultery, even in the very act.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Now Moses in our Law commanded, that such should be stoned: what sayest thou therefore?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And this they said to tempt him, that they might have, whereof to accuse him. But Jesus stooped down, and with his finger wrote on the ground.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And while they continued asking him, he lift himself up, and said unto them, Let him that is among you without sin, cast the first stone at her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And again he stooped down, and wrote on the ground.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And when they heard it, being accused by their own conscience, they went out one by one, beginning at the eldest even to the last: so Jesus was left alone, and the woman standing in the midst.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> When Jesus had lift up himself again, and saw no man, but the woman, he said unto her, Woman, where are those thine accusers? hath no man condemned thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> She said, No man, Lord. And Jesus said, Neither do I condemn thee: go and sin no more.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am that light of the world: he that followeth me, shall not walk in darkness, but shall have that light of life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> The Pharisees therefore said unto him, Thou bearest record of thy self: thy record is not true.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Jesus answered, and said unto them, Though I bear record of my self, yet my record is true: for I know whence I came, and whither I go: but ye cannot tell whence I come, and whither I go.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Ye judge after the flesh: I judge no man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And if I also judge, my judgement is true: for I am not alone, but I, and the Father, that sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And it is also written in your Law, that the testimony of two men is true.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> I am one that bear witness of my self, and the Father that sent me, beareth witness of me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Then said they unto him, Where is that Father of thine? Jesus answered, Ye neither know me, nor that Father of mine. If ye had known me, ye should have known that Father of mine also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> These words spake Jesus in the treasury, as he taught in the Temple, and no man laid hands on him: for his hour was not yet come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then said Jesus again unto them, I go my way, and ye shall seek me, and shall die in your sins, Whither I go, can ye not come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Then said the Jews, Will he kill himself, because he saith, Whither I go, can ye not come?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And he said unto them, Ye are from beneath, I am from above: ye are of this world, I am not of this world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> I said therefore unto you, That ye shall die in your sins: for except ye believe, that I am he, ye shall die in your sins.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then said they unto him, Who art thou? And Jesus said unto them, Even the same thing that I said unto you from the beginning.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> I have many things to say, and to judge of you: but he that sent me, is true, and the things that I have heard of him, those speak I to the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> They understood not that he spake to them of the Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then said Jesus unto them, When ye have lift up the Son of man, then shall ye know that I am he, and that I do nothing of my self, but as my Father hath taught me, so I speak these things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> For he that sent me, is with me: the Father hath not left me alone, because I do always those things that please him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> As he spake these things, many believed in him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Then said Jesus to the Jews which believed in him, If ye continue in my word, ye are verily my disciples,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> They answered him, We be Abrahams seed, and were never bond to any man: why sayest thou then, Ye shall be made free?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Jesus answered them, Verily, verily I say unto you, that whosoever committeth sin, is the servant of sin.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And the servant abideth not in the house for ever: but the Son abideth for ever.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> If that Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free in deed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> I know that ye are Abrahams seed, but ye seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> I speak that which I have seen with my Father: and ye do that which ye have seen with your father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> They answered, and said unto him, Abraham is our father. Jesus said unto them, If ye were Abrahams children, ye would do the works of Abraham.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> But now ye go about to kill me, a man that have told you the truth, which I have heard of God: this did not Abraham.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Ye do the works of your father. Then said they to him, We are not borne of fornication: we have one Father, which is God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> Therefore Jesus said unto them, If God were your Father, then would ye love me: for I proceeded forth, and came from God, neither came I of my self, but he sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Why do ye not understand my talk? because ye cannot hear my word.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> Ye are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father ye will do: he hath been a murderer from the beginning, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaketh a lie, then speaketh he of his own: for he is a liar, and the father thereof.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> And because I tell you the truth, ye believe me not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> Which of you can rebuke me of sin? and if I say the truth, why do ye not believe me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> He that is of God, heareth Gods words: ye therefore hear them not, because ye are not of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> Then answered the Jews, and said unto him, Say we not well that thou art a Samaritan, and hast a devil?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> Jesus answered, I have not a devil, but I honour my Father, and ye have dishonoured me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> And I seek not mine own praise: but there is one that seeketh it, and judgeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> Verily, verily I say unto you, If a man keep my word, he shall never see death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> Then said the Jews to him, Now know we that thou hast a devil. Abraham is dead, and the Prophets: and thou sayest, If a man keep my word, he shall never taste of death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> Art thou greater than our father Abraham, which is dead? and the Prophets are dead: whom makest thou thy self?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> Jesus answered, If I honour my self, mine honour is nothing worth: it is my Father that honoureth me, whom ye say, that he is your God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> Yet ye have not known him: but I know him, and if I should say I know him not, I should be a liar like unto you: but I know him, and keep his word.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day, and he saw it, and was glad.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">57</span> Then said the Jews unto him, Thou art not yet fifty year old, and hast thou seen Abraham?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">58</span> Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily I say unto you, before Abraham was, I am.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">59</span> Then took they up stones, to cast at him, but Jesus hid himself, and went out of the Temple: And he passed through the midst of them, and so went his way.</p>
