# John 6

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> After these things, Jesus went his way over the sea of Galilee, which is Tiberias.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And a great multitude followed him, because they saw his miracles, which he did on them that were diseased.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then Jesus went up into a mountain, and there he sat with his disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Now the Passover, a feast of the Jews, was near.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Then Jesus lift up his eyes, and seeing that a great multitude came unto him, he said unto Philip, Whence shall we buy bread, that these might eat?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> (And this he said to prove him: for he himself knew what he would do.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Philip answered him, Two hundred penny worth of bread is not sufficient for them, that every one of them may take a little.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Then said unto him one of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peters brother,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> There is a little boy here, which hath five barley loaves, and two fishes: but what are they among so many?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And Jesus said, Make the people sit down. (Now there was much grass in that place.) Then the men sat down in number, about five thousand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> And Jesus took the bread, and gave thanks, and gave to the disciples, and the disciples, to them that were set down: and likewise of the fishes as much as they would.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And when they were satisfied, he said unto his disciples, Gather up the broken meat which remaineth, that nothing be lost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Then they gathered it together, and filled twelve baskets with the broken meat of the five barley loaves, which remained unto them that had eaten.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Then the men, when they had seen the miracle that Jesus did, said, This is of a truth that Prophet that should come into the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> When Jesus therefore perceived that they would come, and take him to make him a King, he departed again into a mountain himself alone.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> When even was now come, his disciples went down unto the sea,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And entered into a ship, and went over the sea, towards Capernaum: and now it was dark, and Jesus was not come to them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And the Sea arose with a great wind that blew.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And when they had rowed about five and twenty, or thirty furlongs, they saw Jesus walking on the sea, and drawing near unto the ship: so they were afraid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> But he said unto them, It is I: be not afraid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then willingly they received him into the ship, and the ship was by and by at the land, whither they went.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> The day following, the people which stood on the other side of the sea, saw that there was none other ship there, save that one, whereinto his disciples were entered, and that Jesus went not with his disciples in the ship, but that his disciples were gone alone,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And that there came other ships from Tiberias near unto the place where they ate the bread, after the Lord had given thanks.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Now when the people saw that Jesus was not there, neither his disciples, they also took shipping, and came to Capernaum, seeking for Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And when they had found him on the other side of the sea, they said unto him, Rabbi, when camest thou hither?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Jesus answered them; and said, Verily, verily I say unto you, ye seek me not because ye saw the miracles, but because ye ate of the loaves, and were filled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Labour not for the meat which perisheth, but for the meat that endureth unto everlasting life, which the Son of man shall give unto you: for him hath God the Father sealed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then said they unto him, What shall we do, that we might work the works of God?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Jesus answered, and said unto them, This is the work of God, that ye believe in him, whom he hath sent.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> They said therefore unto him, What sign showest thou then, that we may see it, and believe thee? what doest thou work?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Our fathers did eat Manna in the desert, as it is written, He gave them bread from heaven to eat.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Then Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily I say unto you, Moses gave you not that bread from heaven, but my Father giveth you that true bread from heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> For the bread of God is he which cometh down from heaven, and giveth life unto the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Then they said unto him, Lord, evermore give us this bread.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And Jesus said unto them, I am that bread of life: he that cometh to me, shall not hunger, and he that believeth in me, shall never thirst.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> But I said unto you, that ye also have seen me, and believe not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> All that the Father giveth me, shall come to me: and him that cometh to me, I cast not away.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but his will which hath sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And this is the Fathers will which hath sent me, that of all which he hath given me, I should lose nothing, but should raise it up again at the last day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And this is the will of him that sent me, that every man which seeth the Son, and believeth in him, should have everlasting life: and I will raise him up at the last day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> The Jews then murmured at him because he said, I am that bread, which is come down from heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And they said, Is not this Jesus that son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? how then saith he, I came down from heaven?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> Jesus then answered, and said unto them, Murmur not among your selves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> No man can come to me, except the Father, which hath sent me, draw him: and I will raise him up at the last day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> It is written in the Prophets, And they shall be all taught of God. Every man therefore that hath heard, and hath learned of the Father, cometh unto me:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> Not that any man hath seen the Father, save he which is of God, he hath seen the Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> Verily, verily I say unto you, he that believeth in me, hath everlasting life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> I am that bread of life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> Your fathers did eat Manna in the wilderness, and are dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> This is that bread, which cometh down from heaven, that he which eateth of it, should not die.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> I am that living bread, which came down from heaven: if any man eat of this bread, he shall live for ever: and the bread that I will give, is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> Then the Jews strove among themselves, saying, How can this man give us his flesh to eat?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> Then Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily I say unto you, Except ye eat the flesh of the Son of man, and drink his blood, ye have no life in you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> Whosoever eateth my flesh, and drinketh my blood, hath eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> For my flesh is meat in deed, and my blood is drink in deed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> He that eateth my flesh, and drinketh my blood, dwelleth in me, and I in him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">57</span> As that living Father hath sent me, so live I by the Father, and he that eateth me, even he shall live by me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">58</span> This is that bread which came down from heaven: not as your fathers have eaten Manna, and are dead. He that eateth of this bread, shall live for ever.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">59</span> These things spake he in the Synagogue, as he taught in Capernaum.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">60</span> Many therefore of his disciples (when they heard this) said, This is an hard saying: who can hear it?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">61</span> But Jesus knowing in himself, that his disciples murmured at this, said unto them, Doeth this offend you?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">62</span> What then if ye should see that Son of man ascend up where he was before?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">63</span> It is the spirit that quickeneth: the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, are spirit and life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">64</span> But there are some of you that believe not: for Jesus knew from the beginning, which they were that believed not, and who should betray him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">65</span> And he said, Therefore said I unto you, that no man can come unto me, except it be given unto him of my Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">66</span> From that time, many of his disciples went back, and walked no more with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">67</span> Then said Jesus to the twelve, Will ye also go away?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">68</span> Then Simon Peter answered him, Master, to whom shall we go? thou hast the words of eternal life:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">69</span> And we believe and know that thou art that Christ that Son of the living God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">70</span> Jesus answered them, Have not I chosen you twelve, and one of you is a devil?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">71</span> Now he spake it of Judas Iscariot the son of Simon: for he it was that should betray him, though he was one of the twelve.</p>
