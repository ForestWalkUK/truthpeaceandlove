# John 3

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> There was now a man of the Pharisees, named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> This man came to Jesus by night, and said unto him, Rabbi, we know ye thou art a teacher come from God: for no man could do these miracles that thou doest, except God were with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Jesus answered, and said unto him, Verily, verily I say unto thee, except a man be born again, he can not see the kingdom of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Nicodemus said unto him, How can a man be born which is old? can he enter into his mothers womb again, and be born?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Jesus answered, Verily, verily I say unto thee, except that a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he can not enter into the kingdom of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> That which is born of the flesh, is flesh: and that that is born of the Spirit, is spirit.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Marvel not that I said to thee, Ye must be born again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every man that is born of the Spirit.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Nicodemus answered, and said unto him, How can these things be?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Jesus answered, and said unto him, Art thou a teacher of Israel, and knowest not these things?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Verily, verily I say unto thee, we speak that we know, and testify that we have seen: but ye receive not our witness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> If when I tell you earthly things, ye believe not, how should ye believe, if I shall tell you of heavenly things?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> For no man ascendeth up to heaven, but he that hath descended from heaven, that Son of man which is in heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And as Moses lift up the serpent in the wilderness, so must that Son of man be lift up,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> That whosoever believeth in him, should not perish, but have eternal life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> For God so loved the world, that he hath given his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him, should not perish, but have everlasting life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> For God sent not his Son into the world, that he should condemn the world, but that the world through him might be saved.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> He that believeth in him, is not condemned: but he that believeth not, is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the Name of that only begotten Son of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And this is the condemnation, that that light came into the world, and men loved darkness rather than that light, because their deeds were evil.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> For every man that evil doeth, hateth the light, neither cometh to light, least his deeds should be reproved.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> But he that doeth truth, cometh to the light, that his deeds might be made manifest, that they are wrought according to God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> After these things, came Jesus and his disciples into the land of Judea, and there tarried with them, and baptized.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And John also baptized in Aenon besides Salim, because there was much water there: and they came, and were baptized.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> For John was not yet cast into prison.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then there arose a question between Johns disciples and the Jews, about purifying.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And they came unto John, and said unto him, Rabbi, he that was with thee beyond Jordan, to whom thou barest witness, behold, he baptizeth, and all men come to him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> John answered, and said, A man can receive nothing, except it be given him from heaven.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Ye your selves are my witnesses, that I said, I am not that Christ, but that I am sent before him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> He that hath the bride, is the bridegroom: but the friend of the bridegroom which standeth and heareth him, rejoiceth greatly, because of the bridegrooms voice. This my joy therefore is fulfilled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> He must increase, but I must decrease.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> He that is come from an high, is above all: he that is of the earth, is of the earth, and speaketh of the earth: he that is come from heaven, is above all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And what he hath seen and heard, that he testifieth: but no man receiveth his testimony.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> He that hath received his testimony, hath sealed that God is true.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> For he whom God hath sent, speaketh the words of God: for God giveth him not the Spirit by measure.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> The Father loveth the Son, and hath given all things into his hand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> He that believeth in the Son, hath everlasting life, and he that obeyeth not the Son, shall not see life, but the wrath of God abideth on him.</p>
</div>
