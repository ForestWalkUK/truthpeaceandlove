# John 20

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Now the first day of the week came Mary Magdalene, early when it was yet dark, unto the sepulchre, and saw the stone taken away from the tomb.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Then she ran, and came to Simon Peter, and to the other disciple whom Jesus loved, and said unto them, They have taken away the Lord out of the sepulchre, and we know not where they have laid him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Peter therefore went forth, and the other disciple, and they came unto the sepulchre.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> So they ran both together, but the other disciple did outrun Peter, and came first to the sepulchre.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And he stooped down, and saw the linen clothes lying: yet went he not in.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Then came Simon Peter following him, and went into the sepulchre, and saw the linen clothes lie,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And the kerchief that was upon his head, not lying with the linen clothes, but wrapped together in a place by it self.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Then went in also the other disciple, which came first to the sepulchre, and he saw it, and believed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> For as yet they knew not the Scripture, That he must rise again from the dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And the disciples went away again unto their own home.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> But Mary stood without at the sepulchre weeping: and as she wept, she bowed her self into the sepulchre,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And saw two Angels in white, sitting, the one at the head, and the other at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And they said unto her, Woman, why weepest thou? She said unto them, They have taken away my Lord, and I know not where they have laid him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> When she had thus said, she turned her self back, and saw Jesus standing, and knew not that it was Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Jesus saith unto her, Woman, why weepest thou? whom seekest thou? She supposing that he had been the gardener, said unto him, Sir, if thou hast borne him hence, tell me where thou hast laid him, and I will take him away.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Jesus saith unto her, Mary. She turned her self, and said unto him, Rabboni, which is to say, Master.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Jesus saith unto her, Touch me not: for I am not yet ascended to my Father: but go to my brethren, and say unto them, I ascend unto my Father, and to your Father, and to my God, and your God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Mary Magdalene came and told the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and that he had spoken these things unto her.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> The same day then at night, which was the first day of the week, and when the doors were shut where the disciples were assembled for fear of the Jews, came Jesus and stood in the midst, and said to them, Peace be unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And when he had so said, he showed unto them his hands, and his side. Then were the disciples glad when they had seen the Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then said Jesus to them again, Peace be unto you: as my Father sent me, so send I you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And when he had said that, he breathed on them, and said unto them, Receive the holy Ghost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Whosoevers sins ye remit, they are remitted unto them: and whosoevers sins ye retain, they are retained.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> But Thomas one of the twelve, called Didymus, was not with them when Jesus came.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> The other disciples therefore said unto him, We have seen the Lord: but he said unto them, Except I see in his hands the print of the nails, and put my finger into the print of the nails, and put mine hand into his side, I will not believe it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And eight days after, again his disciples were within, and Thomas with them. Then came Jesus, when the doors were shut, and stood in the midst, and said, Peace be unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> After said he to Thomas, Put thy finger here, and see mine hands, and put forth thine hand, and put it into my side, and be not faithless, but faithful.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then Thomas answered, and said unto him, Thou art my Lord, and my God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Jesus said unto him, Thomas, because thou hast seen me, thou believest: blessed are they that have not seen, and have believed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> And many other signs also did Jesus in the presence of his disciples, which are not written in this book.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> But these things are written, that ye might believe, that Jesus is that Christ that Son of God, and that in believing ye might have life through his Name.</p>
