# John 10

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Verily, verily I say unto you, He that entereth not in by the door into the sheepfold, but climeth up another way, he is a thief and a robber.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> But he that goeth in by the door, is the shepherd of the sheep.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> To him the porter openeth, and the sheep hear his voice, and he calleth his own sheep by name, and leadeth them out.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And when he hath sent forth his own sheep, he goeth before them, and the sheep follow him: for they know his voice.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And they will not follow a stranger, but they flee from him: for they know not the voice of strangers.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> This parable spake Jesus unto them: but they understood not what things they were which he spake unto them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then said Jesus unto them again, Verily, verily I say unto you, I am that door of the sheep.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> All, that ever came before me, are thieves and robbers: but the sheep did not hear them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> I am that door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in, and go out, and find pasture.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and have it in abundance.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> I am that good shepherd: that good shepherd giveth his life for his sheep.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> But an hireling, and he which is not the shepherd, neither the sheep are his own, seeth the wolf coming, and he leaveth the sheep, and fleeth, and the wolf catcheth them, and scattereth the sheep.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> So the hireling fleeth, because he is an hireling, and careth not for the sheep.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> I am that good shepherd, and know mine, and am known of mine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> As the Father knoweth me, so know I the Father: and I lay down my life for my sheep.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Other sheep I have also, which are not of this fold: them also must I bring, and they shall hear my voice: and there shall be one sheepfold, and one shepherd.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Therefore doeth my Father love me, because I lay down my life, that I might take it again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> No man taketh it from me, but I lay it down of my self: I have power to lay it down, and have power to take it again: this commandment have I received of my Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Then there was a dissension again among the Jews for these sayings,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And many of them said, He hath a devil, and is mad: why hear ye him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Other said, These are not the words of him that hath a devil: can the devil open the eyes of the blind?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And it was at Jerusalem the feast of the Dedication, and it was winter.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And Jesus walked in the Temple, in Solomons porch.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Then came the Jews round about him, and said unto him, How long doest thou make us doubt? If thou be that Christ, tell us plainly.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Jesus answered them, I told you, and ye believe not: the works that I do in my Fathers Name, they bear witness of me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> But ye believe not: for ye are not of my sheep, as I said unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And I give unto them eternal life, and they shall never perish, neither shall any pluck them out of mine hand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> My Father which gave them me, is greater than all, and none is able to take them out of my Fathers hand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> I and my Father are one.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Then the Jews again took up stones, to stone him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Jesus answered them, Many good works have I showed you from my Father: for which of these works do ye stone me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> The Jews answered him, saying, For the good work we stone thee not, but for blasphemy, and that thou being a man, makest thy self God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your Law, I said, Ye are gods?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> If he called them gods, unto whom the word of God was given, and the Scripture cannot be broken,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Say ye of him, whom the Father hath sanctified, and sent into the world, Thou blasphemest, because I said, I am the Son of God?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> If I do not the works of my Father, believe me not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> But if I do, then though ye believe not me, yet believe the works, that ye may know and believe, that the Father is in me, and I in him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Again they went about to take him: but he escaped out of their hands,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And went again beyond Jordan, into the place where John first baptized, and there abode.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And many resorted unto him, and said, John did no miracle: but all things that John spake of this man, were true.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And many believed in him there.</p>
</div>
