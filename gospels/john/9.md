# John 9

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> And as Jesus passed by, he saw a man which was blind from his birth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And his disciples asked him, saying, Master, who did sin, this man, or his parents, that he was born blind?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Jesus answered, Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents, but that the works of God should be showed on him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> I must work the works of him that sent me, while it is day: the night cometh when no man can work.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> As soon as he had thus spoken, he spat on the ground, and made clay of the spittle, and anointed the eyes of the blind with the clay,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And said unto him, Go wash in the pool of Siloam (which is by interpretation, Sent.) He went his way therefore, and washed, and came again seeing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Now the neighbours and they that had seen him before, when he was blind, said, Is not this he that sat and begged?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Some said, This is he: and other said, He is like him: but he himself said, I am he.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Therefore they said unto him, How were thine eyes opened?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> He answered, and said, The man that is called Jesus, made clay, and anointed mine eyes, and said unto me, Go to the pool of Siloam and wash. So I went and washed, and received sight.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then they said unto him, Where is he? He said, I can not tell.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> They brought to the Pharisees him that was once blind.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And it was the Sabbath day, when Jesus made the clay, and opened his eyes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Then again the Pharisees also asked him, how he had received sight. And he said unto them, He laid clay upon mine eyes, and I washed, and do see.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Then said some of the Pharisees, This man is not of God, because he keepeth not the Sabbath day. Others said, How can a man that is a sinner, do such miracles? and there was a dissension among them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then spake they unto the blind again, What sayest thou of him, because he hath opened thine eyes? And he said, He is a Prophet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Then the Jews did not believe him (that he had been blind, and received his sight) until they had called the parents of him that had received sight.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And they asked them, saying, Is this your son, whom ye say was born blind? How doeth he now see then?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> His parents answered them, and said, We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> But by what means he now seeth, we know not: or who hath opened his eyes, can we not tell: he is old enough: ask him: he shall answer for himself.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> These words spake his parents, because they feared the Jews: for the Jews had ordained already, that if any man did confess that he was Christ, he should be excommunicate out of the Synagogue.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Therefore said his parents, He is old enough: ask him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Then again called they the man that had been blind, and said unto him, Give glory unto God: we know that this man is a sinner.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then he answered, and said, Whether he be a sinner or no, I can not tell: one thing I know, that I was blind, and now I see.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Then said they to him again, What did he to thee? how opened he thine eyes?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> He answered them, I have told you already, and ye have not heard it: wherefore would ye hear it again? will ye also be his disciples?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then reviled they him, and said, Be thou his disciple: we be Moses disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> We know that God spake with Moses: but this man we know not from whence he is.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> The man answered, and said unto them, Doubtless, this is a marvellous thing, that ye know not whence he is, and yet he hath opened mine eyes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Now we know that God heareth not sinners: but if any man be a worshipper of God, and doeth his will, him heareth he.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Since the world began, was it not heard, that any man opened the eyes of one that was born blind.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> If this man were not of God, he could have done nothing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> They answered, and said unto him, Thou art altogether borne in sins, and doest thou teach us? so they cast him out.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Jesus heard that they had cast him out: and when he had found him, he said unto him, Doest thou believe in the Son of God?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> He answered, and said, Who is he, Lord, that I might believe in him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And Jesus said unto him, Both thou hast seen him, and he it is that talketh with thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Then he said, Lord, I believe, and worshipped him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And Jesus said, I am come unto judgement into this world, that they which see not, might see: and that they which see, might be made blind.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> And some of the Pharisees which were with him, heard these things, and said unto him, Are we blind also?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Jesus said unto them, If ye were blind, ye should not have sin: but now ye say, We see: therefore your sin remaineth.</p>
</div>
