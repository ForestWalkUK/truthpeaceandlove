# John 15

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> I Am that true vine, and my Father is that husband man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Every branch that beareth not fruit in me, he taketh away: and every one that beareth fruit, he purgeth it, that it may bring forth more fruit.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Now are ye clean through the word, which I have spoken unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Abide in me, and I in you: as the branch cannot bear fruit of it self, except it abide in the vine, no more can ye, except ye abide in me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> I am that vine: ye are the branches: he that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me can ye do nothing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> If a man abide not in me, he is cast forth as a branch, and withereth: and men gather them, and cast them into the fire, and they burn.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> If ye abide in me, and my words abide in you, ask what ye will, and it shall be done to you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit, and be made my disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> As the father hath loved me, so have I loved you: continue in that my love.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> If ye shall keep my commandments, ye shall abide in my love, as I have kept my Fathers commandments, and abide in his love.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> These things have I spoken unto you, that my joy might remain in you, and that your joy might be full.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> This is my commandment, that ye love one another, as I have loved you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Greater love then this hath no man, when any man bestoweth his life for his friends.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Ye are my friends, if ye do whatsoever I command you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Henceforth call I you not servants: for the servant knoweth not what his master doeth: but I have called you friends: for all things that I have heard of my Father, have I made known to you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, and ordained you, that ye go and bring forth fruit, and that your fruit remain, that whatsoever ye shall ask of the Father in my Name, he may give it you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> These things command I you, that ye love one another.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> If the world hate you, ye know that it hated me before you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> If ye were of the world, the world would love his own: but because ye are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hateth you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Remember the word that I said unto you, The servant is not greater than his master. If they have persecuted me, they will persecute you also: if they have kept my word, they will also keep yours.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> But all these things will they do unto you for my Names sake, because they have not known him that sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> If I had not come and spoken unto them, they should not have had sin: but now have they no cloak for their sin.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> He that hateth me, hateth my Father also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> If I had not done works among them which none other man did, they had not had sin: but now have they both seen, and have hated both me, and my Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> But it is that the word might be fulfilled, that is written in their Law, They hated me without a cause.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> But when that Comforter shall come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth of the Father, he shall testify of me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And ye shall witness also, because ye have been with me from the beginning.</p>
</div>
