<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">12</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Then Jesus, six days before the Passover, came to Bethany, where Lazarus was, who died, whom he had raised from the dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> There they made him a supper, and Martha served: but Lazarus was one of them that sat at the table with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Then took Mary a pound of ointment of Spikenard very costly, and anointed Jesus feet, and wiped his feet with her hear, and the house was filled with the savour of the ointment.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Then said one of his disciples, even Judas Iscariot Simons son, which should betray him:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Why was not this ointment sold for three hundred pence, and given to the poor?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Now he said this, not that he cared for the poor, but because he was a thief, and had the bag, and bare that which was given.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then said Jesus, Let her alone: against the day of my burying she kept it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> For the poor always ye have with you, but me ye shall not have always.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Then much people of the Jews knew that he was there: and they came, not for Jesus sake only, but that they might see Lazarus also, whom he had raised from the dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> The high Priests therefore consulted, that they might put Lazarus to death also,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Because that for his sake many of the Jews went away, and believed in Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> On the morrow a great multitude that were come to the feast, when they heard that Jesus should come to Jerusalem,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Took branches of palm trees, and went forth to meet him, and cried, Hosanna, Blessed is the King of Israel that cometh in the Name of the Lord.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And Jesus found a young ass, and sat thereon, as it is written,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Fear not, daughter of Sion: behold, thy King cometh sitting on an asses colt.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> But his disciples understood not these things at the first: but when Jesus was glorified, then remembered they, that these things were written of him, and that they had done these things unto him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> The people therefore that was with him, bare witness that he called Lazarus out of the grave, and raised him from the dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Therefore met him the people also, because they heard that he had done this miracle.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And the Pharisees said among themselves, Perceive ye how ye prevail nothing? Behold, the world goeth after him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Now there were certain Greeks among them that came up to worship at the feast.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And they came to Philip, which was of Bethsaida in Galilee, and desired him, saying, Sir, we would see that Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Philip came and told Andrew: and again Andrew and Philip told Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And Jesus answered them, saying, The hour is come, that the Son of man must be glorified.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Verily, verily I say unto you, Except the wheat corn fall into the ground and die, it bideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> He that loveth his life, shall lose it, and he that hateth his life in this world, shall keep it unto life eternal.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> If any man serve me, let him follow me: for where I am, there shall also my servant be: and if any man serve me, him will my Father honour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Now is my soul troubled: and what shall I say? Father, save me from this hour: but therefore came I unto this hour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Father, glorify thy Name. Then came there a voice from heaven, saying, I have both glorified it, and will glorify it again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Then said the people that stood by, and heard, that it was a thunder: other said, An Angel spake to him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Jesus answered, and said, This voice came not because of me, but for your sakes.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Now is the judgement of this world: now shall the prince of this world be cast out.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> And I, if I were lift up from the earth, will draw all men unto me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Now this said he, signifying what death he should die.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> The people answered him, We have heard out of the Law, that that Christ bideth for ever: and how sayest thou, that that Son of man must be lift up? Who is that Son of man?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Then Jesus said unto them, Yet a little while is the light with you: walk while ye have that light, lest the darkness come upon you: for he that walketh in the dark, knoweth not whither he goeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> While ye have that light, believe in that light, that ye may be the children of the light. These things spake Jesus, and departed, and hid himself from them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And though he had done so many miracles before them, yet believed they not on him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> That the saying of Esaias the Prophet might be fulfilled, that he said, Lord, who believed our report? and to whom is the arm of the Lord revealed?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Therefore could they not believe, because that Esaias saith again,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart, that they should not see with their eyes, nor understand with their heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> These things said Esaias when he saw his glory, and spake of him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> Nevertheless, even among the chief rulers, many believed in him: but because of the Pharisees they did not confess him, least they should be cast out of the Synagogue.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> For they loved the praise of men, more than the praise of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> And Jesus cried, and said, He that believeth in me, believeth not in me, but in him that sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> And he that seeth me, seeth him that sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> I am come a light into the world, that whosoever believeth in me, should not abide in darkness.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> And if any man hear my words, and believe not, I judge him not: for I came not to judge the world, but to save the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> He that refuseth me, and receiveth not my words, hath one that judgeth him: the word that I have spoken, it shall judge him in the last day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> For I have not spoken of my self: but the Father which sent me, he gave me a commandment what I should say, and what I should speak.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> And I know that his commandment is life everlasting: the things therefore that I speak, I speak them so as the Father said unto me.</p>
