# John 1

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">1</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> In the beginning was that Word, and that Word was with God, and that Word was God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> This same was in the beginning with God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> All things were made by it, and without it was made nothing that was made.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> In it was life, and that life was the light of men.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> And that light shineth in the darkness, and the darkness comprehended it not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> There was a man sent from God, whose name was John.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> This same came for a witness, to bear witness of that light, that all men through him might believe.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> He was not that light, but was sent to bear witness of that light.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> This was that true light, which lighteth every man that cometh into the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> He was in the world, and the world was made by him: and the world knew him not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> He came unto his own, and his own received him not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> But as many as received him, to them he gave prerogative to be the sons of God, even to them that believe in his Name.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Which are born not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of ye will of man, but of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And that Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we saw the glory thereof, as the glory of the only begotten Son of the Father) full of grace and truth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> John bare witness of him, and cried, saying, This was he of whom I said, He that cometh after me, was before me: for he was better than I.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And of his fulness have all we received, and grace for grace.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> For the Law was given by Moses, but grace, and truth came by Jesus Christ.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> No man hath seen God at any time: that only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Then this is the record of John, when the Jews sent Priests and Levites from Jerusalem, to ask him, Who art thou?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> And he confessed and denied not, and said plainly, I am not that Christ.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> And they asked him, What then? Art thou Elias? And he said, I am not. Art thou that Prophet? And he answered, No.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Then said they unto him, Who art thou, that we may give an answer to them that sent us? What sayest thou of thy self?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> He said, I am the voice of him that crieth in the wilderness, Make straight the way of the Lord, as said the Prophet Esaias.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Now they which were sent, were of the Pharisees.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And they asked him, and said unto him, Why baptizest thou then, if thou be not that Christ, neither Elias, nor that Prophet?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> John answered them, saying, I baptize with water: but there is one among you, whom ye know not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> He it is that cometh after me, which was before me, whose shoe latchet I am not worthy to unloose.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> These things were done in Bethabara beyond Jordan, where John did baptize.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> The next day John, seeth Jesus coming unto him, and saith, Behold that Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> This is he of whom I said, After me cometh a man, which was before me: for he was better than I.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> And I knew him not: but because he should be declared to Israel, therefore am I come, baptizing with water.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> So John bare record, saying, I beheld that Spirit come down from heaven, like a dove, and it abode upon him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> And I knew him not: but he that sent me to baptize with water, he said unto me, Upon whom thou shalt see that Spirit come down, and tarry still on him, that is he which baptizeth with the holy Ghost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And I saw, and bare record that this is that Son of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> The next day, John stood again, and two of his disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And he beheld Jesus walking by, and said, Behold that Lamb of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And the two disciples heard him speak, and followed Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Then Jesus turned about, and saw them follow, and said unto them, What seek ye? And they said unto him, Rabbi (which is to say by interpretation, Master) where dwellest thou?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> He said unto them, Come, and see. They came and saw where he dwelt, and abode with him that day: for it was about the tenth hour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> Andrew, Simon Peters brother, was one of the two which had heard it of John, and that followed him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> The same found his brother Simon first, and said unto him, We have found that Messias, which is by interpretation, that Christ.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And he brought him to Jesus. And Jesus beheld him, and said, Thou art Simon the son of Jona: thou shalt be called Cephas, which is by interpretation, a stone.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> The day following, Jesus would go into Galilee, and found Philip, and said unto him, Follow me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> Now Philip was of Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Philip found Nathanael, and said unto him, We have found him of whom Moses did write in the Law, and the Prophets, Jesus that son of Joseph, that was of Nazareth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> Then Nathanael said unto him, Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth? Philip said to him, Come, and see.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and said of him, Behold in deed an Israelite, in whom is no guile.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> Nathanael said unto him, Whence knewest thou me? Jesus answered, and said unto him, Before that Philip called thee, when thou wast under the fig tree, I saw thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> Nathanael answered, and said unto him, Rabbi, thou art that Son of God: thou art that King of Israel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> Jesus answered, and said unto him, Because I said unto thee, I saw thee under the fig tree, believest thou? thou shalt see greater things then these.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> And he said unto him, Verily, verily I say unto you, hereafter shall ye see heaven open, and the Angels of God ascending, and descending upon that Son of man.</p>
</div>
