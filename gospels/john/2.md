<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">2</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And the third day, was there a marriage in Cana a town of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And Jesus was called also, and his disciples unto the marriage.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Now when the wine failed, the mother of Jesus said unto him, They have no wine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Jesus said unto her, Woman, what have I to do with thee? mine hour is not yet come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> His mother said unto the servants, Whatsoever he sayeth unto you, do it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And there were set there, six waterpots of stone, after the manner of the purifying of the Jews, containing two or three firkins a piece.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> And Jesus said unto them, Fill the waterpots with water. Then they filled them up to the brim.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Then he said unto them, Draw out now and bear unto the governor of the feast. So they bare it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Now when the governor of the feast had tasted the water that was made wine, (for he knew not whence it was: but the servants, which drew the water, knew) the governor of the feast called the bridegroom,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> And said unto him, All men at the beginning set forth good wine, and when men have well drunk, then that which is worse: but thou hast kept back the good wine until now.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> This beginning of miracles did Jesus in Cana a town of Galilee, and showed forth his glory: and his disciples believed on him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> After that, he went down into Capernaum, he and his mother, and his brethren, and his disciples: but they continued not many days there.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> For the Jews Passover was at hand. Therefore Jesus went up to Jerusalem.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And he found in the Temple those that sold oxen, and sheep, and doves, and changers of money, sitting there.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Then he made a scourge of small cords, and drave them all out of the Temple with the sheep and oxen, and poured out the changers money, and overthrew the tables,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And said unto them that sold doves, Take these things hence: make not my fathers house, an house of merchandise.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And his disciples remembered, that it was written, The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Then answered the Jews, and said unto him, What sign showest thou unto us, that thou doest these things?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Jesus answered, and said unto them, Destroy this Temple, and in three days I will raise it up again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Then said the Jews, Forty and six years was this Temple a building, and wilt thou rear it up in three days?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> But he spake of the temple of his body.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> As soon therefore as he was risen from the dead, his disciples remembered that he thus said unto them: and they believed the Scripture, and the word which Jesus had said.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Now when he was at Jerusalem at the Passover in the feast, many believed in his Name, when they saw his miracles which he did.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> But Jesus did not commit him self unto them, because he knew them all,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And had no need that any should testify of man: for he knew what was in man.</p>
</div>
