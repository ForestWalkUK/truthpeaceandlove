# John 18

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">18</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> When Jesus had spoken these things, he went forth with his disciples over the brooke Cedron, where was a garden, into the which he entered, and his disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And Judas which betrayed him, knew also the place: for Jesus oft times resorted thither with his disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Judas then, after he had received a band of men and officers of the high Priests, and of the Pharisees, came thither with lanterns and torches, and weapons.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Then Jesus, knowing all things that should come unto him, went forth and said unto them, Whom seek ye?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> They answered him, Jesus of Nazareth. Jesus said unto them, I am he. Now Judas also which betrayed him, stood with them.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> As soon then as he had said unto them, I am he, they went away backwards, and fell to the ground.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then he asked them again, Whom seek ye? And they said, Jesus of Nazareth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Jesus answered, I said unto you, that I am he: therefore if ye seek me, let these go their way.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> This was that the word might be fulfilled which he spake, Of them which thou gavest me, have I lost none.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Then Simon Peter having a sword, drew it, and smote the high Priests servant, and cut off his right ear. Now the servants name was Malchus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Then said Jesus unto Peter, Put up thy sword into the sheath: shall I not drink of the cup which my Father hath given me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then the band and the captain, and the officers of the Jews took Jesus, and bound him,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And led him away to Annas first (for he was father in law to Caiaphas, which was the high Priest that same year)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And Caiaphas was he, that gave counsel to the Jews, that it was expedient that one man should die for the people.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> Now Simon Peter followed Jesus, and another disciple, and that disciple was known of the high Priest: therefore he went in with Jesus into the hall of the high Priest:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> But Peter stood at the door without. Then went out the other disciple which was known unto the high Priest, and spake to her that kept the door, and brought in Peter.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then said the maid that kept the door, unto Peter, Art not thou also one of this mans disciples? He said, I am not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> And the servants and officers stood there, which had made a fire of coles: for it was cold, and they warmed themselves. And Peter also stood among them, and warmed himself.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> (The high Priest then asked Jesus of his disciples, and of his doctrine.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Jesus answered him, I spake openly to the world: I ever taught in the Synagogue and in the Temple, whither the Jews resort continually, and in secret have I said nothing.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Why askest thou me? ask them which heard me what I said unto them: behold, they know what I said.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> When he had spoken these things, one of the officers which stood by, smote Jesus with his rod, saying, Answerest thou the high Priest so?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Jesus answered him, If I have evil spoken, bear witness of the evil: but if I have well spoken, why smitest thou me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Now Annas had sent him bound unto Caiaphas the high Priest)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> And Simon Peter stood and warmed himself, and they said unto him, Art not thou also of his disciples? He denied it, and said, I am not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> One of the servants of the high Priest, his cousin whose ear Peter smote off, said, Did not I see thee in the garden with him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Peter then denied again, and immediately the cock crew.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then led they Jesus from Caiaphas into the common hall. Now it was morning, and they themselves went not into the common hall, least they should be defiled, but that they might eat the Passover.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Pilate then went out unto them, and said, What accusation bring ye against this man?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> They answered, and said unto him, If he were not an evil doer, we would not have delivered him unto thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Then said Pilate unto them, Take ye him, and judge him after your own Law. Then the Jews said unto him, It is not lawful for us to put any man to death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> It was that the word of Jesus might be fulfilled which he spake, signifying what death he should die.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> So Pilate entered into the common hall again, and called Jesus, and said unto him, Art thou the king of the Jews?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Jesus answered him, Sayest thou that of thy self, or did other tell it thee of me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Pilate answered, Am I a Jew? Thine own nation, and the high Priests have delivered thee unto me. What hast thou done?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Jesus answered, My kingdom is not of this world: if my kingdom were of this world, my servants would surely fight, that I should not be delivered to the Jews: but now is my kingdom not from hence.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> Pilate then said unto him, Art thou a King then? Jesus answered, Thou sayest that I am a King: for this cause am I borne, and for this cause came I into the world, that I should bear witness unto the truth: every one that is of the truth, heareth my voice.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Pilate said unto him, What is truth? And when he had said that, he went out again unto the Jews, and said unto them, I find in him no cause at all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> But you have a custom, that I should deliver you one loose at the Passover: will ye then that I loose unto you the King of the Jews?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> Then cried they all again, saying, Not him, but Barabbas: now this Barabbas was a murderer.</p>
