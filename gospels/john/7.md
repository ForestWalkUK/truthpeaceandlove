# John 7

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> After these things, Jesus walked in Galilee, and would not walk in Judea: for the Jews sought to kill him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> Now the Jews feast of the Tabernacles was at hand.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> His brethren therefore said unto him, Depart hence, and go into Judea, that thy disciples may see thy works that thou doest.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> For there is no man that doeth any thing secretly, and he himself seeketh to be famous. If thou doest these things, show thy self to the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> For as yet his brethren believed not in him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Then Jesus said unto them, My time is not yet come: but your time is always ready.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> The world can not hate you: but me it hateth, because I testify of it, that the works thereof are evil.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Go ye up unto this feast: I will not go up yet unto this feast: for my time is not yet fulfilled.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> These things he said unto them, and abode still in Galilee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> But as soon as his brethren were gone up, then went he also up unto the feast, not openly, but as it were privily.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Then the Jews sought him at the feast, and said, Where is he?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> And much murmuring was there of him among the people. Some said, He is a good man: other said, Nay: but he deceiveth the people.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Howbeit no man spake openly of him for fear of the Jews.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Now when half the feast was done, Jesus went up into the Temple and taught.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And the Jews marvelled, saving, How knoweth this man the Scriptures, seeing that he never learned!</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Jesus answered them, and said, My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> If any man will do his will, he shall know of the doctrine, whether it be of God, or whether I speak of my self.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> He that speaketh of himself, seeketh his own glory: but he that seeketh his glory that sent him, the same is true, and no unrighteousness is in him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Did not Moses give you a Law, and yet none of you keepeth the law? Why go ye about to kill me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> The people answered, and said, Thou hast a devil: who goeth about to kill thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Jesus answered, and said to them, I have done one work, and ye all marvel.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Moses therefore gave unto you circumcision, (not because it is of Moses, but of the fathers) and ye on the Sabbath day circumcise a man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> If a man on the Sabbath receive circumcision, that the Law of Moses should not be broken, be ye angry with me, because I have made a man every whit whole on the Sabbath day?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgement.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then said some of them of Jerusalem, Is not this he, whom they go about to kill?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And behold, he speaketh openly, and they say nothing to him: do the rulers know in deed that this is in deed that Christ?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Howbeit we know this man whence he is: but when that Christ cometh, no man shall know whence he is.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Then cried Jesus in the Temple as he taught, saying, Ye both know me, and know whence I am: yet am I not come of my self, but he that sent me, is true, whom ye know not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> But I know him: for I am of him, and he hath sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Then they sought to take him, but no man laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Now many of the people believed in him, and said, When that Christ cometh, will he do more miracles then this man hath done?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> The Pharisees heard that the people murmured these things of him, and the Pharisees, and high Priests sent officers to take him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Then said Jesus unto them, Yet am I a little while with you, and then go I unto him that sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Ye shall seek me, and shall not find me, and where I am, can ye not come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Then said the Jews amongst themselves, Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? Will he go unto them that are dispersed among the Grecians, and teach the Grecians?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> What saying is this that he said, Ye shall seek me, and shall not find me? and where I am, cannot ye come?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> Now in the last and great day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried, saying, If any man thirst, let him come unto me, and drink.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> He that believeth in me, as saith the Scripture, out of his belly shall flow rivers of water of life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> (This spake he of the Spirit which they that believed in him, should receive: for the holy Ghost was not yet given, because that Jesus was not yet glorified.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> So many of the people, when they heard this saying, said, Of a truth this is that Prophet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Other said, This is that Christ: and some said, But shall that Christ come out of Galilee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> Saith not the Scripture that that Christ shall come of the seed of David, and out of the town of Bethlehem, where David was?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> So was there dissension among the people for him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> And some of them would have taken him, but no man laid hands on him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Then came the officers to the high Priests and Pharisees, and they said unto them, Why have ye not brought him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> The officers answered, Never man spake like this man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> Then answered them the Pharisees, Are ye also deceived?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> Doeth any of the rulers, or of the Pharisees believe in him?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> But this people, which know not the Law, are cursed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> Nicodemus said unto them, (he that came to Jesus by night, and was one of them.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> Doth our Law judge a man before it hear him, and know what he hath done?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> They answered, and said unto him, Art thou also of Galilee? Search and look: for out of Galilee ariseth no Prophet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> And every man wet unto his own house.</p>
