<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">11</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> And a certain man was sick, named Lazarus of Bethany, the town of Mary, and her sister Martha.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> (And it was that Mary which anointed the Lord with ointment, and wiped his feet with her hear, whose brother Lazarus was sick.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> Therefore his sisters sent unto him, saying, Lord, behold, he whom thou lovest, is sick.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> When Jesus heard it, he said, This sickness is not unto death, but for the glory of God, that the Son of God might be glorified thereby.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister, and Lazarus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And after he had heard that he was sick, yet abode he two days still in the same place where he was.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Then after that, said he to his disciples, Let us go into Judea again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> The disciples said unto him, Master, the Jews lately sought to stone thee, and doest thou go thither again?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Jesus answered, Are there not twelve hours in the day? If a man walk in the day, he stumbleth not, because he seeth the light of this world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> But if a man walk in the night, he stumbleth, because there is no light in him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> These things spake he, and after, he said unto them, Our friend Lazarus sleepeth: but I go to wake him up.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Then said his disciples, Lord, if he sleep, he shall be safe.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Howbeit, Jesus spake of his death: but they thought that he had spoken of the natural sleep.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> Then said Jesus unto them plainly, Lazarus is dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> And I am glad for your sakes, that I was not there, that ye may believe: but let us go unto him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Then said Thomas (which is called Didymus) unto his fellow disciples, Let us also go, that we may die with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then came Jesus, and found that he had lien in the grave four days already.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> (Now Bethany was near unto Jerusalem, about fifteen furlongs off.)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And many of the Jews were come to Martha and Mary to comfort them for their brother.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Then Martha, when she heard that Jesus was coming, went to meet him: but Mary sat still in the house.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then said Martha unto Jesus, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not been dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> But now I know also, that whatsoever thou askest of God, God will give it thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Jesus said unto her, Thy brother shall rise again.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Martha said unto him, I know that he shall rise again in the resurrection at the last day.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And whosoever liveth, and believeth in me, shall never die: Believest thou this?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> She said unto him, Yea, Lord, I believe that thou art that Christ that Son of God, which should come into the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> And when she had so said, she went her way, and called Mary her sister secretly, saying, The Master is come, and calleth for thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And when she heard it, she arose quickly, and came unto him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> For Jesus was not yet come into the town, but was in the place where Martha met him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> The Jews then which were with her in the house, and comforted her, when they saw Mary, that she rose up hastily, and went out, followed her, saying, She goeth unto the grave, to weep there.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Then when Mary was come where Jesus was, and saw him, she fell down at his feet, saying unto him, Lord, if thou hadst been here, my brother had not been dead.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> When Jesus therefore saw her weep, and the Jews also weep which came with her, he groaned in the spirit, and was troubled in himself,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> And said, Where have ye laid him? They said unto him, Lord, come and see.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And Jesus wept.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> Then said the Jews, Behold, how he loved him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And some of them said, Could not he, which opened the eyes of the blind, have made also, that this man should not have died?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> Jesus therefore again groaned in himself, and came to the grave. And it was a cave, and a stone was laid upon it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Jesus said, Take ye away the stone. Martha the sister of him that was dead, said unto him, Lord, he stinketh already: for he hath been dead four days.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> Jesus said unto her, Said I not unto thee, that if thou didst believe, thou shouldest see the glory of God?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> Then they took away the stone from the place where the dead was laid. And Jesus lift up his eyes, and said, Father, I thank thee, because thou hast heard me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> I know that thou hearest me always, but because of the people that stand by, I said it, that they may believe, that thou hast sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> As he had spoken these things, he cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> Then he that was dead, came forth, bound hand and foot with bands, and his face was bound with a napkin. Jesus said unto them, Loose him, and let him go.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Then many of the Jews, which came to Mary, and had seen the things, which Jesus did, believed in him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> But some of them went their way to the Pharisees, and told them what things Jesus had done.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> Then gathered the high Priests, and the Pharisees a council, and said, What shall we do? For this man doeth many miracles.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> If we let him thus alone, all men will believe in him, and the Romans will come and take away both our place, and the nation.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> Then one of them named Caiaphas, which was the high Priest that same year, said unto them, Ye perceive nothing at all,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> Nor yet do you consider that it is expedient for us, that one man die for the people, and that the whole nation perish not.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> This spake he not of himself: but being high Priest that same year, he prophesied that Jesus should die for that nation:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> And not for that nation only, but that he should gather together in one the children of God, which were scattered.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> Then from that day forth they consulted together, to put him to death.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> Jesus therefore walked no more openly among the Jews, but went thence unto a country near to the wilderness, into a city called Ephraim, and there continued with his disciples.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">55</span> And the Jews Passover was at hand, and many went out of the country up to Jerusalem before the Passover, to purify themselves.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">56</span> Then sought they for Jesus, and spake among themselves, as they stood in the Temple, What think ye, that he cometh not to the feast?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">57</span> Now both the high Priests and the Pharisees had given a commandment, that if any man knew where he were, he should show it, that they might take him.</p>
