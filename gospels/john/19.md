# John 19

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">19</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Then Pilate took Jesus and scourged him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> And the soldiers platted a crown of thorns, and put it on his head, and they put on him a purple garment,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And said, Hail, King of the Jews. And they smote him with their rods.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> Then Pilate went forth again, and said unto them, Behold, I bring him forth to you, that ye may know, that I find no fault in him at all.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Then came Jesus forth wearing a crown of thorns, and a purple garment. And Pilate said unto them, Behold the man.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Then when the high Priests and officers saw him, they cried, saying, Crucify, crucify him. Pilate said unto them, Take ye him and crucify him: for I find no fault in him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> The Jews answered him, We have a law, and by our law he ought to die, because he made himself the Son of God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> When Pilate then heard that word, he was the more afraid,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> And went again into the common hall, and said unto Jesus, Whence art thou? But Jesus gave him none answer.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Then said Pilate unto him, Speakest thou not unto me? Knowest thou not that I have power to crucify thee, and have power to loose thee?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Jesus answered, Thou couldest have no power at all against me, except it were given thee from above: therefore he that delivered me unto thee, hath the greater sin.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> From thence forth Pilate sought to loose him, but the Jews cried, saying, If thou deliver him, thou art not Caesars friend: for whosoever maketh himself a King, speaketh against Caesar.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> When Pilate heard this word, he brought Jesus forth, and sat down in the judgement seat in a place called the Pavement, and in Hebrew, Gabbatha.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> And it was the Preparation of the Passover, and about the sixth hour: and he said unto the Jews, Behold your King.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> But they cried, Away with him, away with him, crucify him. Pilate said unto them, Shall I crucify your King? The high Priests answered, We have no King but Caesar.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Then delivered he him unto them, to be crucified. And they took Jesus, and led him away.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> And he bare his own cross, and came into a place named of dead mens Skulls, which is called in Hebrew, Golgotha:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> Where they crucified him, and two other with him, on either side one, and Jesus in the midst.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> And Pilate wrote also a title, and put it on the cross, and it was written, JESUS OF NAZARETH THE KING OF THE JEWS.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> This title then read many of the Jews: for the place where Jesus was crucified, was near to the city: and it was written in Hebrew, Greek and Latin.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Then said the high Priests of the Jews to Pilate, Write not, The King of the Jews, but that he said, I am King of the Jews.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Pilate answered, What I have written, I have written.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Then the soldiers, when they had crucified Jesus, took his garments (and made four parts, to every soldier a part) and his coat: and the coat was without seam woven from the top throughout.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Therefore they said one to another, Let us not divide it, but cast lots for it, whose it shall be. This was that the Scripture might be fulfilled, which saith, They parted my garments among them, and on my coat did cast lots. So the soldiers did these things in deed.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> Then stood by the cross of Jesus his mother, and his mothers sister, Mary the wife of Cleopas, and Mary Magdalene.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> And when Jesus saw his mother, and the disciple standing by, whom he loved, he said unto his mother, Woman, behold thy son.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Then said he to the disciple, Behold thy mother: and from that hour, the disciple took her home unto him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> After, when Jesus knew that all things were performed, that the Scripture might be fulfilled, he said, I thirst.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And there was set a vessel full of vinegar: and they filled a sponge with vinegar: and put it about an Hyssop stalk, and put it to his mouth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Now when Jesus had received of the vinegar, he said, It is finished, and bowed his head, and gave up the ghost.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> The Jews then (because it was the Preparation, that the bodies should not remain upon the cross on the Sabbath day: for that Sabbath was an high day) besought Pilate that their legs might be broken, and that they might be taken down.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Then came the soldiers and brake the legs of the first, and of the other, which was crucified with Jesus.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> But when they came to Jesus, and saw that he was dead already, they brake not his legs.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> But one of the soldiers with a spear pierced his side, and forthwith came there out blood and water.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> And he that saw it, bare record, and his record is true: and he knoweth that he saith true, that ye might believe it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> For these things were done, that the Scripture should be fulfilled, Not a bone of him shall be broken.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> And again an other Scripture saith, They shall see him whom they have thrust through.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> And after these things, Joseph of Arimathea (who was a disciple of Jesus, but secretly for fear of the Jews) besought Pilate that he might take down the body of Jesus. And Pilate gave him licence. He came then and took Jesus body.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> And there came also Nicodemus (which first came to Jesus by night) and brought of myrrh and aloes mingled together about an hundred pound.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> Then took they the body of Jesus, and wrapped it in linen clothes with the odours, as the manner of the Jews is to bury.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And in that place where Jesus was crucified, was a garden, and in the garden a new sepulchre, wherein was never man yet laid.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> There then laid they Jesus, because of the Jews Preparation day, for the sepulchre was near.</p>
