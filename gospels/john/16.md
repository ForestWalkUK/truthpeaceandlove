# John 16

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">16</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> These things have I said unto you, that ye should not be offended.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> They shall excommunicate you: yea, the time shall come, that whosoever killeth you, will think that he doeth God service.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And these things will they do unto you, because they have not known the Father, nor me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> But these things have I told you, that when the hour shall come, ye might remember, that I told you them. And these things said I not unto you from the beginning, because I was with you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> But now I go my way to him that sent me, and none of you asketh me, Whither goest thou?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> But because I have said these things unto you, your hearts are full of sorrow.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> Yet I tell you the truth, It is expedient for you that I go away: for if I go not away, that Comforter will not come unto you: but if I depart, I will send him unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgement.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Of sin, because they believed not in me:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Of righteousness, because I go to my Father, and ye shall see me no more:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Of judgement, because the prince of this world is judged.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> I have yet many things to say unto you, but ye cannot bear them now.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Howbeit, when he is come which is the Spirit of truth, he will lead you into all truth: for he shall not speak of himself, but whatsoever he shall hear, shall he speak, and he will show you the things to come.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> He shall glorify me: for he shall receive of mine, and shall show it unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> All things that the Father hath, are mine: therefore said I, that he shall take of mine, and show it unto you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> A little while, and ye shall not see me: and again a little while, and ye shall see me: for I go to the Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Then said some of his disciples among them selves, What is this that he saith unto us, A little while, and ye shall not see me, and again, a little while, and ye shall see me, and, For I go to the Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> They said therefore, What is this that he saith, A little while? we know not what he sayeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Now Jesus knew that they would ask him, and said unto them, Do ye enquire among your selves, of that I said, A little while, and ye shall not see me: and again, a little while, and ye shall see me?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Verily, verily I say unto you, that ye shall weep and lament, and the world shall rejoice: and ye shall sorrow, but your sorrow shall be turned to joy.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> A woman when she travaileth, hath sorrow, because her hour is come: but as soon as she is delivered of the child, she remembereth no more the anguish, for joy that a man is born into the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> And ye now therefore are in sorrow: but I will see you again, and your hearts shall rejoice, and your joy shall no man take from you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> And in that day shall ye ask me nothing. Verily, verily I say unto you, whatsoever ye shall ask the Father in my Name, he will give it you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> Hitherto have ye asked nothing in my Name: ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> These things have I spoken unto you in parables: but the time will come, when I shall no more speak to you in parables: but I shall show you plainly of the Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> At that day shall ye ask in my Name, and I say not unto you, that I will pray unto the Father for you:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> For the Father himself loveth you, because ye have loved me, and have believed that I came out from God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> I am come out from the Father, and came into the world: again I leave the world, and go to the Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> His disciples said unto him, Lo, now speakest thou plainly, and thou speakest no parable.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Now know we that thou knowest all things, and needest not that any man should ask thee. By this we believe, that thou art come out from God.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> Jesus answered them, Do you believe now?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> Behold, the hour cometh, and is already come, that ye shall be scattered every man into his own, and shall leave me alone: but I am not alone: for the Father is with me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> These things have I spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace: in the world ye shall have affliction, but be of good comfort: I have overcome the world.</p>
