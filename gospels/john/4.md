# John 4

<p class="bible-note">Geneva Bible (1599 edition), spelling modernised. <a href="{{ '/gospels/' | relative_url }}">About this text</a></p>

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">1</span> Now when the Lord knew, how the Pharisees had heard, that Jesus made and baptized more disciples then John,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> (Though Jesus himself baptized not: but his disciples)</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> He left Judea, and departed again into Galilee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And he must needs go through Samaria.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Then came he to a city of Samaria called Sychar, near unto the possession that Jacob gave to his son Joseph.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> And there was Jacobs well. Jesus then wearied in the journey, sat thus on the well: it was about the sixth hour.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> There came a woman of Samaria to draw water. Jesus said unto her, Give me drink.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> For his disciples were gone away into the city, to buy meat.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Then said the woman of Samaria unto him, How is it, that thou being a Jew, askest drink of me, which am a woman of Samaria? For the Jews meddle not with the Samaritans.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Jesus answered and said unto her, If thou knewest that gift of God, and who it is that saith to thee, Give me drink, thou wouldest have asked of him, and he would have given thee, water of life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> The woman said unto him, Sir, thou hast nothing to draw with, and the well is deep: from whence then hast thou that water of life?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Art thou greater than our father Jacob, which gave us the well, and he himself drank thereof, and his sons, and his cattle?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> Jesus answered, and said unto her, Whosoever drinketh of this water, shall thirst again:</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him, shall never be more a thirst: but the water that I shall give him, shall be in him a well of water, springing up into everlasting life.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> The woman said unto him, Sir, give me of that water, that I may not thirst, neither come hither to draw.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> Jesus said unto her, Go, call thine husband, and come hither.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> The woman answered, and said, I have no husband. Jesus said unto her, Thou hast well said, I have no husband.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> For thou hast had five husbands, and he whom thou now hast, is not thine husband: that saidest thou truly.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> The woman said unto him, Sir, I see that thou art a Prophet.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> Our fathers worshipped in this mountain, and ye say, that in Jerusalem is the place where men ought to worship.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> Jesus said unto her, Woman, believe me, the hour cometh, when ye shall neither in this mountain, nor at Jerusalem worship the Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Ye worship that which ye know not: we worship that which we know: for salvation is of the Jews.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> But the hour cometh, and now is, when the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit, and truth: for the Father requireth even such to worship him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> God is a Spirit, and they that worship him, must worship him in spirit and truth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> The woman said unto him, I know well that Messias shall come which is called Christ: when he is come, he will tell us all things.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> Jesus said unto her, I am he, that speak unto thee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> And upon that, came his disciples, and marvelled that he talked with a woman: yet no man said unto him, What askest thou? or why talkest thou with her?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> The woman then left her waterpot, and went her way into the city, and said to the men,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> Come, see a man which hath told me all things that ever I did: is not he that Christ?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Then they went out of the city, and came unto him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> In the mean while, the disciples prayed him, saying, Master, eat.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">32</span> But he said unto them, I have meat to eat that ye know not of.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">33</span> Then said the disciples between themselves, Hath any man brought him meat?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">34</span> Jesus said unto them, My meat is that I may do the will of him that sent me, and finish his work.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">35</span> Say not ye, There are yet four months, and then cometh harvest? Behold, I say unto you, Lift up your eyes, and look on the regions: for they are white already unto harvest.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">36</span> And he that reapeth, receiveth reward, and gathereth fruit unto life eternal, that both he that soweth, and he that reapeth, might rejoice together.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">37</span> For herein is the saying true, that one soweth, and an other reapeth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">38</span> I sent you to reap that, whereon ye bestowed no labour: other men laboured, and ye are entered into their labours.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">39</span> Now many of the Samaritans of that city believed in him, for the saying of the woman which testified, He hath told me all things that ever I did.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">40</span> Then when the Samaritans were come unto him, they besought him, that he would tarry with them: and he abode there two days.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">41</span> And many more believed because of his own word.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">42</span> And they said unto the woman, Now we believe, not because of thy saying: for we have heard him our selves, and know that this is in deed that Christ the Saviour of the world.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">43</span> So two days after he departed thence, and went into Galilee.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">44</span> For Jesus himself had testified, that a Prophet hath none honour in his own country.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">45</span> Then when he was come into Galilee, the Galileans received him, which had seen all the things that he did at Jerusalem at the feast: for they went also unto the feast.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">46</span> And Jesus came again into Cana a town of Galilee, where he had made of water, wine. And there was a certain ruler, whose son was sick at Capernaum.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">47</span> When he heard that Jesus was come out of Judea into Galilee, he went unto him, and besought him that he would go down, and heal his son: for he was even ready to die.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">48</span> Then said Jesus unto him, Except ye see signs and wonders, ye will not believe.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">49</span> The ruler said unto him, Sir, go down before my son die.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">50</span> Jesus said unto him, Go thy way, thy son liveth: and the man believed the word that Jesus had spoken unto him, and went his way.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">51</span> And as he was now going down, his servants met him, saying, Thy son liveth.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">52</span> Then enquired he of them the hour when he began to amend. And they said unto him, Yesterday the seventh hour the fever left him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">53</span> Then the father knew, that it was the same hour in the which Jesus had said unto him, Thy son liveth. And he believed, and all his household.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">54</span> This second miracle did Jesus again, after he was come out of Judea into Galilee.</p>
</div>
