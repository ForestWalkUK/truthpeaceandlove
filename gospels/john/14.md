# John 14

<div class="gospel-text">
<p class="gv first"><span class="chap" aria-hidden="true">14</span><span class="vn sr">1</span> Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">2</span> In my Fathers house are many dwelling places: if it were not so, I would have told you: I go to prepare a place for you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">3</span> And if I go to prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive you unto my self, that where I am, there may ye be also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">4</span> And whither I go, ye know, and the way ye know.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">5</span> Thomas said unto him, Lord, we know not whither thou goest: how can we then know ye way?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">6</span> Jesus said unto him, I am that Way, and that Truth, and that Life. No man cometh unto the Father, but by me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">7</span> If ye had known me, ye should have known my Father also: and from henceforth ye know him, and have seen him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">8</span> Philip said unto him, Lord, show us thy Father, and it sufficeth us.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">9</span> Jesus said unto him, I have been so long time with you, and hast thou not known me, Philip? he that hath seen me, hath seen my Father: how then sayest thou, Show us thy Father?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">10</span> Believest thou not, that I am in the Father, and the Father is in me? The words that I speak unto you, I speak not of my self: but the Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">11</span> Believe me, that I am in the Father, and the Father is in me: at the least, believe me for the very works sake.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">12</span> Verily, verily I say unto you, he that believeth in me, the works that I do, he shall do also, and greater than these shall he do: for I go unto my Father.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">13</span> And whatsoever ye ask in my Name, that will I do, that the Father may be glorified in the Son.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">14</span> If ye shall ask any thing in my Name, I will do it.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">15</span> If ye love me, keep my commandments,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">16</span> And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever,</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">17</span> Even the Spirit of truth, whom the world can not receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him: for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">18</span> I will not leave you fatherless: but I will come to you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">19</span> Yet a little while, and the world shall see me no more, but ye shall see me: because I live, ye shall live also.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">20</span> At that day shall ye know that I am in my Father, and you in me, and I in you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">21</span> He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, is he that loveth me: and he that loveth me, shall be loved of my Father: and I will love him, and will show mine own self to him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">22</span> Judas said unto him (not Iscariot) Lord, what is the cause that thou wilt show thy self unto us, and not unto the world?</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">23</span> Jesus answered, and said unto him, If any man love me, he will keep my word, and my Father will love him, and we will come unto him, and will dwell with him.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">24</span> He that loveth me not, keepeth not my words, and the word which ye hear, is not mine, but the Fathers which sent me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">25</span> These things have I spoken unto you, being present with you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">26</span> But the Comforter, which is the holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my Name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, which I have told you.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">27</span> Peace I leave with you: my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, nor fear.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">28</span> Ye have heard how I said unto you, I go away, and will come unto you. If ye loved me, ye would verily rejoice, because I said, I go unto the Father: for the Father is greater than I.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">29</span> And now have I spoken unto you, before it come, that when it is come to pass, ye might believe.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">30</span> Hereafter will I not speak many things unto you: for the prince of this world cometh, and hath nought in me.</p>
<p class="gv"><span class="vn">31</span> But it is that the world may know that I love my Father: and as the Father hath commanded me, so I do. Arise, let us go hence.</p>
