Truth, Peace and Love - PROJECT STATE
=====================================
Version: 0.9 (17th October, A.D. 2026)
Archive: truthpeaceandlove-v0.9.zip

What the site is
----------------
A Jekyll site for GitHub Pages, written for readers who are not Christians.
Live address (from _config.yml): https://forestwalkuk.github.io/truthpeaceandlove
Pages: Home, Gospels (Geneva Bible 1599, spelling modernised), My Story, Blog, Notes.

How to publish a new version
----------------------------
1. Unzip the archive.
2. Copy its contents over the repository (keep the .git folder).
3. Commit and push. GitHub Pages builds the site itself; nothing to install locally.
4. Check the footer says "Version 0.9" on your phone, in light and dark mode.

Settled design decisions
------------------------
- Typeface: Source Serif 4, self-hosted (assets/fonts).
- Title in small capitals; strapline "Found in Jesus Christ" beneath it (_config.yml).
- Red (--rubric) is reserved for Scripture; LORD and KJV are set in small capitals
  automatically by the layout.
- Three fading verses under the header define the name: John 14:6 (truth),
  Romans 5:1 (peace), 1 John 4:9 (love).
- Light/dark switch in the header; the choice is remembered on the reader's device.
- VerseClick pop-ups for Authorised Version references.
- Home-page picture panel, 16:9: blue sunrise (light), moonlit mountains (dark),
  by yayangart on Pixabay, credited in the footer. Not to be used as a logo,
  favicon or social-media image (Pixabay licence forbids use as a mark).
- Avoid in any future pictures: eyes, faces in the sun or moon, crescent-and-star,
  rows of moon phases, pentagrams, zodiac signs, the lotus, strong sunburst rays,
  portraits. Plain sun, moon, stars and landscapes are fine.

How to add or change a picture
------------------------------
Pictures live in assets/images/illustrations/ as 1280 x 720 WebP files.
The file names are set in assets/css/style.css under "Home-page picture".
To show the panel on another page, add "illustration: true" to its front matter.
