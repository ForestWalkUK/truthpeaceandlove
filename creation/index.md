The Bible opens with these words:

> In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth. (Genesis 1:1, KJV)

This section looks at what the Bible says about the beginning of the world, about the Flood in the days of Noah, and about the theory of evolution, which teaches a different account of where we came from.

It matters because of what follows from it. If God made us, we belong to Him and we answer to Him. And the God who made the world is the same God who calls us to Himself through His Son, the Lord Jesus Christ.
